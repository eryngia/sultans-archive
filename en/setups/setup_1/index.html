Title:

Normal

Text:

Hassan is a good friend of yours, but also a poet with principles. No one gets to refute his stories, except the delightful Mutton Stove... <sprite=0><indent=10%><size=85%>Stories are equal parts joy and sorrow. The chance of rolling a Success on a dice is 50%.</size></indent> <sprite=0><indent=10%><size=85%>Occasionally, Mutton Stove would munch on the manuscripts and Hassan will have to rewrite the tale. You can rewind to the previous turn for 10 times.</size></indent> <sprite=0><indent=10%><size=85%> This story is rigorous: each week, you can exchange 1 Sultan Card, and you have 7 days to break a card before you are executed.</size></indent> <sprite=0><indent=10%><size=85%>You start the game with 2 Gold Dice that are guaranteed to produce Successes.</size></indent> Please note: players may obtain additional Gold Dice, rewinds, and redraws in the game.

Bloodshed

Text:

A Stone Bloodshed Card! Behold – there are two parts to every Sultan Card: Bloodshed refers to the act, and Stone – its color, its tier – refers to the target. Stone is the lowest tier, which means you can use it on a target of any tier of your desire. Whether you would "waste" a high-tier target on it... is entirely up to you.
