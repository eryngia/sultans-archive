Sultan Card Draws

Card:

Carnality

Text:

A Silver Carnality Card! Behold – there are two parts to every Sultan Card: Carnality refers to physical pleasures, and Silver – its color, its tier – refers to the tier of the object of pleasure. Though all things are but ants at your feet, oh great Sultan, they are inequal in their insignificance. Some would always be a little bit nobler. And you, you have the right to dictate your subjects' worth.

Card:

Bloodshed

Text:

Ah, Bloodshed, a fine diversion for a ruler. No doubt you have long since grown accustomed to the symphony of blood and screams. Yet the beauty of this game lies in its unpredictability. Not even Your Majesty can know what enjoyment awaits. The thrill of not knowing what delights await makes each move all the more exhilarating. <size=85%>The Mysterious Magician's gaze passes over the trembling courtiers.</size> Your Exalted Majesty, supreme in splendor, how do you intend to resolve this card?
