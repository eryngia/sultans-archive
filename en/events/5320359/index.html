Text:

Lumera came to you. Head respectfully bowed, she made a request you had not expected. "Master [player.name], if you would allow it... I would like to use the gold you gave me—the one you called 'royalties'—to do one thing. I wish to sponsor more stories to be published." Lifting her eyes slightly, she seemed to watch for your reaction. Those eyes shone like a scatter of stars—who could harden his heart before such a gaze? You told her the money was hers to spend as she liked, and that if she wants to print more books, she should go ahead. Lumera's cheeks flushed again; she thanked you lightly. Thereafter you could keep sharing royalties with her... and those coins, in turn, gave birth to even more books.
