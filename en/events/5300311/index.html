Text:

Lately, you notice Shama seems a bit melancholic, and your relationship encourages her to confide in you about her experiences. It turns out she is an illegitimate daughter of a noble, despised by her father due to a congenital deformity. Now, her father has learned about her profession as a prostitute in the House of Delights and believes she has disgraced the family. "My father won’t let me go, and perhaps his assassin is already on his way." As she speaks, Shama slightly curls her lips, an expression filled with a mix of worry and perhaps a special kind of relief, which stirs a ripple within you...

Text:

After listening to your words, Shama just giggles softly, completely not taking it to heart. She has long known and has become accustomed to the fact that promises in House of Delights are not to be trusted – until you seriously ask about her father’s name, the location of her territory, and the situation there. She widens her eyes in surprise and beginning to dissuade you: "You don't need to oppose him for... well, for someone like me." But you only gently squeezed her palm. In the past, you would never consider standing against a great lord, but now, you have gained the power to change fate from this twisted game – even if it's just the fate of a prostitute.
