Text:

In the shaded corridor you find Samir. He looks exhausted, clothes dust-stained. At the sight of you, a weary smile flickers across his face. "I heard about the audience today... my friend, you do have a gift for finding trouble." You clap his shoulder. "Says the man whose head would be on a pike without me." "True." He smiles wanly. Now the Royal Guards are certain he supplied the abortifacients, and the eunuchs are inspecting the women of the harem for signs of recent miscarriage... "They're in a hurry to find a scapegoat and wrap this up." You see through it at once and give Samir a look. "Truth doesn't matter to them. Their necks do. In fact, not even the Sultan cares—what matters is saving face." Samir only shakes his head. "I know that's the easiest way out. But I won't accuse any innocent woman—they've suffered enough. You have no idea how many in this palace—" He stops, sighs, then produces a letter long prepared and presses it into your hand. The ink is still fresh, the handwriting hurried. "I didn't do any of it. No woman ever miscarried under my care. But... I'm sorry for dragging you into this. If the Sultan demands an answer, tell him it was me. At least you won't have to die for my sake."

Text:

You slipped the confession into your robe; only then did he plainly relax, his expression easing. You smiled. "So I'm the villain forever ready to frame his friends, is that it? Fine. Now let's talk, Samir—you want the truth, and so do I. Don't tell me you mean to die without understanding a thing. This letter—you wrote it after the audience ended, didn't you?" Samir smiled, a little sheepish, then fell silent; after a long moment he said quietly, "I do have some suspicions, but I..." He couldn't finish. He sighed and went on: "If you wanted to help me, then please lend me a helper—someone clever enough, able to move unseen. In the meantime, put on a show of investigating, talk to the women of the harem; as for the Sultan... well, you could hand me over at any time, [player.name], my friend. My life was in your hands." You nodded and promised to buy him as much time as you could.
