Text:

Nawfal arranged to meet you at a strange and remote mansion, not very quiet, with the sound of poor children playing and shouting beyond the walls. "Do you remember what we talked about before," he mused, turning his gaze from the window to you, "I've come up with an idea lately." He described the concept of a school. At this school, poor children could learn to read and write, understand and engage with your ideas, all for free. He even planned to provide a nutritious lunch each day for the children. "I want to try planting some seeds, but what I can harvest? I don't know. If you're willing to give it a try, as before, let's split the cost fifty-fifty." Nawfal indicated a number: 10 gold coins.
