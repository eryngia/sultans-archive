Text:

You tell Raed that she already knows the answer, which is why she's been tossing and turning in her thoughts. Hasn't she already used the image of a noble lady to cover the vagrants several times? Hasn't she seen how foolish guards and High Constables fawned over nobles? She possesses everything needed to be a noble—perfect manners, perfect education—so why not use it to seize greater advantage? These benefits can be redistributed by her hands so that more people eat their fill! Raed bites her lip, hesitating, thinking... "Mother told me to keep far away... She spent her life teaching me to be a lady, but at the end, she told me to stay far from nobles—as far as I could. If anyone discovers I am the Sultan's sister... they would surely behead me. And that would be the mildest punishment, I'm afraid." She takes a long breath, then lifts her eyes to you. "But I can't hide forever. I am who I am—whether it's the schooling I had as a child or the blood and flesh that make this body, I can't evade it. You're right. Why not exploit myself to the utmost? Why not do what only I can do?" She leans forward, one hand loosening her messy hair she had hastily tied. "Princess Raedina will return to this city. She will help you greatly. But you must promise one thing to her friend, Raed: you must care for those vagrants—their food and drink, their health, their safety. If they are harmed, Princess Raedina will be heartbroken... And you know how noblewomen are, once they are heartbroken, they hide away—then you'll never find her." You promise to care for the vagrants. "You must also promise never to drag me into that filthy throne. I will never enter the palace, never be a lord, and don't you dare try to shove me anywhere for your political ends..." You promise you will never use her heritage to make her become Sultan or anything else—a promise that now sounds almost ridiculous, to vow to a vagrant that you will never make her Sultan! "Good. Then... I need to go make preparations. We'll speak again." She rises and gives you a haughty nod, every gesture perfectly that of a noblewoman.
