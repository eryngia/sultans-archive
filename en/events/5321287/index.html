Text:

Arzuna earns the king's praise and favor, and that same day she sets an exquisite banquet in the garden, summoning a number of courtiers. The act is improper—but when the Sultan favors a woman, her whims become the court's new order. Your name is also on the list, and you have no choice but to go. You arrive earlier than most. By the stream in the garden, you encounter Arzuna. Surrounded by her maidservants and eunuchs, she looks every inch the noble beauty. She stares you for a long while, then suddenly says, "I hate you, [player.name]." "I had meant to take fierce revenge on you... I meant to become the most powerful woman alive and spend that power on vengeance. Those who wounded and shamed my mother were to answer for it. Only then would I be released from the oath she forced me to swear." She idly turns the bracelets at her wrists, her voice growing low. "But you... Why do you always help me? Each time... cruel fate befalls me in the harem—you are there. It makes no sense. Even if my rise as a favored woman might benefit you, you did not have to..." You remain silent, and she does the same for a long while. "I have never thought of you as a brother, yet you truly seem to treat me as a sister. I am just a lowly dancer, an illegitimate daughter who should never have been born..." She exhales softly, as if she might laugh or weep. But in the end, she only shakes her head and accepts the gold hookah a eunuch offers. "Perhaps you simply require a supporter in the palace. Very well—I will aid you. But only once." You are about to object when the other courtiers arrive. The banquet begins, and the matter is left where it stands. Arzuna smiles and speaks lightly with everyone—including you. It is a banquet in celebration of victory, and no one raises a word that might spoil the mood.
