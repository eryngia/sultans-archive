Text:

You embrace her and tell her everything will be fine. The dog represents a faithful companion, the rope represents a means to an end, a way out; and the flute is the Wife's favorite instrument, symbolizing joy and leisure. Even if she feels her life is drifting like a boat on the sea, with a companion, a way out, and joy, she is sure to find happiness. Hearing your words, she smiled gently, her eyes filled with love.

Text:

You look into your Wife's beautiful eyes and tell her, it might be an omen. The dog represents a barking troublemaker, the rope symbolizes a noose, and the flute is the jester's favorite, pleasing the monarch. These three things combined will bring storms, destroying the small boat. Hearing your words, she clings tightly to your arm and asks what to do. You try to comfort her, telling her you will be extremely careful to prevent this dream from coming true. You both snuggle together for a long time, finding some sense of security from each other's warmth, until she stops trembling.

Text:

You smile and hold your Wife's beautiful face, telling her not to worry, that the dream stems from her deep-seated anxiety and fear, and has no significant meaning. Dogs can be both servile and vicious, ropes can save a person or strangle them, and the flute is both her favorite instrument and the jester's delight. These three things, along with the small boat drifting at sea, all symbolize her anxiety about the future. Hearing your words, she buries her head in your chest and asks playfully, what should she do then? You stroke her hair, telling her that no matter what, you will always be there for her.
