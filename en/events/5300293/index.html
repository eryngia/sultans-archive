You respectfully approach the masked guest, presenting yourself like a humble and polite servant without prematurely revealing his identity, as after all, he is still well-masked at this moment! You lean in and quietly assure this exceptionally distinguished guest that having you host this indulgent party will certainly make it more entertaining: because you will definitely invite him, and any issues that may arise at the party will be your responsibility. He can enjoy himself to the fullest, even with more freedom than in the palace... The masked guest – who is the Sultan – ponders and contemplates... Finally, he agrees with your proposal. After all, everything in the empire is his, and occasionally letting people believe they truly own something adds a layer of intrigue to the game. Seeing this, the shrewd Madam Buthayna immediately recognizes he is no ordinary person. While she ushers you in with the girls surrounding you, she also lavishly flatters the masked guest, promising to serve him even more attentively next time. The masked guest nods, says nothing, and left just like that.
