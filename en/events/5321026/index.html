Text:

You receive an invitation to a small auction, supposedly held by an ancient family looking to clear out some collectibles. Upon arrival, you find they are trivial items that nobody would buy – centuries-old dressing cabinets, second-rate portraits by unknown painters, an old emerald engagement ring... Wait, you notice the back of the portrait has a seal from the Qais family. You also see Qais standing in the corner, his face growing paler under the ridicule of the guests. He must be making a final effort to buy back the family title...
