Text:

The story of the Vizier being so obsessed with a male prostitute that he kidnapped Nawfal, only to be killed by the latter, has spread throughout the court, becoming the most popular anecdote. Many keep retelling the events of that banquet with winks and nudges, each person with their own version. After indulging in dozens of versions, The Sultan finally remembers he needs a new vizier: "Abdul's story is indeed captivating, but someone must take his dirty seat. [player.name], who do you think is suitable?" For some reason, The Sultan singles you out, naming you specifically, as you can't shake off the feeling that a significant trap is laid out here... The trouble a vizier has to deal with is probably far more serious than you think...

You pats your chest, saying you're happy to share the Sultan's worries and speak a lot of flattering words. He squintes and laughs, "Not bad. At least you're more capable than that lascivious old dog before." He nods, "Half of Abdul's territory will be confiscated due to his scandal and added to the state treasury. The remaining part is rewarded to you." You show gratitude to the Sultan once again, and after he leisurely left, the courtiers come up to congratulate you, speaking all kinds of flattering words. In the vast palace, except for Nawfal who doesn't bow his head, everyone else does. With Nawfal quietly shaking his head, you suddenly calm down. Maybe being the Vizier isn't necessarily a good thing...
