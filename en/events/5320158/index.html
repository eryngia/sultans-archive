After a charity relief, Aziz slowly walks up to you. "I think, master – if you allow me to – that this time the porridge wasn't tasty, it was astringent, and the oats weren't soaked well," he says. "I think, Inal should be the one making the porridge. The porridge she makes is so delicious that would make you swallow your tongue." You look at him suspiciously, "How do you know that?" Aziz stands quietly and calmly in the sun without answering. Still, there's no need to ask as you can guess because your household slaves always gather to share dinner. But can you really trust a slave's taste buds? What good foods could he have tasted?
