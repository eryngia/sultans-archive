Text:

A maid takes a great risk to ask for your help. The mistress of her lord, a tax collector, went back to her hometown with her child some time ago and never returned. After a brief investigation, her hometown people claimed they had never seen her. The official handling the case thus ruled that the tax collector murdered her to get her dowry and fabricated her disappearance. But the maid insists her master would never do such a thing; he and his wife have always been very loving. She heard you are a rare kind noble and plucks up the courage to seek your help to find out the truth.

Text:

You interrupt the woman and ask, “Why are you so worried about your master? Could it be that you...” Her face instantly turns red, and she stutters that her master is a good person, very kind, and treats all the servants well... She talks for a long time, in great detail, as if the tax collector were a reincarnated saint. You have a sudden realization and say abruptly, “Then, the official responsible for the trial was indeed wrong. The one who benefited the most from her death is not him, but you.” The young maid is stunned for a moment before responding, “... Yes. It is me.” Strangely, she breathes a sigh of relief, bows to you, and leaves briskly.

You believe the outlines of the case are very clear, and the minister responsible for the case did not misjudge. Should you doubt your noble colleague and instead trust a humble servant? Especially when she speaks so much yet provides no concrete evidence, just relying on emotions. However, emotions are so fickle that one night is enough to turn them into enmity. Upon hearing this, the maid instinctively wants to refute you but quickly realizes it is useless as you have already made up your mind. She leaves in disappointment.
