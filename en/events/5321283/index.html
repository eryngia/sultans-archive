Text:

Arzuna comes to you with unusual solemnity. Since you first met her, this is the first time her face shows such resolve and seriousness. "I must confess something, [player.name]," she begins. She no longer uses her sweet voice to call you "brother," nor does she sway her body deliberately as she sits before you. "At first, I came to you for revenge. I sought to use you to reach those in power—even the Sultan himself—and then wield that power against you. The only way I knew to take revenge was through flattery, pleasing, using, and servility... My mother taught me nothing else." She looks straight into your eyes and continues: "But now, I no longer want to do this. I will not explain why—you will understand. If you do not, it means you are one of those who do good without knowing it, like a figure from a fairy tale—and I cannot harm someone like you. Anyway, I may still harbor hatred for you, for I have spent many years immersed in hatred toward you and your family, and my mother made me swear on her deathbed to continue hating you. But I will not take revenge. You may rest assured, you will not have an enemy who dances well and shares half your blood." She bows. The jingling of her ornaments rings around her, and you notice she seems dressed for travel. "Now, I intend to depart. Having abandoned revenge, I must find a new purpose, one to live for myself." She smiles, delighted with her idea. "I want to be a traveling dancer, like those I saw as a child—dancing while wandering, experiencing every dance on this continent, dancing with every dancer I meet... Perhaps I will return, or perhaps my journey will never end, but I will remain free, always immersed in what I love, always happy." She pauses, smiling at you with a pure, heartfelt expression. "Farewell, brother."
