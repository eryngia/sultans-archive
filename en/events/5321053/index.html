Zephyr has grown plump, and though he wears your family crest and colors, his clothing is of very fine material – definitely not the kind you provide for servants. When you inquire about this, he hangs his head, admitting he receives financial support from "a young lady." "But... nothing can happen between me and that young lady; she's far too prominent, too wealthy, while I'm just your slave. I always reject her, but she never listens – when I refuse her gifts, she bites me!" He shows his arm pitifully, which is indeed covered in bite marks. "I know seeing her means either accepting gifts or getting bitten when I refuse, but I still want to see her... I think of her every day... I run really fast, between her place and this mansion, and the round trip doesn't take me long. And I never neglect my duties here.
