Text:

This morning, Adila knocks on your door. The fully armed warrior carries a heavy travel pack, her bright eyes blazing with anger and pride. "I'm leaving." She proudly lifts her head and announces to you, "I don't need a husband; what I want is the head of that damned dragon. Hah! I've memorized every technique to slay it. I'll find the dragon myself – and then I'll kill it!" Her voice is so firm that you know no matter what you say, you won't be able to change her mind. Perhaps adventure and death are the fates of a warrior. Even so, you can still prepare something for her.
