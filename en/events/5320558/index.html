Samir sent a servant to fetch you, asking to meet the next day in the corridor behind the Audience Hall. As court physician, leaving the harem required no small amount of petitions and paperwork, so it was easier for you to go to him. Still, why would this cautious, discreet doctor, who usually disliked cultivating courtiers, suddenly seek you out? It made you think of the things you had done for Lady Sadani of late... In any case, you decided it was best to go and hear what he had to say.
