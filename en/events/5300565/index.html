Text:

You ask why, after twenty years as a nameless Roaming Swordsman, he now resolves to seek revenge. "Hahaha..." he laughs, scratching his chin. "I don't know either. I thought I'd drift through life this way, never thinking about revenge. After all, I don't want to be king or Sultan - I love being a Roaming Swordsman! Once old Kuk and the others die, no one will pressure me anymore. I can do what I want, live how I want, die how I want." Then he snaps his fingers, rubbing his wrist, seemingly preparing difficult words. "Then... I saw mother's... belongings. I thought, if I completely give up, after old Kook dies, after I die, no one will know about Mekana, about King Ultagi. Only that tattered handwritten booklet - all other history... all names, erased by the Sultan's scribes. Our existence reduced to such little evidence, a few broken plates, broken slippers, gone." He straightens up, eyes gently meeting yours - eyes seemingly not belonging to his alcohol-ravaged body, eyes belonging to a prince. "I don't want to be forgotten. We were good people. Good people shouldn't be forgotten." He says softly.
