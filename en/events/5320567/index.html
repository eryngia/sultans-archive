You happened to hear that a secluded patch of wasteland had come up for sale. It was a low ahill with gentle slopes and a clear little river nearby... good ground for a graveyard, perhaps—oh, you hadn't grown so morbid as to plan your own end; you had simply remembered that Samir's father's bones were likely still in some paupers' trench. He was a criminal, a man of the fallen kingdom; no other burial ground would admit him... Well... though Samir had never said so, perhaps... in the end, he would want his father to rest in peace.
