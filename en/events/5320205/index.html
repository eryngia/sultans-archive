Text:

Nawfal secretly sent a letter through someone, but its content was not confidential. In the letter, he thanked you for the generosity shown that day; no matter what, you had indeed done a good deed. However... he was still worried. Nawfal told you he had do such charity for years, but the number of people coming for porridge only increased. He kept pondering if everything he did was merely futile or how to truly help the poor. At the end of the letter, he helplessly wrote that it was just his self-questioning, not anticipating you could answer. Perhaps the question had no answer at all.
