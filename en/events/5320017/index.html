Text:

Zaki came to visit you this morning with excitement all over his face, telling you that he heard people talking about you everywhere – in the tavern, in the palace, in the bathhouse, everywhere. So what, you casually told him, only words from truly valuable and meaningful people are worth listening to. Listening to your words, his face suddenly turned red. His embarrassed look stirred something in your heart, and a bold thought entered your mind: should you take the time to invite him to the bathhouse? He admires you so much... you could have some deeper communication...
