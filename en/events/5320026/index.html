Text:

You found Lumera in the study. She seemed spellbound, chanting the deciphered starry spell over and over again. Whatever you said beside her, she seemed not to hear a word. After a while, she looked up, a silver star cross flashing in her left eye, both eerie and sacred... She read the spell faster and faster, and many words not recorded in the parchment slipped from her lips, as if some higher will were whispering to her, as if this will had chosen her as an innocent vessel... You finally understood – the meaning of "opening" was to open a door leading to the path of infinity; "opening" meant severing the link to the present world – she seemed to have knocked on the door of the spell. Should you stop her from continuing to read it?

You shouted loudly at her, then shook her shoulders vigorously. Seeing that this couldn't interrupt her pouring chant, you grabbed the oil lamp and set fire to the spell in her hand! The letters on the spell shifted with the dancing flames. The star cross in her eyes gradually faded. In the void, something seemed to sigh... Lumera suddenly collapsed to the ground, fainting as if exhausted. Will she wake up? You worriedly sent for a doctor.

Text:

You stepped back two steps, respectfully looking at her. Her chanting grew more and more frenzied, faster and faster, with her hands starting to move uncontrollably, and you recognized she was gesturing the names of each star spirit in ancient language. According to those extinct beliefs, all of man's power came from the stars, but no living person could withstand the true power of the star spirits, so the vessel must die—dying to achieve eternal life. Her movements suddenly stopped. Then, she looked at you calmly, as if it was Lumera's last glance as a human. "Thank you, Master," she said, then smiled at you before closing her eyes.
