Text:

One morning, when you stepped out of your bedchamber, you noticed something at your feet. It was a palm-sized, transparent crystal with a faint blue tint, like a solidified droplet of some liquid you had once seen. And if you looked closely, scraps of paper seemed to be caught inside. You held it up to the morning sun and finally made out the fragmentary words on the scraps— "for humankind... prepared..." Judging from the hand, it appeared to be part of the labels Samir had written for those concoctions. It was probably a return-gift from those midnight visitors.
