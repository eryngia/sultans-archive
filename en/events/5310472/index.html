The Destroyer's light fills the sky, flames obscuring clouds... Amid this hopeless scene, a brilliant star-streak splits the heavens, bringing earthly strugglers hope: this meteor traces a luminous arc across half the firmament, crashing directly into the burning divine realm... Flames part then rejoin, even that suffocating light momentarily dims! The sky resounds with shattering crystal, a figure falling dimly, raising fresh dust beside you: "I fulfill our covenant, mortal. The Highlord of Star-Souled protects this world!" Heavens, with less dust and brighter light... this warrior might truly inspire.
