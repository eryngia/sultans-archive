You shut your eyes, yet the earth already lies spread beneath you. Divine blood and flesh now become your stairs... almost unconsciously, you ascend. At the summit sits the throne of the Immaculate Purity, the World's Guardian, Master of Light and Flame, the Demon-vanquisher... Countless angels and spirits wander aimlessly, yet to realize their master has gone... Drawn by their call, you step upward. With every step, the scattered divine flesh and power surge toward you. The angels crowd around, weaving the remains of the old god into a new armor. They dress you, crown you, let the white sacred plate wrap your body and illuminate your body. As you take your seat, the entire divine realm is flooded with radiance again...
