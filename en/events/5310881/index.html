Text:

That evening, everything changes. You wake abruptly to find the world drifting in a slow, heavy flow. You can see how the candlelight wavers with a slow, elegant grace, and a moth seems to take an entire night just to flap its wings once. Outside the window, starlight drips as slow as thick honey... And Lumera, bit by bit, manifests in your room. For the first time in ages, you two sit down face to face again. She skillfully offers you the hookah, then, in a relaxed manner of being your friend instead of a slave, grabs a handful of preserves. "I do miss this at times." She waves the preserves at you. And you notice her hand is translucent... Not just her hand, her entire body is nearly transparent, save for the star nestled within her skull, which remains solid and radiant. "Up in the heavens, there is no tea, no hookah, no preserves, and no books." She looks up at you. "Yet, there is true serenity—a tranquility only known to soul... and, if you wish, true listening. You know, the gods do not listen to the prayers They cannot or will not answer, but the stars are always listening." Her words leave you in deep thought... "You have traveled so far, and you have earned your place among the constellations. If you come with me, I will be delighted to have a fellow star to talk to. But if you stay, I will be equally glad—for a kind soul will send books up to me." She nods with a smile. "Take your time to think." With that, she vanishes.
