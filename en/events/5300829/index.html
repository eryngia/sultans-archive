Text:

That night, you dreamed a strange and wearying dream. Before you stood a mountain, and though you knew not why, you had to reach its peak. So you climbed—past emerald meadows, through tangled woods, over slopes littered with broken stone. Yet no matter how far you climbed, the summit never drew nearer. At last, you planted your hands on your hips and roared at the sky: "Show yourself, or I’ll find another damned mountain to climb!" A mocking laugh echoed—and the mountain shrank. Smaller, smaller, until it was no more than a standing mirror before you, reflecting your own exhausted face. You woke aching in every limb. Then it struck you: this dream could make you stronger. A gift, perhaps, from whatever dwelled in the mirror—pleased with how you handled those noble conversations for [xiaochou.name]. You rather wished its favors didn’t demand quite so much toil.
