Text:

You step out for some fresh air from an all-night feast and see the large figure of Aziz blending into the shadows at the corner of the courtyard, motionless, as if one with the stones beside him. You approach and ask him what he's doing. "If you allow me to answer, master, I'm waiting for leftovers." As was customary, the remnants from the feast would be given to the slaves, which isn't wrong, but he continues... "Today's lamb is roasted better than ever, I can smell it from here."

He was startled, rarely showing a moment of panic on his normally dull face, looking around hurriedly, tugging at his coarse cloth hem, hesitating: "I am just a slave, please allow me to refuse." You insisted on your request, being the host of this banquet, you were willing to invite whoever you wanted. But first, you need him to wash his face, comb his hair, then come to the banquet hall as you said. You even tempted him, mentioning how delicious roast lamb is, and how fresh the appetizers are, ensuring he could eat his fill if he wished. Aziz indeed was tempted.
