Text:

Asal was overjoyed when he found you, clutching a thick volume—but it turned out to be nothing more than the table of contents for the entire set. "Done—done! I had seen every book, every marvel in the world! With my own eyes—thanks to you!" He suddenly flung his arms about you and sobbed, barely able to get a sentence out. You awkwardly comforted him for a long time; at last, sated with tears, he gave you the deepest bow in embarrassment and presented the contents as thanks... Yes—divorced from the Encyclopedia itself it meant nothing, but it was proof of the world's wisdom and knowledge, a treasure that could prove useful in extraordinary moments.
