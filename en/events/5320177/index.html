Text:

After calming down, Inal finally explains the 'wolf' she encountered. While selecting meat and poultry at the market, a man suddenly approached from behind, praising her beauty, saying he'd noticed her for a long time, trying to grab her hand, hug her waist, even drag her into an alley! Inal shook her head repeatedly, struggling desperately, she smashed the basket she was holding into his face, screamed for help, and fortunately, a strong butcher nearby recognized her as a regular, acting in time to stop the man. Inal was terrified, thanking the butcher before running home as fast as she could, with the man audaciously following her, shouting that they would meet again, that she couldn't escape. You don't know the man's background or when he might appear again, it seems rather troubling...
