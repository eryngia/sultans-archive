After a fanquet, an unpleasant incident occurred. Today, the guesthouse serves retainers pea-dusted lamb chops, while Habib brings the paying guests hearty stewed mutton. As guests dispersed, one retainer mocked those eating freely – "Look, the meat from your chops? It’s all on our plates now." A fight brews from this. By the time you rush in, both sides bristle, ready to clash.
