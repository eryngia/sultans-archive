Text:

Adila stands ready to depart. Mounted on her stallion and armed with dragon-slaying gear, she cuts a solitary figure. Just as she bids you farewell, ready to begin her journey, a strange procession comes into view. Her grandmother appears in ancient silver armor, leaning on a cane while gripping a sword. Her mother wears silver chainmail, one hand steadying her mother, the other firmly holding an oak shield. Her aunts, grandaunts, and even the household's old maids – all stand adorned in warrior's gear, slowly approaching together. "We all thought," Grandmother stops in front of Adila and you, her voice hoarse and sharp, "and we've prepared, practiced, and worked hard for it, but we never managed to keep our courage like you until the end..." Mother comes up to embrace Adila, the young warrior is taken aback, at a loss. "I thought you were all model ladies, who never touch weapons..." "We gave up being warriors, and then became ladies, because here lies another battlefield. But through generations, we've always hoped our daughters could be stronger warriors than we are." Adila bursts into tears. Then, turning around, she embraces Maggie who stands beside you, and gives you a slight nod. With these final gestures done, she mounts her horse and gallops into the distance. Behind her, the women softly sing a strange song until their youngest, bravest girl disappears into the distance, they finally sing the last line of the song: "I strike! I fight! I break free... At last I've slain the dragon's might! That dared to chain my soul so tight!"
