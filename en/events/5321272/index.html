Rumors about Concubine Arzuna spread with sudden intensity. People mockingly call her a prostitute, an illiterate woman, a vulgar beauty who pleases men with the sway of her hips. Some even boast that a handful of gold coins would be enough to purchase her charms, as though they had truly laid hands upon a woman of the Sultan. The talk unsettles you greatly. After all, you are the one who sent her into the harem—or at least she once lived beneath your roof before entering it. Damaging her reputation likewise damages your own. Perhaps you should not remain indifferent to this matter.
