You find another chance to meet Lamblet. You tell them that the most suitable person you could think of is Abdul. Even if love had once lingered in his life, he would have traded it all for power and gold. He trusts nothing that can't be bartered. Everything Hassan owns is worthless in his eyes. If Hassan were placed at his side, Abdul would surely put the old poet to work in his own slick, calculating way. In no more than three days, Hassan's mind would be brimming with creative urges... probably all about murder plots or managing budget deficits—anything but second-rate love poems! "So be it. Baa. So be it! I will arrange it—just a few tiny adjustments, nothing difficult! Baa-baa-baa-baa—I'm looking forward to it. Baa. You really are quite skilled at pleasing others!" The lamb laughs thunderously, their eyes still fixed on you. "Next time I go looking for another world to play in, I will write a character like you. Baa... Perhaps I will simply snatch you over, baa-ha-ha!" Hassan's gift for poetry, his passion and desire, probably remain intact. But as for the next few days awaiting him—well... best of luck to him.
