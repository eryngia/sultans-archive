Text:

A little chapbook suddenly went viral, describing how you had brutally killed over thirty booksellers and committing every imaginable atrocity against them. You could not clear your name; you even wanted to seize the author and have him killed, but such writers hid better than rats. The scandal not only hurt your reputation, it damaged the name and sales of Lumera's book—the source of the whole affair: people claimed this slave girl had soared to fame only because she had such a cruel patron and sponsor like you. Worse, quite a few hopped up to claim that they were her ghostwriters... Asal sought you out and let out a helpless sigh. In short, you could no longer expect this book to earn anything further.
