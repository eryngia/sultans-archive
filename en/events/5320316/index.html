Text:

"Baa—ah! What are you doing, baa! Why is it that every time we meet, there's chaos, deviation, wrong turns everywhere! Baa! Can't you not make trouble for once?" Deafening bleats pressed in from all sides, as if to crush you. You fought back with your own strength, striving to keep calm. You asked the voice—what was it? What was its connection to Hassan? What did it want? "Who am I? Baa, baa, baa... I am something you could not truly comprehend—I merely like poetry, like stories, like to find a little fun! Compared to those so-called gods you worshiped, you will have found I am the most merciful, baa! As for Hassan, baa... since you have mangaged to reach here, it means you can bear a little of the truth's weight... I will tell you this: the world you see has two parts—one part existed before Hassan, including you; the other appeared only after Hassan." You were startled and pressed it on what the latter part meant— "Baa—you are abusing my patience and my kindness toward lower beings—baa! Begone. I will spare you this time; but remember, baa-baa-baa—you are not to provoke Hassan to question my existence again. Do not even think about it. Do it again, and everyone will only think you are a fool!" The clouds peeled away. You still stood in the courtyard; birds sang, servants' footstepsechoed as before. They told you Hassan had dashed off to chase his sheep and had already taken his leave. What had happened still lingered in your mind—but who would believe it?
