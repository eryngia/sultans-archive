A eunuch from the palace suddenly visits, claiming to serve Zazie, the most favored consort of the Sultan. The esteemed lady has recently become obsessed with a popular novel called "The Orphan's Revenge." Because she likes it, the entire harem has started reading it. Because the women of the harem like it, everyone is talking about it, and countless copies have been sold. You ask the eunuch, puzzled, what does this have to do with you? The eunuch respectfully tells you that the esteemed lady believes the 'master' in the book who helps the protagonist seek revenge looks exactly like you, and she wants to ask you to urge the author to write faster so she can read the ending sooner. You send the eunuch away, completely baffled. Before leaving, he leaves you a copy of "The Orphan's Revenge"...
