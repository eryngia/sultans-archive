Text:

This day, when you arrive at the Guesthouse, you see a crowd gathered around a man selling stuff... You go up to him, and he's holding a pile of odd things for sale; upon seeing you, he grins. "I thought it necessary to visit you, sir, that kid you let go a few days ago was one of mine." The people around you tell you the man’s name is Alim, the head of pickpockets in the Dark Alley, taking care of all the little rogues. You wonder how to deal with him...?

Text:

You reprimanded this head thief to keep his subordinates in line, to stop expecting to profit at your expense. He grinned, ignoring your words. "If the lads try to nab your wallet, it's a compliment, saying you look wealthy! Only poor folks don't attract pickpockets, am I wrong? But there's a way to keep the lads off your back..." He gathered the stolen goods, displayed before you. "If you help balance their loss from not snagging your fine wallet... maybe I'd have more time to watch them, hehe, hehe, hehe..." You saw the stolen goods, all wrapped in a black bag—you had no idea what you might end up buying.

Text:

You praised Alim regardless of his intentions, for looking after the little pickpocket well. The kid had clean clothes, shoes, and no signs of hunger. Alim seemed surprised, then grinned goofily. "The old master knows how to talk sweet, heh, heh, heh... I merely want to take care of my own money-makers. The lads are my hands, eyes, and clips for wallets. When they're fed and warm, they work better... Let's not talk about this, would you like to see my wares?" He gathered the stolen goods, opened them before you. Everything wrapped in black bags—that meant you didn't know what you’d actually get if you bought any.
