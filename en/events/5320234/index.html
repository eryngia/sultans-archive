After the morning assembly, the Sultan suddenly stops Nawfal. "I hear you have quite a taste for hosting gatherings," the Sultan says with a smile, idly toying with a parchment scroll in his hand. "And I hear criticizing me has become quite the fashion at these gatherings of yours." At these words, you quickly drop to your knees, frantically explaining your innocence – you were merely swept along by that scoundrel Nawfal's influence, the liquor loosening your tongue... but your loyalty to the Sultan has never wavered! "Hmm, I'm told you did speak some words in my favor." The Sultan examines his nails with practiced indifference. "So, I've decided to give you a chance to prove your loyalty," he adds with a knowing smile.
