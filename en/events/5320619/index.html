Text:

The eunuch who had come for the pendant returned once more. He said the harem's slave-girls had pooled together to prepare a token of gratitude for you. You tried to decline, saying it was Jenna who had made the jewels and any thanks should go to her, not you. "We are lowly folk, not worth mentioning; but... we do live in that Lapis Lazuli cage." The eunuch smiled, a sly look on his aged face. "We know harp-strings do not sing on their own—gratitude belongs to the hand that plucks them." He left the gift, bowed deeply, and hobbled away.
