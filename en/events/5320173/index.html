Text:

Maggie comes to you with her dowry female slave, Inal, hoping you can spare a few minutes of your time for this girl. Inal kneels and recounts a dream that has troubled her for a long while. In the dream, she stands inside a sturdy house, warm and safe. Then, suddenly, she realizes she is also in the wilderness, meaning she is in two places at once. There are wolves in the wilderness, and she knows they are approaching, so she wonders, if she opens the door to hide in the house, will she also bring in the wolves, endangering the one inside? Dreaming, she stands at the house's door, shivering until she wakes. How should one interpret this dream...
