Text:

After so many talks on poetry with Hassan, you felt you wanted to try writing a poem yourself... So that night you strained in your study for quite a while and finally produced these lines: "If fate were steered by no hand, as when fine wine gose untasted, its sharpness sipped by worms." You did not know whence the lines had come, but all in all, they were rather good, weren't they? You left the poem on your desk and put it out of mind.
