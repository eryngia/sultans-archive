You lean close to Nabhani, urging him to take a closer look at the masked guest's ring, and then softly advise him that relinquishing the key is not disgraceful; it is merely prudent self-preservation. Undoubtedly, he is the most popular man, and the girls' perception of him won't change with the transfer of the key. Nabhani smiles wryly at you and says nothing. At this juncture, what choice does he have? He stands up, approaches the masked guest, and respectfully presents the golden key to the opponent. Although Madam Buthayna doesn't know the masked guest’s identity, her shrewdness quickly graspes his significance, unhesitant in leading him inside. The women quietly expressed their resentment, reluctantly leaving Nabhani's side. The other male guests, not understanding the situation, cursed the masked guest for not inviting them to join in the fun, but they could do nothing about it. You accompany Nabhani for a while longer, helping him digest the bitterness of his disappointment. After that, you both go home separately.
