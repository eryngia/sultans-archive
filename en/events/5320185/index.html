Text:

You told the Sultan that the female slave was just a little brat, with nothing appealing about her, not even worth your attention. You indignantly added that she had been publicly harassed by some damned fellow earlier and you had reason to believe that this whole thing was an outrageous attempt by him to use the Sultan's authority to attack you! "Consider it... if I presented such an unremarkable and ordinary female slave to the great you, how could I redeem such a sin?" You bowed to the Sultan, pleading for the person to be brought to the court for you to confront face to face. The Sultan nodded. So be it. He liked to see his subjects in relentless disputes; it meant there would be a little red blood spilled in the palace today.
