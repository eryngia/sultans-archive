Text:

A wanton Noble lady demands the Sultan's judgment on her marriage. She married a Noble, but the husband, feeling insecure about his performance in bed, secretly had his brother fulfill his marital duties. The Noble lady didn't realize that the nightly visitor wasn't her husband until her husband died unexpectedly in a fall, and the unaware brother sneaked into her bedroom that very night! Now, the enraged and grieving Noble lady demands the entire property of her deceased husband's family – including the brother's share – as compensation for the family's deceit.
