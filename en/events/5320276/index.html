Text:

While walking in your garden... a woman in dirty wrappings suddenly jumps from the bushes, colliding with you. As you are about to scold her, she raises her head and gestures "shh"... It's Zazie! This favored concubine has stripped off all her jewelry and gold. She dresses like a slave girl, smears with something foul-smelling, and her hair is made deliberately dirty and messy... If not for those leopard-like eyes, you wouldn't recognize her! "I don't trust eunuchs to fetch something this important, so I disguised myself... But I can only be away briefly... the Sultan wants me constantly these days." She glances back nervously. "Come on, let's talk inside." She takes your hand, but her eyes question you about that item... "You're impressive! I never imagined anyone could complete such a complex plan under the Sultan's nose!" You can't help but feel nervous: Zazie already knew you had succeeded! Hardly anyone knew about it at all!

Text:

You dutifully hand over the small bottle you worked so hard to obtain. Zazie is overjoyed: "I got it... I can use this to stuff that damned Highlord's mouth! But... I can't help wondering, what will he demand next? What if next time he wants the Sultan's - eyeballs or something, what then?" She clutches the bottle tightly, staring into your eyes... "Let me be honest with you, you might think I'm crazy - but my wish was... power, power greater than the Sultan's! I don't want to be just a vessel for the next Sultan... God, I want to manipulate others' fates too!" She stares at you, seemingly waiting for you to contradict her. But you remain silent... "The Highlord promised to help me, but now I realize relying solely on him isn't the solution. I must give him whatever he demands, which is unacceptable... and I doubt he can truly fulfill my wish anyway. So... I'd like your advise..."
