You tell her that she has already found her place—this city needs someone to occupy it, and she is doing it quite well. People should not lightly leave their position, just as a tree should not lightly move its roots. So, maintaining the present state is best—and often the hardest thing. Raed nods, seeming to agree. "Yes... When I ponder these matters, I also wonder—if in the future I'm no longer the vagrants' leader, what will they do? A little girl who comes from nowhere has kept them alive this long—where will they find another Raed?" She smiles, picking up the rose tea the servant prepared and drinking it all in one gulp. "I'll be Raed freely—the vagrants' leader. There's plenty they need me to do." And so, the Raed you knew returns—the rough, messy one who laughs loud and picks her nose.
