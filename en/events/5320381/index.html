马尔基娜来找你了，她心情似乎极好，嘴里哼着歌，手中抱着一大捆布料。 穿过庭院来到你面前之后，她行了一个漂亮的礼，而后，就把那些布都摊在了你的面前。 “我觉得这些比较适合神明！” ……哈？你听的一头雾水，而她则一拍脑门，大笑了起来。 “我忘了说前因后果了——您一定要原谅我，因为我太兴奋了！总之啊，托您的福，我觉得自己现在已经学会如何从里而外地塑造一个人了——所以，现在我想塑造更大的东西！” 你沉吟着，她的意思该不会是说…… “您就不好奇吗？不好奇神明是什么样子的吗？为什么祭司们要这么穿，为什么神的形象要是那样？说不定我也能把凡人打扮得跟神一样……说不定人们崇拜的说到底只是一副神圣的外壳！” 你小心地问她，这个“凡人”是……？ “当然是您啦！只要您点头，我就试试看怎么把您打扮成大家心目中的神！试试看吧——哦，您一定要点头，这一定超级好玩！” 你重重叹了口气…… 要点头吗？

Text:

You give a slight nod—and in that instant, Malkina lets out a cheer and throws herself at you! "I knew you'd agree! I knew you were like me! That's why I followed you... why I showed up in this game! I've thought of so, so many ways—what should a god look like? I've studied how those temple styles—oh, I've done so much research!" She pulls a few scrolls from the cloth and spreads them before you. The scrolls are covered with embroidery patterns of every kind, all in the traditional styles considered "sacred." "Leave the clothes to me—but there are two things only you can do!" You ask what they are. "First, an attendant—no god can wander about alone; there has to be an entourage. You must find an attendant. I'll dress that person too, and then you two will appear together!" ...Fine, and the second thing? "Power! A god must have might, right? You can't rely on appearances alone!" The moment you begin to seriously consider this, Malkina sees the light hidden deep within your heart... She is startled for only a second—then she lights up like a blazing torch with excitement. "No seamstress or cosmetician has ever done this—they only ever shape a mortal body. But I—oh heavens—I am going to shape a god! Isn't that the pinnacle of craft? I can't imagine anything happier... Thank you, thank you!" She kisses your cheek and prostrates herself. As for you... now you have to consider how to become a god.

You shake your head and refuse the absurd proposal. You have already walked too far down the path toward godhood, and you fear this will be the final step... Malkina is clearly devastated. When you try to explain that mortals are exactly as timid, incapable, and unremarkable as yourself, she leaves the room on her knees.
