Text:

This morning, sudden clamor and the intense scent of roses reached from outside your home. Startled, you opened the door to find an incredibly luxurious palanquin carried by eight slaves stopped at the entrance, with Nayla sitting atop it. She reclined against a mountain of cushions, wearing white gauze of unimpeachable purity, her hands full of gold bracelets and gems, gold adorning her ankles. "The empire's most beautiful woman wants to marry you, my dear." She lifted her gold-powdered eyelids and raised her voice, "Will you accept me as your wife?" The onlookers erupted in cheers. Nayla must have calculated that such a public proposal would put you in a moral bind, betting you wouldn’t reject a lady’s proposal in public... Of course, she’s quite charming and has plenty of properties; she might indeed be a good choice for a new wife... What will you decide?

Hearing your answer, Nayla excitedly stood up from the palanquin and pounced straight into your arms. You barely caught her and her belongings. Around you, people erupted into a huge cheer, and at that moment, they seemed to forget her bad reputation... Now she is nestled in your arms, shedding tears of happiness, practically like a simple little girl. After this, she had the dowry moved into your courtyard, and the valuable jewelry and veils caused countless men to exclaim in envy, and you knew from this moment on, you can't reconsider. Nayla would never allow such a thing to happen.
