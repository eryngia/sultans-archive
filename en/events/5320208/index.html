Text:

You told Nawfal to help the poor, we must help the strongest and most vulnerable among them – the impoverished mothers. He pondered for a while and admitted you were right. But how to achieve this, how to try? Over a pot of tea, you both discussed and planned how much money, how many people it would require. "Let's start from your territory, give it a try," he concluded. "I'll pay half, you'll pay half. If it works well, we'll try it in my territory too, again half and half." Thus it was agreed.

Rite:

God's Grace

Prompt:

ID:

5320208_prompt_02

Text:

As a power holder, you know the entire empire is built on injustice. Despite your and Nawfal's efforts, the existence of nobles means draining the lifeblood of commoners... But at least you can help them accept their fate and live a bit happier. You suggested organizing rituals and celebrations, encouraging everyone to listen to sermons and understand that worldly suffering is a necessary path to eternal paradise... Nawfal remained silent for a long time then sighed deeply. Maybe, maybe you were right. With a pot of tea, you both discussed and planned the costs and manpower required for such an endeavor. "Let's start from your territory, give it a try," he said. "I'll pay half, you'll pay half. If it works well, we'll try it in my territory too, again half and half." Thus it was agreed.
