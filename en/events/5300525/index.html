The Sultan had a strange dream that left him unsettled. He asked you to interpret the dream for him. In the dream, the Sultan saw a beautiful woman standing on the edge of a cliff. As he approached her, she began to slowly jump off the cliff, and he ran to see her shattered body below. Then, he suddenly saw the woman standing next to him again, jumping off the cliff and shattering once more. This scene repeated countless times until he finally woke up.
