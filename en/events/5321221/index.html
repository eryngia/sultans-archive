Text:

Raed went to you. Wary, she had surely heard from the streets that you've been prying into her affairs. "What do you want? To blackmail me? I've nothing left worth a lord's blackmail." She laughed coldly and opened her hands. "Or did you hear some tragic tale and want to give us more coins? I won't refuse that." You told her you mean no harm—at least not in your asking—only curiosity. "Then I must ask a favor in return." Arms folded, Raed stared at you. "Never speak of this to anyone."

Text:

You nodded and told her you would never tell anyone, because you knew it might cost Raed her life. You also warned her that though she had concealed her noble schooling from childhood very well, her movements and posture still bore traces—about velvet carpets, about the curtsies of the nobility. Raed was silent. After a while, she drew a dagger, touched its blade to her own brow, and then handed it to you. "This is a token that we now share this secret... that I have entrusted it to you."

Text:

You told Raed you could not do that—because you needed her to serve you in return for your silence. If one day you judged her service wanting, you would hand her and her vagrants to the Sultan; you guessed he would take quite an interest in his half-sister—perhaps an interest not limited to taking off her head. Raed stared at you for a long time, then let out a soft laugh. "Fine. That's as it should be—people should use one another; whatever you say." She gave you a dagger as a token—symbol that you now knew her secret... and from then on she was in your hand.
