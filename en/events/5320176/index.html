Not long after, you glimpse Inal under a tree in the courtyard, humming a song while chopping something. A sweet fragrance drifts over, and you realize she's cutting ripe dates, seemingly garnished with honey, figs, and the like – these are probably what she bought with the money you gave her. Beside her is a small bowl of ice, and the chopped dates glisten with a delightful syrup. The ice has been finely crushed and mixed with cheese. Everything is prepared as Inal takes this delicious treat and runs towards the corridor. Who's on duty outside today...? It should be Aziz, right? You don't make a sound and quietly leave.
