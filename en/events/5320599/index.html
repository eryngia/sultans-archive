Text:

Seeing you turn to this page, Jenna self-consciously curled a lock by her temple. "Ah—this isn't meant to trouble you; take it as a joke if you like! See, every jeweler fights for the great ladies' gold. But ordinary folk outnumber them by the hundreds, even thousands! Many cheap stones are quite pretty; mixed colors and patterns can be designed to charm.. It's a shame to discard them just because they're cheap. I want to make jewelry the commoner can afford—" You listened to her earnest pitch and then glance at the title she gave this section. She blushed and murmured, "Oh, and of course, while no one is looking, I might slip some coins from those little purses into my own..." You could't help but laugh.
