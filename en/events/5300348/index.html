Text:

The maid who pleaded for her master came again! This time, she was tightly wrapped in a coarse cloak. When she stopped you, you noticed her eyes were red from crying. She looked at you desperately, knelt on the ground, 'I know that a bit of money cannot catch your eye... and I have nothing left but this barely acceptable body...' She said, loosening the cloak she was tightly holding. Only then did you realize she was naked under it. 'As long as you can help clear my master's name, I am at your disposal...' She said, sobbing and trembling, lying on the ground with her bare back illuminated beautifully by the sunset.

Sighing, you considered accepting her, but then you heard a commotion outside. Qais broke into your residence, harshly pulled the kneeling girl off the ground, and tightly wrapped her cloak around her. He then looked at you coldly: 'The Sultan Card can twist a person's heart, I knew that long ago. But you... you are truly shameless! I despise you.' Then he whispered something to the girl, making her burst into tears before walking out with him. After that, the news spread about your disgraceful attempt to coerce women into submitting – Heavens, you didn't even have time to do anything!
