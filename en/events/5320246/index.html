Text:

After braving the Sultan's detailed inquiries and finally breaking the Sultan Card, the ambiguous teasing in the Lapis Lazuli palace only increases. The courtiers crowd your shoulders with playful grins, saying that Lady Maggie looks so dignified, but in private, who would have thought... You're tired, weary of coping – until a cold snort suddenly rings out. "Who could have imagined the court would fall so low – and all because of you?" Nawfal, your political enemy, glares at you before raking his eyes over the courtiers. The nobles bow their heads, masking their weary "here he goes again" looks. Nawfal kneels before the Sultan, back rigid, voice ringing with conviction: "Your Majesty, this farce has gone on long enough! I beseech you – strip [player.name] of the power you bestowed. No one should be spared punishment for murder, adultery, and countless other sins, all for some wretched game!" He hesitates, eyes narrowing briefly, then bows deeply. "Such a right belongs to the king alone." The Sultan listens intently, a faint smile playing on his lips as he regards you, clearly weighing Nawfal’s words.

Text:

Could this be a good chance to escape this game? With this thought, you step forward as well, kneeling before the Sultan. You admit that Nawfal is right; the power and burden these cards bring have overwhelmed you.”: "Before I commit further absurd errors, I humbly implore Your Majesty to end this game. Thus, I am willing to bear the current accusations." "How could that be, my loyal subject?" the Sultan laughs heartily. Surely this is just what he expects. The moment you dare to confront and counsel him, he relishes your downfall, eager to see you corrupted like him, a mirror of his own soul. Nawfal wants to say more, but the Sultan waves him off. As you leave the court, Nawfal stops you and says coldly, "Dragging your family into this will only lead to disaster." His gaze is sharp, holding both warning and advice, yet the dark circles under his eyes stand out starkly against his pale skin. You sigh softly, don't say a word to him, and leave alone. What choice do you have now?

Text:

You immediately knelt on the ground, accusing Nawfal of having a wolf's ambition. Your king was only looking for a puppet show to enjoy, yet Nawfal dared to overstep? Who did he think he was? Your flattery performance enraged Nawfal; with anger and disdain, he used his linguistic prowess to mock you as a mere clown, criticizing all your actions and words from the day you were born, while you adeptly countered his every move, quietly wondering why his smart head was devoted to remembering these old feuds... This was the most common scene in the Sultan's court. The nobles were always heatedly fighting for Gold Coins, power, and status, while the Sultan laughed heartily, indulging and even encouraging you to fight each other. Leaving the court, Nawfal stopped you, as such warning, "Don't think this is the end of the matter." His gaze was sharp, but there was a smidgeon of dark blue in his eyes, glaringly evident against his pale skin. It seemed he hadn't been sleeping well lately. You shrugged habitually, not engaging in conversation, and left directly.
