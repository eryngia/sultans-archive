Text:

Concubine Arzuna sends a gift from the harem—an honor granted only to those women who are especially favored by the Sultan. When you open the exquisite silk wrapping, a generous cascade of fragrant petals pours out. The eunuch messenger explains that Concubine Arzuna gathers them each morning outside her chambers and dries them by her own hand. "She says the petals may be steeped as tea. Such tea brings cheer and restrains impulses toward killing, violence, and wrongdoing," the eunuch recites stiffly, seeming not in the least aware of the peculiarity of his words. "She says all this is owed to your assistance. These are the words the honored Concubine Arzuna instructs me to deliver."
