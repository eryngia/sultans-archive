Text:

Hassan comes to see you. After only a few days apart, he looks noticeably thinner. "My old friend... my dear [player.name], last time was nearly scared me to death..." He takes your hands the moment he sees you. "So this is what it feels like to serve the Sultan... and this is what you face every day... By the heavens, if I were you, I couldn't endure a single day!" He tells you that since that "royal delicacy," the mere sight of pomegranate seeds, caviar, tuna and other delights turn his stomach. As a result, he has grown thinner and even drank less than before. "Now I think... home-cooked food is the best! Nothing beats home cooking!" Hassan says sincerely. "I wrote a little poem—for you—about home-cooked fare. Oh, my friend, thank you. Without you, I would likely be a headless corpse today!" He seems to be in fine spirits. It looks like his accidental diet has done him some good.
