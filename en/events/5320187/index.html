Text:

Inal finds you, her usually lively face now gravely serious as she kneels and presents her request. "If you allow, I'd like to buy my freedom. I can gather the money; madam has agreed. She even allowed me to save for it..." She shyly presents a handful of small change, totaling about two gold coins. You're somewhat surprised and ask why she suddenly has this thought. Inal once more brings up the dream that made her uneasy, "That depraved wolf of a noble, the merciless indulgence of the Sultan... if not for your kindness, I'd have been sent to them already, right? It's all because I'm a slave, all my happiness, all my joy, are reflections on water, shatterable with a touch from master or master's master. But if I were free..." She bites her lip, determined, "I think I'd... have more choices." You want to tell her even free people have masters, like courtiers oppressed by the Sultan. But it seems since that the vague dream of freedom has already lodged in her heart. What is your decision?

Text:

You took the money. She let out a long sigh, her lively character prevailing again. She said she checked out slave prices everywhere, feeling that those sold for one Gold Coin couldn't be as good as her, while those sold for three Gold Coins were prettier than her. She shyly admitted that the money included your wife's usual reward and some that "others chipped in for me." Saying so, she probably remembered to share the good news with her companions and left joyfully after bidding farewell.
