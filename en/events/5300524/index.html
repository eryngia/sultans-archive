Text:

A river that flowed through the territories of two Nobles suddenly changed its course recently. Now it flows through one Noble’s land but hardly through the other’s. The latter presented this matter to the Sultan, requesting an adjustment of their land borders. He is willing to cede a larger piece of land in exchange for the area where the river flows. The Sultan asked what you think about this matter...

Since the river is changing course, it shows that it's tired of that noble, right? When you say that, the noble is furious while the other noble laughs happily. He nonchalantly walks to your side and chats casually. From now on, he will be your ally at court. As for the one who loses the river, his territory will soon wither, and he himself will naturally not appear in court, causing trouble for you.

You step in front of the guard, draw his sword, and throw it between the two nobles, telling them to let the blade decide. The Sultan is entertained, clapping his hands and urging them to fight to the death. The two nobles nervously pick up the sword, trying to maintain their dignity, and then begin to fight fiercely. Ultimately, one will survive covered in the other’s blood, winning the land and the river. But apart from the Sultan, no one in the palace will cheer for this.
