Text:

After Jawad’s death, Zephyr seems very anxious, and other servants say he often stays out all night… Just as you plan to discuss this with him, he suddenly brings a woman dressed in coarse hemp. “I love this woman, and she loves me. We want to live together. Master, I will continue to serve you, I will share my food with her, please permit this.” The woman trembles all over, sticking close behind him, like a leaf lost from its tree, or a little fish lost from its school. According to The Sultan’s law, a slave is not entitled to have a wife. What will you do?

At your words, Zephyr's eyes widen; he can hardly believe what he's hearing. You repeat yourself, asking if he would continue working for you as a free man. Of course, you'll pay him wages – modest, but enough for him and his wife. Zephyr stands stunned, trembling from overwhelming kindness, unable to speak. The girl behind him, his soon-to-be wife, loses patience. She fiercely grabs his arm and bites it, snapping him back to reality. They both kneel at your feet, expressing their gratitude for your generosity, then leave together. Zephyr looks at her, and she looks back at him, their steps light as if they could float away.

You assure Zephyr and his woman that within your mansion's walls, they are safe – no one can harm them or separate them. They kneel at your feet, expressing their deep gratitude for your mercy and kindness. As they prepare to leave, Zephyr lets the woman go first, but remains kneeling. He looks up at you and says: "You know her true identity; I cannot hide it from you... She's by my side now, and I swear she'll never bring you harm, nor will I ever hurt her, my master. You must trust me!" You simply nod, preferring to let the matter rest.
