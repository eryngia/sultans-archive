You knew [xiaochou.name]'s doubts would never fully fade—and sure enough, it came to you... though only in dreams. "I never desired to conquer anything—not people, not lands." The dream-version of [xiaochou.name] stood in a corridor lined with mirrors, countless reflections of you and [xiaochou.name] stretching into infinity. "But watching you struggle with conquest... it tempts me. After all, humans crown it a noble endeavor. Wrap slaughter, lust, or sheer stupidity in conquest's cloak, and suddenly, it gleams with honor." You stayed silent for a moment before replying: "That very thought proves how little you still understand us." "Really? Then now is the perfect time." [xiaochou.name]'s voice turned sly. "Thanks to you, Master [player.name], my power has grown... enough to do things you might not approve of. Watch closely—see how I conquer." In the mirrors, every reflection of you and [xiaochou.name] blurred, warping into the faces of nobles, mercenaries, officers, and bandits you'd once brushed past. [xiaochou.name]'s influence—and yours—seeped into them like poison. You reached out to stop it—but the dream shattered first.
