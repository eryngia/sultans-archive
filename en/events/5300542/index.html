Text:

Riel comes to you aggressively this day. Behind her, two strong enforcers vigilantly observe the situation, while someone stays by her side, constantly keeping one hand on their knife hilt. "Hey, I heard something that makes me feel I should do something, but can't. I hate this feeling, so I came for your help - since you're the craftiest city dweller I know." She winks at you, an expression mixing intimacy with contempt. "You probably frequent brothels, right?" Before you can answer, she waves dismissively, as if the answer doesn't matter. "My people tell me some of my tribe have been taken to a place called 'House of Delights' - a brothel, filthy place." She says, "I want to charge in and rescue them - my people learn killing and hunting from childhood, not spreading their legs! But... uh, the place has tight security, tighter than the Jackals, I'd say." "Help me get inside. I'll repay you." She stares at you directly, her tone completely unapologetic.
