Text:

A person has been pacing in front of your door for a long time, holding a long, slender package. You notice it is Qais, the overly Forthright Courtier, now showing some form of contemplation. You send someone to invite him into the study to inquire about his purpose. He grits his teeth and places the package on your desk, uncovering the old silk covering it. "This is my family's ancestral sword – I know your family must also have one, but mine is older and sharper... Alas, I present it to you." He looks as though he wants to say more, but after hesitating for a moment, he said nothing, saluted, and prepared to leave.

Text:

You sensed he's concealing something. When pressed, he smiled wryly and said, "Sometimes I wish you weren't so perceptive." He recounted his family's history—territories, mansions, and properties lost over two generations. By the time he inherited his title, even his hereditary succession rights were gone—he's now the last of the Qais family entitled to call himself a noble. His mother remained deeply troubled by this, and your recent prominence had rekindled what he called "inappropriate hope" within her. "Truthfully, I've abandoned such aspirations, considering our sovereign... but my mother threatens to take her own life..." He sighed helplessly and shook his head. 'Forget this matter. Pretend I never came—she'll eventually accept reality." You nodded thoughtfully. You could indeed speak to the Sultan at court, giving him a chance to reclaim his hereditary rights. Elevating an ally's status would benefit you as well, but you wondered: is it wise to draw the Sultan's attention to him again?
