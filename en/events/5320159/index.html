Text:

Passing by the slaves' banquet, you notice the meal box in Aziz's hand seems a bit special. It's not the style you provide for the slaves, but a meal box made from hollowed-out white stone. Seeing you notice him, Aziz stands up slowly and greets you, "If you will allow, I could introduce my meal box, master." He tells you it's made from a kind of refractory stone used for fixing stoves. It cools food slower when stored, making it taste better, and if you store leftovers inside covered, they keep longer. You take the meal box and scrutinize it. Although it's covered in indelible grease stains, the meal box indeed has a unique feel to it, it's a good thing.
