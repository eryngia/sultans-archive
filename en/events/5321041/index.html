Text:

An uninvited guest shows up suddenly. She is wrapped in exotic silks, with each strand glimmering with gold. It’s unimaginable how exquisite her attire should be if such precious fabric is only used to cover her face. She slightly bows to you before suddenly pulling down the veil. “I am Arumina, I heard my father discussed my marriage with you, allow me to beg you… my marriage is my own choice, don’t listen to my father’s nonsense.” You examine her clear yet naive eyes… she is far from being an unparalleled beauty, not yet mastering the attire and jewelry on her, which is quite endearing.
