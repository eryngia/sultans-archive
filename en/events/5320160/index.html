Text:

This evening, the courtyard fills with a strange fragrance. You go out to observe. To your eyes, you see Inal and Aziz leaning against each other, sharing a small handful of red berries – such fruits have recently been imported to the Sultanate; many nobles have tasted them. But slaves doing so... perhaps only these two before you have. Noticing your presence, Inal jumped up in fright, while Aziz remained unhurried. "I bought them with your rewards, if you allow. If you don't, please punish me." He stuffed the remaining berries into his big mouth, then silently kneeled down, preparing to receive a whipping.

Text:

You bypass him and walk directly to Inal. "How do the berries taste?" you ask. "Are they delicious?" She trembles, unable to form a proper reply—her usual sharp tongue is entirely gone. Behind you, Aziz, still kneeling, speaks slowly, "They are quite sour. They should be eaten with honey. But I only had enough money for a small handful of berries, and I didn't want to steal honey from the kitchen—that would be wrong. Still, with honey, the sourness and sweetness together, maybe with some ice cream, it would be incredibly delicious." You are stunned. That is, in fact, exactly how the popular court desserts are prepared these days. Though only a slave, Aziz's tongue is as discerning and sensitive as that of a noble. You sigh, comfort Inal, and then let Aziz stand up. You assure him that you are not those stingy masters who reward their slaves with money yet don't let them spend it. Aziz slowly nods, "If you permit it... then I'll remember it, Master."
