Text:

The day after quelling the rebellion, Abdul commits suicide at home. He knew what was good for him - otherwise, his entire family might have died. No one cares about his death because all eyes focus on the vacant Vizier position he left... The court erupts in a new round of verbal battles for this chair. Finally, the Sultan grows tired. He simply points to your name, making you Vizier - since you suppressed the rebellion Abdul caused, you can handle the rest of his mess. The couriters are silent for a moment, then they all congratulate you with smiles, warmly interacting with you. Surrounded by flattering voices, you feel a bit elated...
