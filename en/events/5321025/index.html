Text:

Due to negligence, or perhaps due to a temporary financial shortcoming, you missed the chance to acquire the title. By the time Qais learned of this news, there was no time to gather funds – he could only watch as the family’s glorious title slipped into someone else's pocket again. It's said his mother fell ill due to the result, and Qais became somewhat despondent as a result... “Please don’t apologize, friend, I am very grateful for all you’ve done for me. I just don’t understand…” he can't help but quietly seek answers from you, “In your view, are titles and past glories really so important?”

You unhesitatingly tell him that past glory is certainly important, and we must not forget it. It's our surname and our ancestors’ efforts that allows us to stand here. Text:

You look straight into his eyes, telling him that if those empty titles are so important, you wouldn't have stood up to denounce the Sultan originally. However, even if time can turn back, you will still make the same choice. You pause, then shift your gaze away and quickly add, "I'm very sorry... I mean, I didn't mean to suggest this wasn't important, just that I didn't tell you about the news at the time. Actually, I intended to buy the title directly for you, but I didn't expect it would..." "You don't need to apologize for such matters..." He is bewildered for a moment before sighing, "Moreover, you’re right. I also think it's not important... we... we’re similar people." He gently graspes your arm, a rare relaxed smile on his face. Never before has your souls feel so close, seemingly already merged as one.
