Text:

Inal comes home in a panic, telling you she saw the wolf of her dream. It happened while she was at the market buying ingredients – while picking a fat duck. "A duck must have plump wings to be tasty, then I opened the wings to inspect... oh, sorry master, I digress... then he appeared! He looked like a wolf... his eyes were glowing green! He stared at me, asked whose family I belonged to! I was terrified, I... I dropped the duck and ran home! Thankfully I hadn't paid for it yet... I brought back all the money – oh, master, what should I do, things from dreams are coming out!" She gesticulates frantically, tears on her face, visibly panicked. Just as you consider how to comfort her, you notice the large slave, Aziz, seems to be quietly eavesdropping from outside the courtyard.
