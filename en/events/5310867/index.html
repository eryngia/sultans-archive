Text:

Your presence in the mortal realm has reached its limit. Since the beginning of all things, no great god has ever done what you have: intervene in the mortal world so directly, in divine form. This is not only because you are a newborn god, a god of cycles, a god who slew gods... but also because this place is the highest peak of the mortal realm. As you draw near the earth, you see it: the great tower of desire, the pinnacle of power... built from the suffering and stories of all living things. Even yours are among them—your life as a mortal. Like you, it twists, piles, records, and climbs through cycle after cycle... until it nears the domain of the gods. At its peak stands the mightiest mortal of all, daring to look down on you, a god descended to earth.
