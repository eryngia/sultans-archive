Text:

A half-grown lad was sent to find you. He wore a set of clean yet ragged clothes, and pride gleamed on his thin face. He bowed and handed you... a pigeon—yes, a pigeon. A dead pigeon. A very plump one. Seeing your confusion, the boy raised his voice: "I'm a butcher's apprentice. This is today's pigeon from my family for you. Isn't your lady wife building up her strength? She needs pigeon every week? So I'll be bringing one every week. You don't need to worry about anything else: pigeons only go coo-coo-coo, pigeons never cause trouble, and pigeons know their master; they know which eaves are the safest." He rattled it all off fast and hard, clearly memorized. When he finished, he bowed with great pride and left your residence. Setting aside what all that was supposed to mean, you examined the bird—it had been cleaned to perfection, and it did look rather quite suitable for roasting... perhaps you should have Habib cook it.
