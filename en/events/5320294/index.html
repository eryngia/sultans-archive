Text:

This day, Hassan came pale-faced; you had never seen him look so stricken. Trembling, he handed you a thick sheet of vellum. It was the Sultan's decree: "Your poems in praise of [player.name]'s table have reached even Us. Come taste the royal cook's work—let Us hear what you can write." Sweat crawls down your spine. You never thought such poems would reach the Sultan's ears... A minister's household feast with such renown—the sovereign will not be pleased... "Please—go with me. You know me—I'm hopeless when I'm nervous—and what could be more terrifying than standing before the Sultan himself? [player.name], I beg you!" Hassan pleaded again and again, and you too felt you must go; if he performed poorly before the Sultan, the consequences could be far worse... You made an appointment with the eunuch who brought the decree: <size=+10>3 days</size> from now you will enter the palace. Remember: <size=+10>prepare the poetry well</size>, and do not miss the time. To keep the Sultan waiting is a crime punishable by death!
