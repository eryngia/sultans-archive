Text:

When you returned home from the palace that day, Arzuna was waiting for you in the reception hall. She was dressed in full regalia, her hair elaborately braided, her face painted more gorgeously than usual. She looked up at you with a mysteriously knowing smile... "I've realized something, my dear Lord [player.name]. I know how you should use me... Send me to the Sultan. You will have an ally, a supporter, a follower, a servant in the sovereign's harem—trust me, I can capture his heart. Oh, I've always been good at this!" You were startled by her ambition, but after calming down, you studied her and realized her charm was indeed irresistible. Perhaps she really could satisfy the Sultan... What do you say?

Text:

Think of it this way: if one of the Sultan's concubines stands with you, those who oppose you will find it difficult to voice their objections. Besides, ever since Arzuna came, your household has rarely been at ease. Perhaps sending her away is not a bad solution... You agree to her request, though you caution her that powerful courtiers often offer beauties to their ruler. Yet once the beauty enters the harem, she must depend upon her own wit and capability. She lowers her head obediently, saying she knows this well. Still... "If you might smooth the way for me a little, it would be better. After all, who knows the Sultan's tastes more thoroughly than you, my lord?" Indeed, the woman you present must neither seem shabby nor prove herself lacking in propriety...

Text:

After much consideration, you refuse her. The harem is a place of tangled alliances and rivalries. She is only a dancer, untrained in such intrigues, and she cannot even read. Sending her there would likely bring you greater trouble. There is also another concern you do not speak aloud—you still worry whether this suddenly appeared sister harbors resentment toward you. If she does, placing her at the Sultan's side would be far too dangerous... Arzuna says nothing. She simply smiles, bids you farewell, and then takes her leave. The smile leaves you deeply uneasy.
