Text:

You asked Malkina whether she had distilled anything of the face of power. Smiling, Malkina brought out paints and fabrics and dabbed here and there on you as she spoke. "Power is confidence, isn't it? The more confident you are, the more power you hold—and only the confident can truly maintain it. Otherwise, even if you get it, you cannot keep it. I learned that from your stories. So—flatten the brows, relax the expression, a touch of boredom and a touch of alertness... Make the back hem longer than the front, since you don't bow often but must stand tall... Ah, and not too many jewels, but each one must be precious; and makeup light, tones gentle..." When she finished, you looked in the mirror and found the person there truly looked like someone who had spent too long in the Lapis Lazuli Hall...
