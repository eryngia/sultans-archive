Text:

After you begin assembling your own forces, Jenna finds you. She presents you with a banner fashioned from thin sheets of gold, hanging on the wall like flowing liquid. The banner is studded with gemstones, and on closer look, the stones spell out your name. "I have always hoped to take part in deeds that change the world, because I've heard that only such deeds can spark a person's finest inspiration," Jenna says, looking into your eyes. "Now I finally have the chance to join the tide you have raised. So I made this banner... I am proud of it, and I am proud to have a friend like you." When this banner rises with the sun above the city walls, people gather behind you, believing that the rightful ruler has arrived.
