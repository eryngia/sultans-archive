Text:

Alim came to you, still wearing his teasing smile, still speaking of life-and-death matters. He told you one of his pups had suddenly gone missing; he had searched high and low, only to learn that the just-of-age boy had been dragged into a noble's residence and never seen again. He had asked around—beggars, street-sparrows, singing girls—and there had been many such cases of late; someone had been kidnapping people on the Dark Alley, and that kidnapper... Here Alim looked at you. "Someone saw you and him making a 'trade' in an alley." He had come to demand an accounting. You finally understood, hurriedly showed him the diamond you had bought, and told him everything you and Jenna knew. Alim nodded and gave you several out-of-the-way sites, said to be the gem-seller's bolt-holes, apparently linked by tunnels; old Alim had already mapped them for you. If you meant to shadow the merchant, his intelligence would surely prove useful.
