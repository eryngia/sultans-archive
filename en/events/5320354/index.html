Text:

At first light, Asal comes to you, looking extremely excited, and a fat bag of money is clutched in his hands. "Madame Lumera's book sold well... incredibly well! I already ordered a reprint! This is the share from the first edition—I will of course give you a share, just as a mirror naturally returns light to the sun!" Leaving aside that metaphor... you weigh the bag. It is indeed satisfying. "But there is a small problem now, for which I hope you can help... Just as a pomegranate tree needs a gardener to prune away the excess branches if it is to flourish." After saying this, he holds your gaze for a moment. Seeing that you haven't quite understood, he has to explain: "I mean... there are quite a few shoddy pirated copies appearing on the market now. All reprinted from my... no, our edition! If you can resolve this... our sales will do far better."
