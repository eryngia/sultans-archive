Text:

After a day's morning assembly, the Sultan suddenly stopped Nawfal. "I heard you have a penchant for gatherings," the Sultan said with a smile, fiddling with a scroll of parchment in his hand. "I also heard that criticizing me seems to be in vogue at such gatherings." Upon hearing this, you hurriedly knelt on the ground, repeatedly declaring your innocence; you were just unintentionally, unintentionally caught up in it and never spoke such words. The Sultan nodded to you tolerantly, "I know, [player.name], you may leave; even the traitor raised by this upright minister says you're incredibly loyal to me. Ah, even though it's a bit dull, hearing about loyal ministers once in a while is always pleasing." As you were about to exit the court with a smile, you heard Nawfal speak up beside you, his voice cold and stubborn: "I never said anything at those gatherings that I haven't said to your face. If you wish, I can say it again." His aloofness angered the monarch. Not surprisingly, the Sultan threw him into prison.
