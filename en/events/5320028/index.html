Text:

You follow Lumera through the bustling market, walking through the seeking eyes of the commoners. Finally, her steps halt in front of a low, shabby shanty in the slums, where a gaunt, filthy old man squats, daydreaming. Upon seeing Lumera, the man’s eyes suddenly brighten. He jumps up, trying to grab her hand, but you step in front of her. “I’m here for vengeance,” the girl calmly tells the man, “Father.” ... This is her father? Surprised, you glance at the man. His expression becomes even more enthusiastic; he rubs his hands together, mouth agape, showing a grotesque set of yellow teeth: "I knew you’d make it big! Are you a noble’s concubine? Or a top-grade prostitute? It’s time you give back to your dad. What did you bring for dad? Wine? Cigarettes?” Lumera’s ever-unchanging expression cracks slightly, and through the fissure, you glimpse pain. “I repeat, I'm here for vengeance,” she reiterates, clenching her fist.

The man just now finally understands her words, and scratches his head, "Revenge? What did daddy do? What are you talking about?" Lumera explains he sold her mother to a brothel in the Dark Alley in exchange of wines. When he had no money, he sought out mother and lied to her that he still loved her, let her go with him, then sold her again. Until she was captured by the owner of her brothel, until she was beaten by her owner, until she was dead from the owner's torture. Hearing that, the man feels surprised, "That's all? I didn't do anything wrong! The owner killed your mother, not me! I loved your mother, I just needed money at that time! I didn't have anything else to sell! How can a child blame her father due to such a thing – my lord, surely you agree? You won't allow a child to do such a cruel thing! I'm not a slave, I am a FREE MAN!" He yells madly, many people raise their heads from windows of slum and stare at you three, Lumera stares at you too. "You said you will help me. Then... will you help me kill him?"
