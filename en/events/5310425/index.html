Just as your army prepares to attack, two silent armies emerge from the other side of the world, still shrouded in darkness, advancing without banners, scouts, in tight phalanx, without a sound. Neither you nor The Sultan's armies can discern their origins, interrupted ka solutions o attack in this eerie atmosphere... Finally, in view, a pale white force of pure specters – their knights, armor, weapons, horses spectral. Another force is more terrible, appearing as rotting corpses wielding buried weapons, riding skeletal warhorses, trailed by slaves who clawed their graves... not far from reality. "Here are warriors pledged to the Fallen Dynasty until death – an oath of vengeance gathers them," the Roaming Swordsman indicates, "And there... are the Oathbreakers who surrendered to The Sultan, seeking restful sleep – now part of vengeance itself." "Ultimately, the gods leave us only dead men – let them lead the vanguard!" declares the nameless, countryless man as he rides to join the dead's ranks.
