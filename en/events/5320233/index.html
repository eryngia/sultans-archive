Through some circuitous means, Nawfal secretly meets you in a secluded place. He tells you that he has discovered the identity of the traitor from the banquet – the young noble who had been working for the vizier, his long-time rival. Nawfal has already expelled him from their ranks. "Our youth shouldn't be like this," he sighs. "They should be discerning, ambitious, carrying a flame that never dims." Lost in thought, Nawfal's expression grows somber. The path forward remains unclear – a question that will take time to answer.
