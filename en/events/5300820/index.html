You pointed to the sky above—the meaning was plain. "Ah... I see. So the high enjoy watching the low kill, and the low in turn take pleasure in seeing those beneath them do the same? Do nobles relish slaughtering common folk?" [xiaochou.name]'s words left the servants silent. Just as you had only dared to gesture toward the heavens—your servants, too, dared not speak of the rights and wrongs of nobility in your presence. "Arrange a meeting for me," [xiaochou.name] said. "Let me see these nobles and hear what they have to say."
