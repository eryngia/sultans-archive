After the morning assembly, the Sultan suddenly stops Nawfal. "I hear you have quite a taste for hosting gatherings," the Sultan says with a smile, idly toying with a parchment scroll in his hand. "And I hear criticizing me has become quite the fashion at these gatherings of yours." At these words, you quickly drop to your knees, frantically explaining your innocence – you were merely swept along by that scoundrel Nawfal's influence, the liquor loosening your tongue... but your loyalty to the Sultan has never wavered! "Oh, is that so?" The Sultan studies his nails with languid indifference. "That's not quite what I've heard, my dear subject. But I'll still give you a choice – either spend a few quiet days in the dungeons... or perhaps years, decades," he gives you an enigmatic smile. "Or, you could amuse me and beg for my mercy."
