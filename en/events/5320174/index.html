Text:

Perhaps to ask you not to drive her away or to prove her value, Inal served you a plate of ruby-like pomegranate syrup with crushed ice from the icehouse. She mixed the ice into the syrup and then kneeled to present it to you. With one bite, the sweet scent of pomegranate permeated your heart. With your praise, she withdrew happily, seemingly no longer bothered by the dream. After Inal left, your wife told you Inal was a houseborn slave born at her home, enslaved since her grandparents' generation, never experiencing the life of a free person or considering buying her freedom. "A simple-minded girl, great at cooking, a bit too talkative, but has always served well." You nodded, a strange thought causing some slight unease, but now... that's the best you can do.
