Text:

The old eunuch who had come for the collar returned once more, his gaze betraying an effort to conceal his displeasure. He told you that the ruby piece—the flawless coronet Jenna had made—had sparked a brawl among the slave girls. It was too precious, too beautiful! In the end a pale-skinned slave claimed it, biting a companion's nose in her triumph. Proudly adorned with the jewel and stained with blood, she knelt before the Sultan, declaring that she would kill for the immense favor of his bed—precisely the sort of words the Sultan relished. She did indeed win a night's favor, but her appearance and skill were unworthy of the royal bed. The dazzling jewel entered the Sultan's collection, and she was flung back among the slave girls—only to be immediately strangled by the companion she had wronged. The Sultan ordered the old eunuch to tell you he had greatly enjoyed the spectacle you had arranged; truly, you knew best how to please him.
