Text:

Samir sent a servant to fetch you, asking to meet the next day in the corridor behind the Audience Hall. As court physician, leaving the palace required no small amount of petitions and paperwork, so it was easier for you to go to him. When you found Samir, he was leaning against a column, head bowed, eyes on the floor. Hearing your footsteps, he looked up and smiled. "You came, my old friend. Oh, I asked for this meeting because I recently have noticed something puzzling." He drew from his robe the record of the harem women who had served the Sultan—yes, the one you had slightly altered for Lady Sadani—your heart gave a thump. You had, of course, expected someone to review it, but you had thought it would be those fat eunuchs who managed the harem; lying to them was easy, but to lie to Samir, the court physician... not so much Yet before you could weave a story, he slipped the booklet away as if it were nothing. "Naturally, such trifles like this wouldn't linger in my head; I would tell the Sultan everything was in order, nothing amiss. But..." He sighed lightly. "My friend, the next time you plan to alter something—I never said you altered anything—just tell me, alright? There are ways to make it look much neater." He winked; you both laughed. After that, you chatted pleasantly for a long while and then parted.
