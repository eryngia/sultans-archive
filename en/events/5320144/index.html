Text:

Nayla came to see you, telling you she wanted to know who the strongest man in the whole empire is – by "strongest," she means exactly what you think, the sensuality. "All women are the same, oh really! When we see a man adorned with sparkling gems and a sword, striding down the street, we ponder if he's just as impressive with nothing on, hm?" She giggled, "If you think some women don't think like that, it's because... they're not as naughty as me, hence not as... alluring, right?" Before you could answer, she waved a finger at you. "I know how to get the answer. If I do what needs to be done with a few more men, I can tell you who the strongest man in this capital is... Will you help me? Even if not to help a beautiful lady, then to satisfy your own curiosity, or perhaps your pride, hm?" While giggling, she rolled up a parchment with a long list of names and tucked it into your clothes, her fingers lightly brushing across your chest. "Beyond those listed, there's you too," she said with interest, "Of course, I've already sampled your flavor, but I've heard seeing something as a competition or test can better unleash one's potential... so, I look forward to your performance."
