[xiaochou.name] came to you again. This time, it appeared directly in your parlor, clutching several scrolls of parchment covered in writing, seemingly deep in thought. You approached and gestured to [xiaochou.name], who looked up at you and sighed. "I have seen much, heard much—even mimicked those poets and written much. And yet... it all feels so dull." You took the scrolls from its hands—they were scrawled with love poems. The words were carefully chosen, but the sentiment... well, something felt off. "I cannot live without you" sounded fine enough, but "You shall not live without me either"... was that really fit for a love poem? Just as you began to wonder if you had shown [xiaochou.name] the wrong examples of human desire, [xiaochou.name] continued: "You give it pretty names, but I see it clearly—humans are born in desire, struggle in desire, and drown in desire. I have witnessed this tale play out a thousand times. Is there nothing... fresher? Even just a little?"
