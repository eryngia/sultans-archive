Text:

You can't make a decision so easily; at least, you should listen to Kaya's side first. So, you ordered the old slave Kaya to be helped over. Upon seeing Kaya, Nayla acted like she'd been stung by a bee, abruptly standing up. She trembled, gritting her teeth, appearing like a hurt ten-year-old girl rather than a dignified noblewoman of many attractions. Kaya was evidently surprised as well, squinting her eyes and examining Nayla closely... Then she smiled gently: "You're so beautiful, young lady; indeed, I knew from the start you'd be a great beauty." Nayla sneered, spewing venomous curses at Kaya like a viper. She called Kaya a traitor, a whore, a harlot; she had believed in Kaya completely as a child, so when her mother told her Kaya was sneaking into her father's bed, everything she believed was destroyed – she grew more agitated, almost hitting the old slave, but you held her back. Kaya listened quietly, her smile unchanged, gazing at Nayla as if at a misbehaving child: "As you said, that's how things are, my young lady, my little princess. I'm sorry, I failed you. I know someone like me deserves to die like a fly by the roadside, but oh Lord, I actually got to see you again before departing! Thank the Lord, thank you." She bowed laboriously, kissing your and Nayla's toes in turn. Nayla stood frozen in place, repeatedly licking her lips like she wanted to speak but didn't know what to say. Kaya was helped away, leaving just you two staring at each other in the parlor. Then, as if to conceal something, she covered her face with a veil and ran out of your house.
