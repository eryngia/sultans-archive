Text:

That night, as you removed your jewels and placed them back into their casket, your jinn follower appeared beside you. She shrank herself tiny and stood inside your jewel chest, hopping from one gem to another, then clutching a cluster of gold thread to examine it. "You are truly a fine master, with so many wonderful treasures," she declared, lifting her chin with a proud smile on her delicate face. "But you could be greater still... I wish to be surrounded by even more splendors—and I can bless you, you know. I am a jinn, and jinns possess the power to bless. Wish to me, and I shall grant your desire... oh, but only one wish!"
