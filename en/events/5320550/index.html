Text:

On some night, you return once again to this void. Darkness spreads endlessly under your feet, like the cosmos, like despair, like an endless slumber from which you won't wake. You fall, and suddenly, cryptic hieroglypics and symbols once again float before your eyes. They bounce and zigzag, assembling into a question. "What do you want? What are you looking for by deciphering that book?" You realize it refers to the Book of Dragonslaying.
