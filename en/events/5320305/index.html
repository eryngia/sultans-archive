Hassan arrives in high spirits, saying that Nawfal has suddenly sent for him with a teaching post. "He said there's a place called the Nursery, full of children who can't yet read... He asked if I could teach them what poetry is—what the art of letters is—oh, I'd love to!" Cautiously, you suggest it might be best not to teach the children love poems... "Eh, we'll see when the time comes!" Hassan is as breezy as ever. "And I suppose that court official you're acquainted with won't keep me on a tight leash, will he?" You watch him sway away. Behind him, the little lamb pauses a moment and meets your gaze.
