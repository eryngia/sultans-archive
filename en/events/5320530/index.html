You rest your chin in your hand, deliberately looking Maggie up and down until she grows visibly nervous. Only then do you nod and say you can't see anything wrong – with her well-proportioned figure, she might actually make an excellent swordswoman. Maggie's cheeks turn slightly pink as she keeps protesting that you're teasing her – how could she ever be a swordswoman... but you can tell she's delighted. After some playful banter, Maggie snuggles up to you and says that when family commitments are less demanding, she'll ask Adila for more guidance on sword fighting.
