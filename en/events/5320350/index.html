Text:

At day's end, as you left the palace after audience, Nawfal remarked, half in passing, that a collection of thought from the former dynasty had appeared on the Angler's counter. How strange—he had thought he alone possessed that book in the whole realm. It was beyond doubt he had seen through the whole affairs; his face was calm—tinged with displeasure, but not anger. In that habitual needling tone of his, he blandly warned you not to bring trouble down on your head, as though you lacked the cultivation to read, to understand how sharp those ideas were. Hmph! You tossed him a "mind your own business" look; and in full view at the palace gate you nearly fell out brawling again. Though you parted on bad terms, you had to admit his warning carried weight. Back home, you had Asal read the text carefully that very night to see what passages might need trimming...
