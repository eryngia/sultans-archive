Text:

The atmosphere between Adila and Maggie seems strange to you... That night, you went to find Maggie and asked her about her impression of Adila as a swordsmanship teacher. "Oh, she might be the most powerful woman, right? No woman can hold a sword so steadily, and stab so deeply!" Maggie's eyes sparkled, "I believe if dragons existed, she would definitely slay them." An unreasonable jealousy started bubbling inside you; Maggie has never praised you like that... Certainly, you've never thought about slaying dragons, but... Perhaps sensing your displeasure, Maggie subdued her enthusiasm and promised she wouldn't practice with Adila anymore... You open your mouth – it is indeed the result you wanted, but is it what Maggie wants? Because of your unfounded feelings, should you take away Maggie's cherished interest and laughter?
