As fervent crowds gather in your name, as miracles unfold one after another, minor disorder inevitably arises within your army. At that moment, a row of stars begin to shine, revealing themselves in the broad daylight. They arrange themselves into arches and beacons, guiding the soldiers' formation before descending as luminous warriors who stand at the head of every line. It's the stars! They arrive to honor their covenant with the fallen dynasty! To fulfill the promises of the past and bear witness to the dawn of a new era—this time, the stars themselves will stand at the forefront to record the story.
