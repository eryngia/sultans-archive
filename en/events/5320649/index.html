"You might just be able to do it—you might! Let our Mistress enter this world. Let her see it with her own eyes. Let her taste what it means to be bound by flesh!" "My Mistress may not be the strongest being you could ever glimpse, but she is without doubt the most beautiful, the most elegant, the most noble... She cast us aside into this place freely, for this is a barren, material world! But I think it is only right we invite her to come and see for herself!"
