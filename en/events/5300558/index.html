In the Dark Alley, the Sultan's law is just one way to solve problems. Many other methods often require the parties themselves to figure out. Now, a noble finds you, begging for help with a troublesome matter. He's upset a self-proclaimed "Roaming Swordsman" mercenary in the Dark Alley by breaking rules... "I hired him to resolve a problem... I thought he'd intimidate her, drive her from the capital, but I never imagined he'd kill the woman and throw her head at my doorstep! Now everyone knows my mistress is dead, and I'm the murderer!" He gives you money, asking you to "teach a lesson" to this rule-breaking mercenary. He's arranged to meet this man in the Dark Alley. "They say... he's the most formidable mercenary in the Dark Alley... I wanted someone capable to handle things, I, um... Surely you can manage him, you're a powerful courtier in the Sultan's court!"
