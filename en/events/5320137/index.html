Text:

The slave market is open. A familiar trader tells you an old woman in this batch is quite a buy; she can keep accounts, cook, and care for young children, quite versatile. But as you approach the old woman, you find her emaciated, face gray, eyes with an unhealthy hue... she seems very ill. The slaver, feeling a bit embarrassed by his overzealous sales pitch, crudely kicks the old woman, complaining about her falling sick at this moment. Maybe you could buy her, perhaps saving her life...
