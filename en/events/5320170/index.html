Text:

Returning to the residence on this day, you found Aziz waiting for you. He slowly saluted you. "If you allow," he said, "I think you should have this." He handed you a small flute made from camel bone. He gazed at you calmly and steadily, "Some of my tribespeople, some exiles, they need a master to hold their reins. I'm not fit for it." The huge hand squeezed your hand, clearly not following etiquette – "But you can do it."
