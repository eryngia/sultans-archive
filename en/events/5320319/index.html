Text:

"Like me? Baa? Like me?" The voice took on a hint of puzzlement—which meant you had struck a chord, and it was growing more interested in you! You steadied your breath and spoke as smoothly as you could: It had come here, meddled in Hassan's life, joined this game because it longed for excitement, for curious, for fun, didn't it? It loved poetry, loved probing the unknown, loved indulging its curiosity—was that wrong? In that case, you and It were alike: you wished to peer at It precisely because you could not hold back your own curiosity—that curiosity was like a beast devouring you from within; you had to feed it, had to sate it! "Ha-ha-ha-ha, baa—baa-ha! You truly were amusing! The Sultan's Game... how delightful, baa!" Thunderous laughter erupted, and then the clouds pressing upon you eased a little... "I like you; unlike those weaklings who worship—baa-baa—other gods.I despise them, and I despise their gods alike. Come then, come, baa-baa-baa, I will reward you! I will give you a single chance to choose, amusing lower being—just once—you could pick one poem from my bookshelf, read it, even alter it, baa—just once!" A blaze of gold flashed before your eyes.
