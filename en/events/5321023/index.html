Text:

That day, after finishing discussing affairs as usual with you, Faraj did not immediately stand up to leave. In the flickering candlelight, he traced the patterns on the woven carpet, finally raising his eyes to look at you, his expression faintly aggrieved: "Why do you not..." He hesitated, then plucked up his courage and said, "Why don't you use me?" "What?" "Carnality!" he boldly raised his voice, looking straight at you, "You have that need, don't you? I am willing to ease your worries, but... why don't you use me?"

Text:

"I cannot betray Maggie or break the promise I made before our marriage." Faraj pauses, pain flashing in his eyes, but it quickly fades. He nods, then, as if nothing has happened, nods again: "I understand." He even forces a bitter smile. "What I admire is precisely who you are now." Before leaving, he takes off an amber gemstone pendant he always wears and places it beside you. "I will always look up to this sun, even though it shines equally on everyone." With these words, he gives you a slight bow and leaves. After this, neither of you brings up the matter again.

Text:

You are utterly speechless for a moment before regaining your sanity, then awkwardly explains to him that you consider him a good companion, a brother, even family. You never think of having that kind of relationship with him! "You must think the same, right? You must have misunderstood your feelings for me," you confidently say, wanting to pat his shoulder and make a joke as usual, but somehow feels you can't do that anymore. Faraj's expression turns confused, bewildered, as if wanting to explain or argue something but seems accustomed to following your arrangements, finding it difficult to refute. In the end, he gives up thinking about it, reluctantly saying goodbye. You sincerely hope he will understand... after all, you really consider him a good brother...
