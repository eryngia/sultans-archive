You approach the man being neglected and quietly remind him that Nabhani is the Iron Guard of The Sultan, a renowned swordsman of the empire. Is it somewhat unwise to challenge someone like him? Halfway through your words, the man's legs already begins to tremble... You tell him that countless people have surrendered to swordsmen of this caliber before him, and it is no disgrace; on the contrary, people would praise him for daring to challenge. Moreover, what is less dignified than gambling with one's life? Why sacrifice everything just for a moment of rage? The man drops his sword, bows deeply to both you and Nabhani, and flees. People heave a sigh of relief, and the Ladies of Delights tacitly agree never to mention this abandoned duel again. As you left, Nabhani smiles and waves at you. “Well done, you're always able to persuade others, whether it's an unpopular coward or The Sultan.”
