Lumera kneels before you, pushing a heavy bag of Gold Coins towards you. "You knew about the book all along..." her eyes flicker. "At first, I just wanted to write a diary, but the pages grew... and then a copyist approached me. Even the maids who heard the story said I should publish it, so... These are my royalties. I'm just your Slave Girl; what's mine is yours. Please, you must accept it." She lowers her head deeply, seemingly embarrassed, before continuing, "Also, I'm about to write the ending. The scoundrel will die... In the story, who should be the one to kill him?"
