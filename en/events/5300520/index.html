While the noble was unaware, you stepped forward and grabbed the antlers, shaking them vigorously. The seams of the leopard body split, and the monstrosity collapsed into several pieces. The noble stared at the floor, pale and speechless. The Sultan lazily waved a finger, and the guards dragged the poor soul, who had hoped to win the monarch's favor, out. Courtiers whispered, incredulous – even the scammer himself – at your audacity. Your daring act became a favorite tale among the common folk for quite some time.
