Text:

When you break the Sultan Card, the court fell into a rare silence, even your political enemies didn't jump out to say anything. It was a silence most odd. In your imagination, he should have come out criticizing you, saying you didn't flatten an oasis, a town, a country to meet the conquest card's standard... Yet you couldn’t do that! You don't have private troops! Lost in thought, Nawfal suddenly steps forward. The nobles around him lower their heads, hiding the "he's at it again" reflections in their eyes. Nawfal kneels before the Sultan once more, spine rigid, voice clear and firm: "You must not let this absurd game continue, Your Majesty, please rescind the power you granted [player.name], otherwise, I have reasons to suspect he will seize the opportunity to eliminate his foes!" He glances sideways at you, supplementing, "... and you would hear no dissenting voices." The Sultan seems intrigued by his words, smiling at you as if really considering giving you a warning...

Text:

Could this be a good opportunity to escape this game as well? Thinking this, you step forward and kneel before the Sultan. You admit that Nawfal is right, the power brought by this deck and the heavy burden it brings are unbearable: "Before I make bigger, more ridiculous mistakes, I implore you to end this game." "How could that be, dear minister?" The Sultan laughs heartily. Isn't this exactly what he hoped for? At the moment you jump out to accuse and advise him, he hopes to witness your fall, to see you become like him, become one of his own at the end of the game. Nawfal wants to say more, but the Sultan waves him off. As you leave the court, Nawfal stops you and warns, "Don't think the matter is over." His gaze is sharp, but the dark circles beneath his eyes are evident against his pale skin. It seems he hasn't been sleeping well lately. You shrug dismissively, without interacting with him, you leave of your own accord.

5320194_prompt_02

Text:

You immediately knelt on the ground, accusing Nawfal of having a wolf's ambition. Your king was only looking for a puppet show to enjoy, yet Nawfal dared to overstep? Who did he think he was? Your flattery performance enraged Nawfal; with anger and disdain, he used his linguistic prowess to mock you as a mere clown, criticizing all your actions and words from the day you were born, while you adeptly countered his every move, quietly wondering why his smart head was devoted to remembering these old feuds... This was the most common scene in the Sultan's court. The nobles were always heatedly fighting for Gold Coins, power, and status, while the Sultan laughed heartily, indulging and even encouraging you to fight each other. Leaving the court, Nawfal stopped you, as such warning, "Don't think this is the end of the matter." His gaze was sharp, but there was a smidgeon of dark blue in his eyes, glaringly evident against his pale skin. It seemed he hadn't been sleeping well lately. You shrugged habitually, not engaging in conversation, and left directly.
