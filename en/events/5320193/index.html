Text:

Almost as soon as you break the Sultan Card, a voice echoes in the lapis palace. "If not for today, I would not have known you have such wealth. Why didn't you help those suffering from disasters with some money in the past?" Your political enemy – Nawfal is shaking his head, casting a sharp look around at all the courtiers and passionately criticizing the disaster in some borderland of the empire, urging everyone to open their wallets... Yet, the nobles lower their heads, avoiding throwing money away on something not their territory. Finally, Nawfal kneels before the Sultan once more, spine rigid and voice clear and firm: "You must not let this absurd game continue, Your Majesty, please rescind the power you granted to [player.name], otherwise, I have reasons to suspect he will hide under the guise of cards to gain undeserved benefits and prestige!" He glances at you sideways, adding, "... ones rightfully yours." The Sultan seems intrigued by his words, smiling at you as if really considering asking you to present more gold...

Text:

Could this be an opportunity to escape the Sultan's Game? Thinking this, you stepped forward and knelt before the Sultan. You admitted that Nawfal was right, that the power and the heavy burden of the Sultan's Game was too much for you to bear. "Before I commit greater, more absurd mistakes, I beg you to end this game. In doing so, I am willing to bear the current blame." "How can this be, my dear subject," the Sultan laughed heartily. Isn't this what he expected? At the moment you jumped out to criticize him and advise him, he had hoped to see your downfall, to see you become like him at the end of the game, his kindred spirit. Nawfal wanted to say more, but the Sultan waved him away. Leaving the court, Nawfal stopped you, warning, "Don't think this is the end of the matter." His gaze was sharp, but there was a smidgeon of dark circles, glaringly evident against his pale skin. It seemed he hadn't been sleeping well lately. You shrugged habitually, not engaging in conversation, and left directly.

Text:

You immediately knelt on the ground, accusing Nawfal of having a wolf's ambition. Your king was only looking for a puppet show to enjoy, yet Nawfal dared to overstep? Who did he think he was? Your flattery performance enraged Nawfal; with anger and disdain, he used his linguistic prowess to mock you as a mere clown, criticizing all your actions and words from the day you were born, while you adeptly countered his every move, quietly wondering why his smart head was devoted to remembering these old feuds... This was the most common scene in the Sultan's court. The nobles were always heatedly fighting for Gold Coins, power, and status, while the Sultan laughed heartily, indulging and even encouraging you to fight each other. Leaving the court, Nawfal stopped you, as such warning, "Don't think this is the end of the matter." His gaze was sharp, but there was a smidgeon of dark blue in his eyes, glaringly evident against his pale skin. It seemed he hadn't been sleeping well lately. You shrugged habitually, not engaging in conversation, and left directly.
