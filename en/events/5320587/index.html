Text:

Raed came to you. The moment she entered, she slammed a massive spiked mace down before you and demanded to know whether you were connected to that damned kidnapper. You waved your hands and explained at length, until it became clear that two vagrants under her protection had vanished. "I'm not petty enough to keep a tally, but anyone can see there aren't as many beggars on the streets as before." She frowned and glared hard at you, as if to see straight through you. "I tracked the carriages the kidnapper uses... he thought covering the coach tight would keep anyone from noticing! But... hmph!" Raed did not share many of her tricks, at least not until she had confirmed you were truly trustworthy. "Your turn to explain, Lord [player.name]—why did you meet the owner of those carriages in the Dark Alley?" You had known that buying those diamonds would bring trouble, but you hadn't expected the first trouble to come from the Dark Alley. Patiently, you explained everything you and Jenna knew and even showed her the diamond. Raed gave it a single glance and tossed that rare, priceless gem back to you like a pebble. "Fine, I doubt you'd spend that much blood-money just to trick me." She nodded, and only then did she share the kidnapper's favorite tricks for shaking pursuit. Those tidings would surely prove useful to your investigation.
