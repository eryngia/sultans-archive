Text:

Asal came with a roll of manuscript. Bowing deeply at once, he presented the volume, then confessed he had stolen a book from Nawfal's vault. "It was the only book in his vault that I had not read... I simply could not accept watching it slip past me, and in a moment of... alas, I am deeply ashamed—I stole it. Now I have finished reading it and copied it out." You reached for the book, but Asal did not let go. The two of you hung there in an odd stalemate—wait. You suddenly grasped his meaning: he expected you to help him return the book...? "Ah—so you did catch my meaning! That was exactly it! You would help, would you not? Otherwise, wouldn't you become my accomplice? After all, we entered Lord Nawfal's vault together..." ... You had an overwhelming urge to shake his head and see what it contained. "All you need do is delay Lord Nawfal and his personal servants for a moment. I have studied the path to pry open the vault and put the book back... I remembered exactly where it sat, and I guaranteed no one would find out!" And to bribe you, as it were, he laid the cursed manuscript in your hands: "This one is for you."
