Text:

Mahir stirs awake. The Dragon's Eye has not killed her—she is simply drained by the searing heat of the furnace. Outside the window, a storm rages, and the earth trembles faintly. You cannot tell what the sound is: the wail of the wind, the rumble that foretells an earthquake, or the cries of wronged spirits at midnight? Perhaps it is tied to the work you have done this day—a great creation, forged by mortal hands! In the flash of lightning, you glimpse colossal, bizarre shapes on the horizon among the clouds. Gods? Demons? They peer your way furtively, stunned, curious, as if craving... or awaiting something. "How do you plan to use the Dragon's Eye?" Mahir asks, gazing out with you. "It is essentially a nexus of infinite possibility. Anything can be poured into it—energy, magic, even wishes... What do you think it should be used for?"
