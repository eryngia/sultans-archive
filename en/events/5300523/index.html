Someone presented a popular booklet from the market, which told a legendary love story between a king and a fallen princess. You reacted with great anger. The book depicts the king as a weak fool, ignoring his national duty and involved with a past enemy, actually suggesting that a dignified ruler would need to grovel to a washerwoman for love! This is our enemies trying to tarnish the almighty Sultan's image in the eyes of the people, and they must be severely punished! The Sultan, after hearing your words, seemed thoughtful. He gestured and gave some instructions to the guards. Probably some people will lose their lives because of this. You wiped off your sweat. At least, the Sultan was very pleased with you.

Text:

After flipping through a few pages, you casually tossed the book aside, saying as lightly as possible that this is just a woman’s trifle, your wife occasionally reads such things as well. You did everything you could to convince the Sultan that women are short-sighted, brainless fools, and the things they focus on are not important. Of course, deep down, you knew the exact opposite is true—a woman drawing water at the well might know more than the Sultan on the golden throne. But fortunately, the Sultan accepted your words, mumbled his agreement, and never brought up this topic again. You, the author of the book, the printer of the book, the seller of the book... many, many people kept their heads.
