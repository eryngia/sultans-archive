Text:

Your servants came laughing to announce that Master Hassan had come to drink with you. Hassan was welcome in every noble house: the servants liked him because he put on no airs and slipped them tidbits from the banquet; the nobles liked him because any feast shone more elegantly with him present. Bleating and laughter drifted up the hall; you stood in the doorway to welcome the poet. He embraced you warmly and again thanked you for helping him at the last banquet. "I would have built a wall of ten thousand poems to keep all those conflicts out... but ten thousand seemed still not enough!" he said with a laugh, settling easily on the carpet in your receiving room and grabbing a handful of the pastries your servants had set out. "Your sweets were excellent... Believe me, I seldom said so. Trust old Hassan—I knew whose wine is best and whose pastries smell the finest!" You chatted lightly for a while; then, idly teasing his little sheep, you asked how old it was. In your memory it had always been about this size... "Ah, you mean Lamblet? Yes, that's its name—how old? Hm... let me think, I came by it when..." Hassan's eyes went a touch glassy... You felt something was off—something seemed to be spilling out of the sheep and boring straight into your and Hassan's minds...
