Text:

So you make a suggestion: let him keep the capacity for love, but punish him a little for his overproduction of love verse. For instance, send him to someone who understands love the least, and let that person grind him down for a while... The rolling clouds pause; then a huge ovine eye opens within them—like a malformed sun—staring straight at you. "You're very good at pleasing those above you, aren't you, baa? You always have a way... ha, ha, ha, baa! Tell me, how will you do this!" You ponder: who could be the one who understands love the least?
