Text:

You plan to open a guesthouse in the Dark Alley, a haven for those who can help you despite their lowly status. Yet you hesitate – who should manage it? This person must come from common stock, skilled at handling peddlers and the various classes of society; they need to cook well, as a guesthouse's greatest attraction is free meals; this person must be sufficiently loyal to you, otherwise it would be quite dangerous; and they must be clever and adaptable – who knows what might happen in the Dark Alley? "What about that cook from your territory?" After hearing your concerns, Maggie thinks for a moment before suddenly suggesting, "I believe his name is Habib? When we first married and you took me to tour your territory, I remember we once had an excellent meal... Because I praised how delicious the food was, you specifically called him out to commend him, which is why I remember his name." You slap your forehead – Maggie's suggestion strikes true. So, you send word to summon Habib from your lands. He arrives tomorrow, and your guesthouse opens its doors.
