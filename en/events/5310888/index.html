As you ride the river of the faithful, irresistibly advancing through the world and savoring the joy of leading a vast army... something stirs in the distance. It's a thought, a gaze from an insect. Weak and insignificant, yet sharp enough to pierce your heart like a needle. It's a thread tied impossibly tight to you! Why has it escaped your awareness until now? You send your infinite power toward it—and meet only refusal, denial, and disappointment. In fury, you rip the offending eyes out of the insect's head! You embed them like jewels into the heaven that belongs to you—and to this new era. Behold the age I shall create, pitiful non-believer! And yet... with these eyes, the world seems clearer to you, and so does the form of [player.name].
