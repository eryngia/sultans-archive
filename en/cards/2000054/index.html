Title:

Adventure Enthusiast

Text:

One of the four esteemed individuals granted the honor of serving as the Sultan's personal guard, permitted to bear arms in the Sultan's presence. An affable man oft ridiculed for his passion for adventure stories and ancient heroics, he steadfastly upholds the ideals of the very paragons he admires.
