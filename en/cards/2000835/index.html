Shield of Abbara

ID:

Title:

Accessory

Text:

The surface of this round shield is etched with a masterful depiction of the Sultan’s triumph at the Gorge of Abbara, where the armies of the previous Sultan was crushed. Of course, there are seditious whispers – that the Sultan did not conquer the capital through war, but traitorous treachery... The official tale remains official: a hard-fought victory, a triumphant return, and the severed head of a fallen monarch raised high as the faithful roared their the name of their Sultan.
