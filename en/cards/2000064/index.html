Text:

One of the four esteemed individuals granted the honor of serving as the Sultan's personal guard, permitted to bear arms in the Sultan's presence. The best swordsman in the capital, yet too handsome to guard the harem and too clever to serve the army. Instead, he lingers in the House of Delights, befriending prostitutes and artists.
