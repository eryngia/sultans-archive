Text:

In the Sultan's Game, there are four types of cards, corresponding to four types of tasks that must be completed. Each task is divided into four tiers, corresponding to the difficulty of completion. When you receive a Sultan Card, whether by choice or not, you must complete the depicted task within [sudan_life_time] days. Otherwise... well, I shall wish you a swift and painless decapitation.

Text:

Verily, in this world, everything has its tier, be it people, things, or Sultan Cards. Gold, Silver, Bronze, and the lowly Stone... These cards will accept sacrifices of their own tier or higher, but not vice versa. For instance, you may slay a Silver-tiered slave to resolve a Bronze Sultan Card. In some cases, a card's tier may change... that is something for you to discover yourself.

Text:

Oh, Carnality! Who doesn't love the carnal pleasures, except for those cowardly prudes worrying about "consequences"? You can make love with anyone to fulfill this card's demand: men, women, men and women... or be even more creative. But do take note. Typically, the same person can only be used to resolve the Carnality Cards once. The Sultan didn't put you through this game to encourage fidelity, ha!

Only the weak rule with order. Our Sultan, whose strength is without equal, rules with fear. Bloodshed is one of his favorite pastimes. This card calls for a blood sacrifice. Cast your gaze towards loves ones, innocents, enemies – the only thing that matters is whether they are of the right tiers.

Compared to other Sultan Cards, Conquest is perhaps less entertaining. But it is part of what being a Sultan entails. Just think about it – how vast his land, how exalted his name! Military victories and sufficiently challenging adventures are both forms of conquest recognized by the cards.

Extravagance means more than just spend money. It is to misspend, to waste, to squander what others hold dear. The higher the tier of the card, the more lavish the spending must be. Fortunately, in His Majesty's realm, there are plenty of ways to tastefully throw away one's wealth.

Rest assured, to make the game more enjoyable, the merciful Sultan has absolved you of all crimes you must commit to complete this game, be it adultery, larceny, or murder. Naturally, the victims will still curse and haunt you, and those who vie for the Sultan's favor will ostracize you in envy. But this is exactly the kind of drama the Sultan loves, wouldn't you say?

To survive, you may want to delay resolving a Sultan Card for as long as possible, since as soon as one is resolved, you must draw the next. But beware, though it may seem favorable to delay receiving the next task, opportunities are oft fleeting. Acumen and judgment will go a long way.

Joining the Sultan's Game does not affect your regular duties, such as Managing the Estate. As a noble, you need only pay the slightest attention to domestic matters to reap rich rewards. And forget not your status as a courtier. Don't make yourself a stranger at court. Make yourself seen, or at least make sure the Sultan's Palace is not filled with only enemies.
