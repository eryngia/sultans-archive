Ah, you’ve noticed it! The Sultan’s Game has granted you the power to see everything in its proper tier – Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Stone. Even the Sultan Cards bow to this hierarchy, nestled neatly within these four ranks. Now, resolving a Sultan Card typically demands a character or item of matching caliber. Bronze for Bronze, Silver for Silver. But given your... unique predicament, I had a quiet word with the Sultan. A subtle nudge to ensure that, should you wield a higher-tier piece, it can serve to settle a lesser card. For instance, you might sacrifice a Silver target to satisfy a Bronze Bloodshed Card.
