Silent Woods

ID:

272

Subtitle:

You failed to create more.

Text:

Your world continues to expand, reaching out into the chilling winds. You nurture forests, sculpting them into mysterious forms... Mist swirls, shadows weaving through. The world's hidden truths are etched onto fallen leaves, yet they remain unseen by any. Time and time again, you shape everything in your possession, yet the creation of a soul eludes you. As the cold intensifies, the wind fades away, until silence lulls you into oblivion, and the cycle begins anew... Deep within, you cling to the hope that one day, someone might dream of you, infusing their soul into the forest—and with it, heralding an apocalypse.
