Happy Ending

ID:

302

Subtitle:

Tempered by dragonfire, guarded by stars

Text:

The wicked game was laid to rest forever. The starlight gradually dimmed. The flames failed to consume your soul, yet still, there was the smell of ash. Your memories were the first to go. Your companions became strange, nameless silhouettes. The ground beneath you shifted, alien. At last, you stood upon a land that extended out into infinity, where you were to wander – without a past, without a destination. None you met seemed to know you. Until one day, as you passed by a city, you saw a woman standing upon a barbican, her gaze cast stubbornly into the distance. She saw you. As your eyes met, you saw the starlight of tears. It was Maggie. Her name lit up your dulled heart. You started to walk towards her. She ran over. As you opened your arms, she fell into your embrace. The dragon’s breath had erased you, yes. Yet the threads of fate had pulled you back to her. Something unnameable stirred and rose from the depths of your soul, and in a moment of profound clarity, you understood: this was happiness.
