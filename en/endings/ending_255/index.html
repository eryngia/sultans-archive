The Realm of Conquest

ID:

255

Subtitle:

A warrior’s fate is to repeat his deeds of valor until death claims him.

Text:

The Sultan fell before you. You raised your sword high, and the crowds around you erupted into wild cheers—an eviscerate hero, a new ruler who defeated the Sultan! The empire never experienced a more glorious moment—mere mortals reaching an unparalleled realm! Soon, the colossal dragon head was installed above your throne—the empire's most talented goldsmiths sculpted gold reliefs of the dragon slaying deeds, affixed beside the throne—it eyed the courtiers, some timid ministers collapsed out of fear at their first sight of this skull! However, the satisfaction from defeating the dragon soon began to wane. You grew restless... every time the court nobles bickered and chittered, and every time Nawfal brought budget and report complaints to you, your discomfort only grew. At last, you decided to march your army west, seek out a colossal sea monster lurking in a trench—you tallied all worthy hunts, this one closest to the nation! All your soldiers rejoiced at your decision, while nobles, officials, and ministers stood aghast. Nawfal tried all they could to deter you, "How can a Sultan leave the nation? You still consider yourself just a daredevil seeking glory?!"—But you couldn't wait any longer; a hero who gained the throne through such acts should create ever more heroic deeds! First was the sea monster, next came the golden-winged serpent, then the Banshee's forest, your troops ravaged through the world's frightful domains; should a local king, lord, or chief impede your passage, you simply annihilated their nation too. Countless challenges, countless conquests, endless bloodshed! Such a life befits you! You must always run forward, forever fleeing that distant call behind: "Return—you are the Sultan—you have responsibilities—!"
