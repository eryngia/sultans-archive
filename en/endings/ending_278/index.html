Eternal Peace

ID:

Subtitle:

May the mundane world never disturb your eternal peace.

Text:

You still remember who you are, and what you are. Through the guidance of oracles and revelations, you have devoted immense time and effort to aid your companions in upholding this tumultuous realm—it even saw periods of improvement over certain decades! Yet, you are aware that this status quo cannot last much longer, as a human empire cannot, and should not, rely solely on divine power for its existence... And so, with the support of other great beings who participated in creation, you readied yourself for the long-awaited repose... Thus, the legend unfolds: at the edge of the ancient empire lies a hidden tomb where the guardian of that realm, [player.name], the Lord of Light, lies dormant. Only when darkness once again shrouds the land can the descendants of heroes awaken Them...
