The Myth of Dreams

ID:

279

Subtitle:

You remain an elusive figure in this reshaped world.

Text:

You have melded into the darkness, residing within dreams... However, like wine spilling from an overfilled goblet, or blood trickling from a severed throat, your influence seeps into reality, drop by drop. Is this a result of your own desires, or is it humanity's avarice at play? Not even [player.name] can unveil the answer. As more mortals draw power from the dream realm, demons, gods, and even stars enter the fray. You are compelled to assign them ranks, grant them dominions and duties... and ultimately, face their challenges. Whispers of your downfall circulate in the chronicles of the dream realm. Some recount a mighty king ascending to divinity and vanquishing the Lord of Darkness, while others proclaim a formidable priestess wielding love as her blade in this conquest... You fracture into shards, each transforming into a formidable demon deity or an immortal monster, yet none of them carry the name [player.name].
