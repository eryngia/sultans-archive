Escape to a Distant Oasis

ID:

101

Subtitle:

Above you, the sky stretches impossibly high; beneath you, the grass grows impossibly green. You live with impossible freedom, and all of it is your rightful due.

Text:

Following the secret map, you crossed the desert, traversed mounds that moved and labyrinths of razor sharp stones, and hid from deadly sandstorms by crawling across an ancient riverbed... At last, you reached this paradise yet unknown to and untouched by tyranny. Such beauty and tranquility, it felt less like a real place, but a dream come true... You plunged into the lake at its heart and tasted sweetness: the purity of molten snow from the highest mountain peaks. You also tasted bitterness: the saltiness of tears streaming down your cheeks...
