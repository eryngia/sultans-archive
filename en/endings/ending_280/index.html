Text:

You, alongside your companions who still hold memories of you, have maintained a delicate balance between day and night, between reality and dreams. Humanity continues to yearn for revelations drawn from dreams, while wizards persist in their quests for power through voyages within dreams... Nonetheless, with your intervention, these practices have gradually faded into the veils of myth and cryptic lore. In the current era, the world no longer trembles at the thought of being reshaped at the Creator's whims. Their dominion is confined solely within Their own domain, within the realm of dreams. Whispers suggest that [player.name], the Lord of Dreams, occasionally graces mortal dreams—be it to reminisce with former companions or to safeguard the world's forbidden truths.
