The Realm of Heroes

ID:

292

Subtitle:

Not only does history remember your name, but the myths that follow shall also bear your tale.

Text:

The Sultan has fallen before you. You raise your sword high above as the masses around you erupt in frenzied cheers—a Dragonslayer hero, a valiant blade that vanquished the Sultan! This is a moment of unparalleled glory that the empire has never witnessed; mere mortals reaching such heights! Soon, the massive dragon's head decorates your throne—a masterpiece of gold engravings crafted by the empire's finest goldsmith captures the epic tale of dragonslaying, set beside your throne—watching over the assembly; those faint of heart among the courtiers stumble in awe at their first sight of this head! And you, the greatest hero among men, sit upon the throne. You wield the longsword tempered by dragon breath and cloak of dragon scales, with your Vizier, [Vizier's Name], by your side. Your claim was so unquestionable, your preparations for the throne so thorough, that the transition of power was as smooth as a hot knife through butter. You swiftly and successfully transform the nation, weaving ancient traditions into a new order and granting people more freedom; bringing smiles, prosperity to markets, and reducing hunger. To this day, your statues dot the continent and people pray for the courage to overcome adversity; they kneel faithfully and rise anew, eyes filled with strength.
