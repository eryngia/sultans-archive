The Palace of Dreams

ID:

283

Subtitle:

The gardener of dreams, the tyrant of souls, the wanderer in the garden... The lonely you.

Text:

Shortly after dethroning the Sultan, you find Sadani in his harem. You propose to her that you can pave the way for her child to ascend as the next Sultan, on the condition that she appoints you as the Regent to oversee the kingdom. Her eyes sparkle momentarily, then dim abruptly... "But they will question my child's lineage. The Sultan... the late Sultan... the timing of his visit to my chambers and my conception... it simply doesn't add up. I've considered this before, but the nobles just won't lend their support... If you thrust me onto the throne now, all three of us—you, me, and my child—will face certain death." While her concerns are well-founded, she fails to understand who you truly are... or rather, "what" you truly are. You unfurl your hand, revealing the tiny clay key to her. It embodies the pinnacle of spiritual achievement, the authority of the enlightened. You could have created an entirely new world with this key, yet you missed the portal it was destined to unlock—or perhaps you consciously veered away from that path. Now, with this immense power in hand, ruling over a mere mortal kingdom is but child's play. Following this revelation, you outline the blueprint of your future kingdom to Sadani, detailing how you will build the Halcyon Days as vast as the palace itself. You will relentlessly extend its boundaries... witnessing its growth, with blooms sprouting one after the other across its lawns. Each flower embodies a living dream, an unguarded entity within the dream realm. You reign as the king of this realm of dreams, while all others are but flowers in your garden at best. As you stroll through the garden, plucking at whim, you crush the dreams of others. Disobedient courtiers awaken with their eyes wide, startled from their dreams—they all dream of your colossal figure towering above, gazing down upon them, casually trampling them underfoot. The shattering of their dreams results in maddening agony... Then, they come to realize that the battleground extends far beyond the confines of the court. They might see political maneuvering as the primary arena in this game of thrones, yet driving a dagger into their core is also a facet of this game, not to mention the torments within their dreams. Amidst these struggles, all they have achieved is the most insignificant victories, while you... you reign as the master. You hold sway over both bodies and spirits. Initially doubtful of your words, Sadani witnesses the most venerable and hostile noblewomen kneel before her, wailing and pleading. They vow to testify that the child within Sadani's womb indeed carries the late Sultan's lineage. They implore you to cease your torments within their dreams, beseeching that they teeter on the edge of madness. Despite her uneasiness, Sadani is crowned as the ruler as you foresaw. And you, the Regent by her side, emerge as the true ruler of this kingdom. Henceforth, the days remain much as they did under the previous Sultan. The populace continues their familiar routines, and all accolades and admiration merely require a shift in recipient. As night falls, you wander in your garden, scanning for any burgeoning weeds, encroaching vines, and thorny plants, uprooting every dream unfit for your empire...
