A Momentary Careless

ID:

11

Subtitle:

You were assassinated

Text:

A dagger silently slips into your unprotected chest. Pain shatters your mind. Scorching blood floods the icy blade. You look into the cold eyes of the masked assassin. Did the Sultan send him? Just because through your guile, you managed to avoid death by an inch? No... no. Those eyes are full of hatred, a hatred that will not die even if you died. Who is he...? You do not know, and you can no longer think.
