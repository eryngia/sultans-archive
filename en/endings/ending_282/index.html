A Myriad of Reunions

Subtitle:

In other lifetimes, we shall always find each other...

Text:

On the eve of your new era, the call from your companions, who place their trust and love in you, reaches your ears. Just as you have done countless times across the cycles of the game, the unwavering [player.name] stands resolute against the temptation of power, even the allure of divine power and eternal life... You have woven your divine essence into the fabric of the future, dispersing it across infinite epochs—not for command or domination, but simply to bestow a parting gift, that [player.name], alongside his companions and beloved ones, would eventually find each other again in any era, in any form they may be reborn into... And as a special reward for yourself, you will retain the memory of each tale from your myriads of reunions...
