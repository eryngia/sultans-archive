To Set Things Right

ID:

286

Subtitle:

You have set things right in the history of reality, even if it is just a single page.

Text:

To Set Things Right: Unveiling a New Era in [player.name] Research Today, Nursery National University hosted a prestigious academic symposium led by the university's History Department, drawing experts and scholars from across the globe. During the symposium's commencement, Professor Juanita, the distinguished President of the History Department at Nursery National University and an esteemed historian and [player.name] researcher, delivered a lecture titled "To Set Things Right." Here are excerpts from her speech. "Our gathering today not only commemorates the millennium of [player.name]'s birth but also revolves around a crucial text from an extraordinary archaeological revelation that has captivated the world: [player.name]'s Manuscript. Given our collective expertise in this domain, it is presumed that we are well-versed in the manuscript's contents, necessitating only a brief overview. This manuscript, spanning over three hundred pages, represents [player.name]'s first personally authored, documentary-style text definitively attributed to him. Regrettably, due to preservation challenges and other factors, a mere thirty pages remain legible today. Among these, a particularly touching passage resonates. 'Today, I returned home, embraced Maggie, and pressed my cheek against hers... Then I lifted Lady Becky from his soft cushion nearest the brazier, taking my place as the master of this household. The wine glasses were already filled to the brim, and the servants presented the hookah... After wetting my throat with wine and stretching my back, I drew near to Maggie, telling her how her husband, with his unmatched cunning, had managed to rid us of a troublesome magician... She chuckled in my arms. She must have fancied me, loved me, been willing to share her life with me, but at that moment, she probably didn't realize how important she was to me or the fact that I had chosen her to set things right.' This passage marks the manuscript's conclusion. Subsequent to this, the influential courtier, husband, nobleman, and lord appeared to cease documenting his life. Who was this "magician" mentioned in the text? Why did he abruptly stop writing? And most importantly, what does 'to set things right' mean? Despite decades of study on [player.name] with numerous works dedicated to him, he now presents us with a profound mystery after a millennium! Therefore, we have convened this conference, with the hope that over this four-day symposium, each of us can pool our collective wisdom, engage in dynamic discussions, and endeavor to decipher the significance of the final page of [player.name]'s manuscript. This discovery could upend all our prior conclusions about this influential figure. However, as a scholar and historian, the notion that our entire body of work may have been founded on inaccuracies both unsettles and excites me, as it opens up a vast new realm for exploration. That concludes my speech. I now pass the microphone to our first speaker. Thank you." Reporter Argus King will continue reporting on-site discussions from this symposium. Notably, this symposium has been sponsored by Double Cross Technologies. Reported by Argus Tus.
