999

Text:

You make your speech until your mouth feels dry, but it clearly has no impact on the Sultan. Even the Magician pays you no mind. As you come to a stop, exhausted, wondering if your life is about to end, the Sultan suddenly casts you a curious look. "Verily so," he muses, "A wise monarch should not commit such acts... But neither should a wise monarch abandon his own delights. In which case – why don't you play in my stead? You, my loyal subject, I command you to play this game for me, this Sultan's Game!" He bursts into laughter as you stand there, dumbfounded...
