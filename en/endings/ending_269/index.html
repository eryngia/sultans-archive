Lively Little Home

ID:

269

Subtitle:

Why isn't my home a world in itself?

Text:

You said, let there be light, and Maggie lit a lamp. It was far too cold outside... so together you put up walls and a roof. Little by little, your room gained a fireplace, tables and chairs, carpets, cups and plates, beds... Where [player.name] labored became the so-called "Yang," and the realm Maggie tended formed the "Yin." With light and warmth, boundaries, water and dry ground, and the cycle and union of Yin and Yang, the world began to function, slowly expanding with each passing day you lived...
