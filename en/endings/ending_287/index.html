Manifestations of Gods

ID:

287

Subtitle:

You have all the time in the cosmos to recall Their names.

Text:

The first step in creating a world is to forge the essential tools. You need to bring forth beings to collaborate in the world's creation, following your grand design. While the original Creator favored embodiments of concepts and laws, you, a solitary soul, yearn for companionship... Drifting serenely amidst a swirling storm of particles, you extend your hands. Instinctively, you begin to shape the forms of souls from your memory, crafting their likenesses from the particles. Then, with a gentle breath, you bestow a fraction of the Creator's power upon them... And so, They spring to life. The "gods" of this realm awaken in your hands, gazing up at you. After a fleeting moment of bewilderment, They join you in the act of creation.
