Text:

You granted Sadani noble standing, permitting her continued presence at court - partly to appease the old guard (for she was once a consort!), and partly because Sadani was iron-willed, her cutting sneers and withering disdain proving useful tools. Upon her appointment, she vanished for a time, long enough to birth a child, some whispered - before striding back into court with renewed arrogance. Indeed, she became an invaluable ally, reading your will in the slightest glance and delivering the necessary verdicts in matters too delicate for your direct hand. Years passed. Then, without warning, Sadani adopted a child. The court buzzed with outrage - "She grows restless in her widowhood!" they hissed. "That is no foundling, but her own bastard!" Worse still, they muttered of the veiled guard she kept in her household, a shadowed figure whose presence reeked of impropriety... "Let those who take issue challenge him to a duel," Sadani remarked idly, examining her nails. "He has sworn to defend my honor unto death. And do recall - this matter was already brought before the Sultan." Ah. So the Sultan knew... well then. The matter was dropped.

Text:

Seliman returned to court, his face bare once more. As for Sadani - once a consort - vanished for precisely the length of time required to birth a child, then strode back into court with all the imperious bearing of a woman who had turned scandal into power. Bound by law from remarrying, she wore her disgrace like a coronet. Years later came the child - a boy with Seliman's sharp features and, some whispered, his temper. When tongues began wagging, Sadani - now styled as Master Sadani - bared her fangs: "Shall a woman be denied the comfort of a child's laughter? Must orphaned beauty now be suspect?" Her words cut deeper than any blade, her fury a weapon honed in palace intrigues. The murmurs died quickly enough when Seliman - that hulking relic of royal favor - offered "honorable satisfaction" to the most vocal critics. Strange how few cared to cross swords with a Iron Guard who had survived two sultans' reigns. The matter was dropped.

Text:

You bestowed a noble status on Sadani, allowing her to continue frequenting your court. This was partly to pacify the traditional elite — after all, she had been a consort! — and partly because Sadani was a formidable woman; her sneers and disdain were quite useful. After receiving her appointment, she disappeared for quite a length of time — as long as it takes to have a child — and returned to court with newfound arrogance. She was indeed a loyal supporter of yours, willing to articulate the conclusions you desired in politically thorny situations according to your cues. Years later, Sadani suddenly adopted a child, prompting some discontent. They accused the former consort of restlessness, claiming it was her own child; they also pointed to a constantly veiled guard she maintained at her residence, calling it improper. "Oh, anyone disgruntled can duel with that guard," Sadani said nonchalantly, "He claims he'd fight to the death to defend my honor. Remind you, I've already submitted this matter to the Sultan." Ah, so the Sultan knew...well then...this matter ended there.
