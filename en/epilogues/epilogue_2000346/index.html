Text:

Jawad had followed you to this foreign land, abandoning his fortune, only to find his guts twisted with regret. Yet the man had a gift. He learned the tongue quickly enough, and soon discovered that one art transcended borders: the art of bribery. Before long, he left you behind, weaving himself into the schemes of borderland warlords. Through smuggling and monopoly, he amassed a fortune that would make even the Sultan's treasurers blush...
