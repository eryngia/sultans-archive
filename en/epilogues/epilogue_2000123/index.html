Text:

As Lumera ventured beyond the empire's borders, her vision widened. She had read thousands of books, walked thousands of miles, and met innumerable souls. In time, her wisdom and magic grew beyond mortal bounds, and in the mouths of the locals, she became an immortal of legends. In the end, such fame has became a burden. They made her decision inevitable: to leave the mortal realm behind. It is said that when she ascended Kunlun, five immortals descended from the heavens to welcome you. But in the howling blizzard, all you saw was her smile, as she turned to look at you for the final time.

Text:

The supreme emperor once sought to make Lumera his bride. But after conversing with her at length, he granted her a holier purpose – to extend his life and forge the fabled elixir of immortality. The great altar and the sky-gold furnace drained the empire's coffers dry. But you knew the truth – this was no path to eternal life. At last, before the Emperor's very eyes, Lumera jumped into the furnace. She was remade, transfigured by sacred rites, shedding the dust of mortality and ascending at last to the fate she had long been denied: A star, burning high above, watching over the world below. This was no path meant for mortal men. As this dynasty drew to its end, three emperors perished after swallowing the ashes from the sky-gold furnace. The last, in a final act of desperation, threw himself into the flames, chasing the elusive possibility of apotheosis. The chronicles all put the blame you, the treacherous courtier of foreign blood. But by then, you were of course long gone.
