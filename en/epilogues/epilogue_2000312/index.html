Nawfal was not your new Vizier, and he was content with this arrangement. He served the realm while stepping back from the game of thrones... Yet he understood your precarious position all too well: how every step forward was met by twice as many hands dragging you back. So he chose to aid you differently, from the shadows. He expanded the Nursery you had created together, establishing new branches in every major city. No longer just for the poorest children, these Nurseries now welcomed commoners' sons, bankrupt nobles' offspring, even slaves' children. Nawfal summoned those who had survived the Sultan's games with you to teach there, pouring every ounce of his will into planting seeds of justice, fairness, and wisdom in those young minds. He placed his hopes in the next generation or the one after that. In a future neither of you would live to see. The nobles scoffed at Nawfal's class-dissolving ideas as perilous absurdity - slaves learning beside nobles? You shuffled, demurred, changed the subject each time the issue arose... It was all you could do. You two never wrote. Never spoke. Yet the trust between you remained unbroken.
