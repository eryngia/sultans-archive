Zazie showed utmost deference at your coronation. She blessed you, knelt before you, and begged leave to scrape out her days in the realm, swearing never to breathe a word of what passed between you. After the dagger found you, she strode into the palace with her son in her arms. The rebels proclaimed the boy the rightful heir of the late Sultan, and so Zazie became Regent. For all its legitimacy, this new reign lacked much else. The child took a chill at two and a half, then perished between dusk and dawn. Zazie wept, refusing all hands near the little Sultan's corpse. "I'll find another boy to crown!" she shrieked. Madness had taken her. So they put her to the sword.
