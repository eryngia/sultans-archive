Maggie was taken aback by your decree - though none could deny its logic. She was your wife, your most trusted confidant; and what a fledgling regime needed most was nobility both capable and loyal. She ordered a smaller chair of silver and ivory placed beside your golden throne, then faced the court veiled in azure gauze embroidered with silver. Her tone was gentle, her counsel sharp, her mind keen - yet when the nobles found no fault in her actions, they turned to slander. "She flaunts her station," they hissed. So for Maggie's sake - and your own - you bypassed every master and protocol minister, proclaiming her your heir. Should fate strike you down, the empire would be hers. Ah, how the nobles raged! Maggie, a woman, was lawfully named heir. And as the Sultan trapped in gilded chains, this act etched your name into eternity.
