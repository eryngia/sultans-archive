Text:

You granted Zaki a position of power in the new court: Master of Lords' Apparel. No mere ceremonial post, this was a dagger sheathed in velvet. While their lands suffered, no lord could strut about in silks and jewels unchallenged. Zaqiyi became both spymaster and arbiter, his piercing gaze weighing every embroidered hem and gilded buckle against the grim ledgers of their domains' fortunes. Each dawn, the lords would slink through the colonnades like whipped dogs, their eyes darting nervously toward Zaki's station. The wheat had withered in the eastern fields... but surely that wasn't their fault? The ruby-studded belt... perhaps too ostentatious? Only when the young master passed his silent judgment - a barely perceptible nod - could they shuffle forward to prostrate themselves before the throne. Zaki wore this authority with great honor. In your new realm, he had found his purpose and flourished.

在故国的传统中，所有的贵族都要有所特长，要么是诗歌，要么是骑射，又或是朗诵或是演奏。复辟之后，贵族们纷纷举办豪华的诗歌朗诵会、绘画展览、骑射比赛，争先恐后地炫耀着自己对这个新国家的支持，以及自己不俗的品位。 在这股风潮中，作为你最出色的学生，扎齐伊选择的是诗歌的艺术。他谢绝了所有的任命和邀请，一头扎进故国的诗集，又向你身边的宫廷诗人拜师，花了许多时间，终于作出了一首让他自己满意的诗： 《吾王之伟大》 吾王伟大， 无需多言。 有目者可见， 有耳者可闻； 有心者，如我， 可将心中之物腾空， 以安置， 吾王之伟大。 这是扎齐伊一生中所作的第一首诗，也是最后一首。在写完这诗之后，他说自己已经没什么其他可写的事了。 他终身都侍奉在你的身边，从书记官，到税官，再到将军，他胜任了你出于信任而交托给他的所有工作。
