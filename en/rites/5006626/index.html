Text:

Aziz washes his face at your courtyard's pond, reties his coarse cloth belt, then stands tall with princely confidence. “If you allow,” he says, “I am ready.” Aziz is seated, and it surprises everyone. But as you are the host and he stays beside you, no one dares question it. He calmly tastes each dish laid before him, slowly offering critiques now and then. Were it not for the coarse clothing and sunburned skin, he’d seem like a prince. Thus, people’s imagination paints this finically tasteful, nobly attired young slave in a rosy light. Rumors swiftly spread of [player.name] knowing a foreign prince. By the end of the feast, after all guests depart, he prostrates at your feet. “Please allow me to thank you; I feasted well, Master.”
