I Want the Head of Lord [player.name]

Text:

You looked up in shock, meeting her cold, hateful gaze—no more pretense, no more hiding. "[player.name], I hate you," she said, twirling the golden ring on her wrist—a habit you once found charming, but now... it sent chills down your spine. "Not only because your father deceived, betrayed, and hurt my mother. Not only because your family ignored us all these years. Not only because you used and humiliated me... but because after I entered the palace, when I needed you most—even when I was useful to you—you still stood by and did nothing. How does that saying go? More harmful than our enemies are the friends who refuse to help us." She smiled, confident of victory, and leaned against the Sultan's shoulder. But to her surprise, the Sultan refused her. "Absolutely not! He still has to play games with me!" With that, he plucked the golden crown from Zazie's head and placed it on Arzuna's... He looked at you with an amused smile—perfect, perfect. With Arzuna's murderous gaze, he had another tool to torment you.

"I want Lady Zazie's golden crown! I've loved it for so long! Please, I just have to have it! After all, everything she has is yours, and everything in the empire is yours. Just reassign what's yours and give it to me, please? Your Majesty! Your Majesty!" The Sultan laughed at her tease. Then he turned, reached over, and casually took the golden crown from Zazie's head, placing it on Arzuna. Zazie's hair fell loose... her face ashen. She slid off the throne and said not a word. Everything continued as before. Audiences proceeded; the Sultan ruled. The only difference was that the Golden Throne was no longer crowded.
