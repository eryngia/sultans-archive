After Results

Result 1

Title:

A Beacon of Light

Text:

When Jenna steps into the baths gathering, every lady freezes for a moment. Like the rest, she wears only a silken veil, clinging damp against her skin in the rising steam. But over the silk she gleams with jewelry—gold and silver, agate and sapphire, amethyst and emerald, and diamonds that chime as she moves. Ordinarily, no noble lady would flaunt such splendor in public. Yet here, in a space that blurs the line between intimacy and society, Jenna does not display her beauty, but uses herself as a living canvas, revealing her craft. "Oh... like a lighthouse, piercing even the mist with light!" Maggie comments, laughing as she rises from the pool. She takes Jenna's hand. "My dearest Jenna, tell me, is this necklace for sale? And this darling belt? Look at those earrings—magnificent!" The others crowd in eagerly. Jenna smiles, half kneels, and places her ruby earrings into Maggie's palm. "Your husband is my benefactor, and you are his dearest treasure. These jewels belong with you." Maggie beams—not for the jewel, but for the gift itself.

Title:

A Beacon of Beauty

Text:

When Jenna steps into the baths gathering, every lady freezes for a moment. Like the rest, she wears only a silken veil, clinging damp against her skin in the rising steam. But over the silk she gleams with jewelry—gold and silver, agate and sapphire, amethyst and emerald, and diamonds that chime as she moves. Ordinarily, no noble lady would flaunt such splendor in public. Yet here, in a space that blurs the line between intimacy and society, Jenna does not display her beauty, but uses herself as a living canvas, revealing her craft. "Ha! Look at you—dressed up ten times more flamboyantly than me. You'll steal all my spotlight!" [s1.name] teases as she emerges first from the bath, studying the glittering ornaments. "Your earrings are divine. The mist cannot dull their shine." The others crowd in eagerly. Jenna smiles, half kneels, and gently clasps her ruby earrings onto Nayla's ears. Admiring herself in the water's surface, Nayla nods in satisfaction amidst a chorus of compliments. "They are perfect. I'll have the payment sent to your estate, and I'll commission an entire new season's work from you! By the heavens, a woman without new jewels is as bare as a woman without clothes!" The noble ladies murmur agreement, encircling both her and Jenna in delight...

Result 3

Title:

Splendid Feast

Text:

After leaving the baths, every noble lady departs with vivid memories: the pool strewn with petals, the refreshing pomegranate tea, the dazzling display of gems... Word spreads swiftly, reaching the ears of the Sultan himself.He nearly rises from his golden throne in fury. You prostrate yourself at once, hurriedly explaining: yes, it was indeed a banquet of jewels—but only for women! Not a man present! Not the sort of banquet he imagines, but merely a gathering where ladies flaunt their adornments. When his spies return with the same report, the Sultan finally, grudgingly, sets aside his wrath. He permits you to break the Sultan's Card. The affair brings you reputation.
