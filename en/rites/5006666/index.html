Indeed, you have a subtle feeling he is not a naive hothead. Despite his many sharp questions, there's more of an observation and scrutiny in his eyes... Gently striking the parrot adorned with chrysolite in the crook of his arm, he continues, "But now...you are right. We are planning something more important, and we must not let it slip. Besides, we will need a purer team, we need to gather more power... I will handle this." He solemnly looks at you, "If you really think he is a traitor among us, a...spy, then he very likely has forces against me. [player.name], I pray you'll allow me to handle this matter. No matter what happens, do not be the one who openly deals with him." You nod and agree.
