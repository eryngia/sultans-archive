Text:

When you handed the book to Lumera, the young girl sighed softly. Her reading voice was lighter than before, no longer the detached storytelling voice she used with the maids, as if she were an observer of a tale. There was irony and mockery in her flat words, but no more hatred. The hatred had ended by her own hand on that thrilling night. At the end, Lumera closed the book, 'I hope you won't want to hear this story anymore, Master...' She paused and lowered her head, 'And I hope there will never be such a story in this world again.'
