Title:

Golden Blossom

Text:

Iliona leads you to this place. An ordinary crossroads, it seems, the kind soon to be swallowed by the wilds. "You know, summoning the Queen might not work on the first try..." The jinn darts irritably around the crossing. "Don't look at me like I'm some kind of swindler! It's about whether you were ready before, and whether the future's ready too. Ah, see that road? No flower there. That's no good. Means 'now' isn't the time to summon her." Still muttering things you don't understand, it ignites, calling forth flames in the air. The very fire that once held it prisoner in the lamp. The flame drifts down, touching earth. Where it lands, a golden flower blooms, radiant, delicate. And when Iliona emerges from its heart, the spaces between worlds shudder, spilling pure Dew of Aether. You have no idea what just happened. But Iliona declares the ritual a triumph. Try making sense of jinns.
