Title:

Madam's Instruction

Text:

Simply arranging for you to serve clients has given Jalila immense satisfaction. Everyone who enjoys you must pay her while she stands nearby directing them how to squeeze, pinch, and extract more pleasurable sounds from your throat. When the crowd suggests she take a turn with you, Jalila laughs, pulling several gold coins from her belt and tossing them before you. "Very well, I'll try him too. However... Skills Worth Remembering

Text:

You pass her assessment. She approves of your qualifications - your outward submission, the defiance in your eyes, and that aristocratic quality your status can't erase... She casually summons a respected elderly nobleman, making him kneel before you both, then patiently demonstrates how to properly torment him. "Grasp both nipples together... You're drenched with sweat; discipline is more taxing than anticipated. Jalila, pleased with your performance, jokingly invites you to seek employment at the House of Delights anytime.
