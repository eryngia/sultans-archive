Result 2

Title:

Fears and Gains

Text:

The heavy fruits require such sturdy branches. All day, Nayla stayed with Aziz, catering to him like a king – as long as he can satisfy Nayla's desires over and over again. Aziz did so. By the time the sun rose again, he was already covered in myrrh oil and honeydew, jeweled with precious gold and silver accessories, resting on a river-like soft bed while Nayla fed cheese, wines, and grapes from lips, all the while being attended to by slaves – one massaging him, one holding instruments of indulgence for any eventuality, and another kneeling at his feet for them together to humiliate and amuse... Finally, as the sun rose to its zenith, when Nayla once again requested satisfaction, Aziz gave a tired and irksome response. It ended his bliss. He was slapped, helplessly watching the three slaves strip him of each newly donned ornament before kicking him to the ground, to be insulted and trampled by Nayla again... in the end, you were left silently watching Nayla's game, feeling some of those firm qualities had already vanished within that prostrated massive frame... When Aziz cries for your help, you inexplicably understand the Sultan.
