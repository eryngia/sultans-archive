Result 1

Title:

What divine hands, what all-seeing eyes, have forged your mind in the furnace of creation?

Text:

"Sometimes I get trapped by strange things too," she says with a light laugh. "Honestly, I feel like the whole world is a maze. Most of the time, I'm just lucky not to be lost in it!" Then her tone shifts, bright with a kind of revelation. "It's strange. All day I've had this feeling—you might need my help. I don't even know how I could help you, but... I knew I should come!" She tells you of a dream: she had replaced her eyes with diamonds. To you, the image is unsettling, even grotesque, but she recounts it with delight. And somehow, as you listen, a quiet relief settles over you, as if her words polish away the weight on your soul. "I love my creations the way I love people—no, even more than people!" she admits with a gleam in her eyes. "They're purer, more beautiful, closer to me. And thanks to you, I know now I was right to feel that way!" The two of you talk for a long while!
