Title:

Will's Power

Text:

Cruel fate ensnares you, wrapping like precious silk, layer upon layer, slowly suffocating you... But suddenly, a breeze caresses your face and reveals a startling truth - wasn't all this your choice? You could have remained silent, followed the crowd, even eagerly licked the Sultan's boots and encouraged him to cast misfortune onto others! Yet you chose to stand and oppose him. Every step was your decision... You recall those emotions - the sense of duty, that unbearable anger... in that moment, you took pride in yourself. You open your eyes, mind crystalline with clarity. Having chosen this path yourself, complaints become meaningless. You're prepared to challenge fate now, and if necessary, to seize it by the throat. Your destiny belongs to you alone, not to heaven.

Torrential Rain

Text:

The world's malice pours down like black rain, drenching you completely. You curl inward, arms tight around your body, defenseless. As pain torments you, Maggie gently embraces you. Her slender frame shields you from much of the cruelty... "None of this is your fault, my love," Maggie whispers in your ear. Yes, if the world stands wrong, then you must correct its error. Even if you die trying, at least you can say, "I did nothing wrong."

Title:

Enter the Protagonist

Text:

You suddenly recall what Nabhani said when you first entered this game, as he stood beside the Sultan... "So you've become the protagonist now, haven't you?" You had no time to consider his words then, but looking back, Nabhani's view of the game has remained remarkably consistent. He sees each encounter as merely a chapter in a story - enemies as backdrop, friends as decorative flowers - everything exists to highlight himself as the protagonist. Therefore, nothing warrants complaint! You smile wryly. Perhaps Nabhani is right... Now that you've become this game's protagonist, you should deliver an unforgettable performance for your audience. With this thought, you straighten your posture, roll your stiff shoulders and neck, then glance at your reflection. The curtain has risen, and the protagonist has taken the stage!

Result 10

Title:

Way of the World

Text:

Your thoughts turn to the cultists. Isn't their daily existence just like living in the Sultan's game? Sometimes starving, sometimes feasting, sometimes beaten by Purist priests, other times pressed against warm flesh in darkness... They never know where tomorrow finds them, whether they'll survive; they can only accept, then accept again. Ah... they understand the truth. This is the way of the world, the nature of life - whoever attempts to predict their path is a foolish sage; whoever expects to control their future is a blasphemous saint! Understanding this, you feel the burden on your shoulders suddenly vanish. Stop questioning why this fate befell you - such destiny awaits everyone. "Unpredictability" forms fate's very essence.
