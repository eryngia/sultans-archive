The Mirror dazzled with wit, testing Shama with the boundless knowledge from [xiaochou.name]’s mirror—yet Shama matched every turn, weaving in ancient texts and seizing the reins of the conversation... Weaving the World

So, with pride, you introduced your wife, Maggie, to [xiaochou.name]. Where does her taste manifest? In this home, of course—the lush gardens, the halls that welcome guests, and most of all, in the very presence of [player.name]! And let us not forget your adornments, the perfumes that shift with the seasons, each chosen by Maggie herself! People see only your commanding presence in the halls of lapis lazuli, but the discerning know: behind every great man stands a wife, a household, a legacy. Those of true breeding, the wise and learned among the powerful, they recognize such things at a glance—choosing allies with care. No one seeks alliance with an upstart lacking depth! As you and [xiaochou.name] spoke, the details piled up—some even you had never noticed. "I begin to understand. A person of taste expands their influence—arranging themselves, then others, and in turn, those arrangements become part of them... You humans, at times like beasts, all teeth and claws, and at others like trees, silently growing, claiming territory..."
