Text:

Jenna has become the empire's most sought-after jeweler. Her workshop is so busy that the carpets are nearly worn through from all the visitors. None of this, of course, would have been possible without your steadfast support. "I dare say I could call myself the finest jeweler in the entire empire—yes, the whole empire, not just this city. I've handled the Sultan's crown, polished charms for the poor, and even introduced a new gem unknown to anyone before..." Her voice falters, and her gaze drifts toward the window, lost in thought for a moment before she continues. "But they're still not enough... far from it! There's still so much I want to do, so many corners of the world I long to see. Even imagining it fills me with joy!" She rises and pulls you into an embrace. No words are spoken. You both know the bond between you is unbreakable.
