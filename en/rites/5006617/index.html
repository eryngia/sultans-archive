Text:

It is Inal's first time handling such an important task; she was so excited that her face turned red, getting up before dawn to cook the day's porridge, monitoring the heat and stirring non-stop, believing that only this way can draw out the natural sweetness of the wheat – it tastes much better even without expensive sugar. She speaks truly. Sounds of appreciation arise from the crowd as they taste the porridge, praising it as the finest they've ever had. Hearing this, Inal lifts her head proudly before lowering it shyly…

It is a tall old man, who seemed to once be in the trade where ones often die young, his body foreboding in scars. But Aziz came, pinning him to the ground with sheer strength before he can react. No matter how he struggles or begges for mercy, the silent giant would not let go. Finally, he tearfully admits he has been sent by the original person in charge to make trouble, as he couldn't swallow the fact that he had been outclassed by an insignificant female slave. But regardless, he probably won't dare cause any more mischief. No one else is hurt, and no one else dares to mess around here.

Result 3

Title:

This is getting out of hand. Something has to be done.

Text:

Aziz subdues the nearest boy. He is frail and fragile, as though a leaf, fallen in just a shove, yet his mouth still spry with curses against your family, spitting towards the porridge tent. Aziz grabs his leg, lifting him upside down like fowl. The boy screams and struggles, but the higher Aziz lifts him, the further from the ground he gets, the more he turned from cursing to crying to begging for mercy, saying he will be grateful and stop causing trouble. Aziz tosses him back to the ground, he vomitws all the delicious porridge he has just eaten. The crowd was silent, then immediately exploded in anger. In the end, it is Inal who bravely steps in front of Aziz. Under the intimidating presence of his large frame and the allure of the fragrant food in Inal's hands, order is finally restored.
