Title:

Like a Meal

Text:

You gritted your teeth and told it to treat you like food. You could nourish it; after all, you possessed strength and ideas, did you not? You even had the Dragon's Eye! Your tiny power could surely serve it... You want it to just think of you as a dessert! "Dessert! Baa-ha! Dessert! A hero with the Dragon's Eye says they're just a little dessert!" The sheep roared with laughter again, and the small cloud-creatures around it joined in. But when they laughed too hard, their shapes came undone, the clouds unconsciously reforming into generals, priests, kings, dictators—all kinds of murderers. "Very well, I will treat you as a tasty snack. I will make good use of your power... you're right, this will be useful to me, baa-baa-baa-baa —" The louder its laughter, the weaker the pressure on you became. You could stand a little straighter. "Ah, but baa... since you seem so interested and full of ideas about creating a world—ah baa, sorry, I forgot again. After all, you're the one who gathered all this power. You were supposed to be the Creator, baa-ha-ha—so what do you think? Who should be my enemy, baa? After all, enemies create friends, and hatred gives rise to love, doesn't it?" Its enemy? The enemy of the new world? Before you could even breathe easy, you had to think once more...

Title:

Like a Child

Text:

You wondered what a sheep that created the world would want to hear. Since it led a legion of strange creatures... you tentatively spoke the word "child." You told it that compared to its ancient, immense power, you were merely a child who had existed for mere decades—not even a child, at most a precocious infant! So you hoped it would guide you, teach you, treat you like its own child... Your words made the sheep and the clouds around it pause. Then they slowly gathered, as if thinking and discussing. "I am not yet a god. Only those who have created a world—and those two shameless castaways—can call themselves gods, baa. But... since you gave me your power, I can create my own world. I will be a god, baa. And a god... should probably have divine offspring." They surged toward you, pressing in from all sides. "You helped me become a god, baa, so it would only be fitting for you to be my divine offspring, my child, my heir! And then... if I grow bored and go off to play in other worlds, you will take the role of those two castaways—staying in this world forever, waiting for my return! Ah-ha-baa, perfect!" Castaways? What did it mean...? But there was no time to think. The sheep nodded in satisfaction and immediately asked the next question. "Ah, but baa... since you seem so interested and full of ideas about creating a world—ah baa, sorry, I forgot again. After all, you're the one who gathered all this power. You were supposed to be the Creator, baa-ha-ha—so what do you think? Who should be my enemy, baa? After all, enemies create friends, and hatred gives rise to love, doesn't it?" Its enemy? The enemy of the new world? You had to gather your thoughts once more...
