Result 1

Text:

You invested a significant amount of money in an unknown craftsman, and the Sultan allowed you to resolve a Sultan Card for it. Mahir used the excess funds to build an observatory. She drags you over with the promise of an unforgettable night together. She shows you a complex and magnificent instrument, with intricate patterns conforming to some natural logic and rhythm. Through the lens, you peer into the distant starry sky, the timeless gleam of faraway starts captured in your eyes, while Mahir's excited voice echoed in your ears. "I knew it... You know, I always felt that we are all governed by a force, just like these stars, moving in definite trajectories. I want to discover the laws governing that force, governing everything. That is my dream." "I think people call that foce 'God,'" you reply instinctively. The realm of God is quietly shedding its veil of mystery. Now, you have gleamed it. In the future, you will reach it.
