Title:

Fall into ignorance together

Text:

After you present the golden Carnality Card to the entire court, the Sultan lets out a wry, self-mocking chuckle: "Oh, my trusted one, I only meant to help you finish that last card – Who would have thought it would come at such a steep price? Amid his eerie, mocking laughter, guards seize you... Meanwhile, the Sultan strides unhurriedly forward, allowing his eunuchs and handmaidens to equip him with a sex toy befitting a monarch's desires – a set of gilded, bladed armor designed to weaponize his loins. You thrash wildly, too fierce for the five soldiers to hold, but then more join in – your colleagues, those who've feared you, shunned you, or used you in the Sultan's twisted game. They claw at your neck and your ankles, jabbing with knives, hairpins, and rings, tearing into you like butchers at a slaughter. Finally, you exhaust all your strength, and this struggle and betrayal only sharpens the Sultan's appetite. He advances with his gilded weapon, penetrating deeply – he begins to savor the true feast. As his blades carve through you, the Sultan Card begins to fracture, unleashing a flood of darkness – the lovers etched upon it, the tormented souls bound together, all stir to life. These are the countless souls ranked by this game, then mercilessly slaughtered. Now, you join their ranks, twisted into a record of the world alongside those you once harmed. So, with a final surge, you clutch the Sultan's chest – dragging his soul with you, trapping this moment in eternity... Thus, your suffering will be endless, and neither of your souls will ever find release.
