Divine Judgment

Text:

You provocatively pull the Sultan's Signet Ring out of your pocket – this part, of course, was within the Sultan's expectation, otherwise, why would he anticipate the battle with you? But when you lift the ring high, ready to mock the Sultan's isolation to boost morale… a greater, holier force descends from above. The starry sky glimmers for the sacrifices of the multitude; the aurora, like tears, moistens the firmament. Lumera – she represents the stars – returns to the mortal realm once more for your battle. The radiant starlight cloaks the blood-red hues of the setting sun; whether the Sultan's roars or others' cries, all fade after Lumera's gentle reassurance to you…God reaches out from the sky, through the burning city and smoke, plucking the ring from your hand like a flower. <size=+10><font="Title SDF">The symbol of power is thus shattered</font></size>, the ancient Magic is abolished…and now, let the slaughter among mortals begin – the stars shall witness this.

Title:

Divine Intervention

Text:

The Sultan has high expectations of you, but that doesn't mean he will compromise his standards: the rebellious traitor must face the piercing light of death from the Signet Ring. As its magical power surges, the sunset light and shadows in the hall twist and writhe, morphing into crimson beasts poised to devour you whole. Just as you brace yourself to resist, a greater, holier force descends from above. The starry sky glimmers for the sacrifices of the multitude; the aurora, like tears, moistens the firmament. Lumera – she represents the stars – returns to the mortal realm once more for your battle. The radiant starlight cloaks the blood-red hues of the setting sun; the deadly tides fade into nothingness. Lumera holds you close, shielding you with her back from all calamity, until she finishes speaking those words of gratitude and comfort that can never be said enough。 The Sultan feels such shock and rage that he pushes the ring's power to its limit – until it cracks. At last, even the embodiment of kingship and power reaches its breaking point – the ring no longer glows and leaves a charred scar on the Sultan's hand, hissing with heat. The starlight fades in turn – the meddling of gods and magic ends here. Let the slaughter among mortals begin!
