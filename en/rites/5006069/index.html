Result 1

Title:

The Dragon Keeps Its Promise

Text:

With its vast and razor-sharp talons, the dragon tenderly lifts the card, small as a grain of sand. Then its breath comes, scorching and absolute, falling upon the card already veiled in dark mist. In the flames, you glimpse the magician and Sultan writhing in agony. Their silhouettes twisting as if crying out in silence – but the dragon doesn't care. The heat intensifies, warping the card in its merciless fire. Countless Sultan Cards rain down, their screams echoing as they fall into the dragon's breath and dissolve into smoke. The smoke spreads like a dark web across the sky. Lost in this strange sight, you barely notice your companions rushing toward you in panic – desperate to save something, or prevent something. Then you feel it: the dragon's fire finds you too, its flames tracing destiny's path, leaving a tender kiss upon the chosen one's still-beating heart...

Text:

As you closing your eyes, you feel a gentle, protective force. The stars seem to watch over you, distant yet gentle. In that moment, you feel yourself floating among the clouds, watching everything on earth. "You... are free now." The immense energy fades away, leaving nothing in this world but ashes. The dragon watches those ashes and murmurs, "There are no more Sultan Cards... I'll take these ashes to the world where I heal. Go now, go – I owe you nothing!" A gentle, cleansing breeze brushes away the dust and blood from your face, filling you with an overwhelming sense of relief, as well as complete exhaustion. For the first time since Sultan's Game began, you can finally breathe freely.
