Result 1

Title:

This isn't fair at all!

Text:

The beast accompanying this challenger has a fierce glare and drools profusely, making it appear extremely difficult to handle. No wonder this swordsman was unbeaten throughout the city. You attempt to reason with him, claiming that such a beast in combat is unfair: "Do you even bring it to bed with a wench?" "Why not?" ...It seems you'll have to win this fight for the safety of the prostitute you've never met. At this moment, someone jumps to your side. "Well then, you wouldn't mind if he has a summoned beast too, right?" Nabhani nods at your opponent, then winks at you, mimicking a howling sound. This son of a! You couldn't help but roll your eyes at the trouble he's caused you!
