Title:

May You Walk Together Through Life

Text:

Red carnelian is the perfect stone for newlyweds, and you suggest that Jenna incorporate a dove motif. These birds mate for life, tending each other in sickness, mourning one another if parted forever. Jenna nods and sets to work. Soon, she presents two silver bracelets, each engraved with a lifelike dove whose eye gleams with red carnelian. The young couple is thrilled. They clasp the bracelets on their wrists, doves facing each other, hands intertwined. Though they cannot pay much, Jenna still finds a way to give you a modest reward for your support.

Result 2

Title:

May Your Love Shine Eternal

Text:

You hand Jenna an amethyst, a stone famed in old tales for its hypnotic, intoxicating beauty—much like love itself. You hope the violet haze it brings will linger between the couple, sustaining their affection. Jenna nods and sets to work. Soon, she presents two copper bracelets. At first, the newlyweds are a little disappointed—they had expected silver. Then the bride gasps in delight: set into the warm copper is a gleaming amethyst! The groom lifts his own bracelet for a closer look, noticing the delicate silver filigree encircling the stone, snugly held in the copper. The bracelets are tender and beautiful, precious beyond their size. In the trials of married life, these stones may serve as quiet talismans, lending strength and comfort. Jenna conveys your explanation of the amethyst and your blessings for the couple. Before your guesthouse, they bow deeply in thanks, promising to carry this gift and its blessing with them throughout their life together.

Result 3

Title:

May Your Days Be Free of Worry

Text:

Emeralds are a rare sight on this street—you've never seen anyone wearing them! You suggest that Jenna try them; such a striking stone will be remembered, and so will she. Jenna agrees—after all, the gem comes from you. The silver bracelets set with emeralds leave the newlyweds stunned. The bride almost steps back in surprise, and the groom instinctively reaches to steady her. They can hardly believe they now possess such a precious gem. After Jenna's gentle persuasion, they understand your intention. Tears glimmer in their eyes as they admire the bracelets, marveling at their brilliance and beauty. Later, if their child falls ill, the husband loses his job, or an elder becomes sick, these two stones might just come in handy. Grateful beyond words, the couple departs, promising to tell everyone of these wedding bracelets, Lord [player.name]'s generosity, and Jenna's masterful craftsmanship.
