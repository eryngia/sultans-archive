Bronze Conquest Card

Text:

There was a prosperous city near the Sultanate, that had been trading treasures and spices with an Eastern Empire for decades, if not centuries... They also made a lot of money, maybe too much money from the Sultan. It was about time for them to pay the price. Once the target of conquest was decided, the melancholy that had shrouded the court during these games was swept away. The courtiers began to talk about the inevitable victory enthusiastically. There was only one question left – whom the Sultan would send to take the loot.
