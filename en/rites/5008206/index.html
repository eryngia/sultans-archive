Result 1

Title:

The Princess in the Mist

Text:

She always arrived as night fell, just as the music and dance reached their peak, as if the entire world existed solely to welcome her. Her voice carried effortlessly through the archways, through the haze of smoke, through the murmurs of servants, reaching the ears of everyone present. Ah... the figure gliding into the ballroom draped in pearls and gossamer... her honeyed skin glowing, eyes sparkling like gemstones, fingers delicate, muscles sculpted to perfection... This—this is Princess Raedina! She raised a finger to her lips in a delicate "shush." Even this simple gesture was breathtaking, her layered gold bracelets clinking softly with the movement. Every noble present felt as though she was looking directly at them, and as she twirled her silk scarves into the dance, she seemed to merge seamlessly with the celebration itself.

Title:

She was nearly conquering the city already.

Text:

"Oh, forgive the trouble, I should have consulted you sooner... You see, I've only just arrived in this city... I do wish your master were as generous as my father, he would have made my stay comfortable, wouldn't he?" Using words, glances, and subtle suggestions, she silently rearranged every pawn on the board, keeping rivals in check, preventing them from uniting against the vagrants, making them believe they were making way for the princess while actually clearing paths for the beggars. And when they thought they were shielding her, no one noticed the children hidden beneath her skirts.
