Text:

Qais visits you again, seemingly eager to confide in you. He has arrested the true culprit, reuniting the man with his child. Through the investigation, he acknowledges his narrow-mindedness and believes that you are still the same: upright and kindhearted. Even though you are a player in the Sultan’s Game, he trusts that you will use your position for good rather than harm. Thus, he is willing to serve you with devotion.
