Text:

A few guards lead you through filthy, dark corridors and to a cell. Nawfal is resting against the wall, his fine cloak covered in blood and filth. He looks extremely tired and only slightly raises his eyelid upon your arrival. "Have you come to ridicule me? Go ahead, laugh out loud." His voice is extremely hoarse, "I was betrayed, but I will not let that traitor go. I will drag him out and bleed him dry with my own hands!" His eyes gleam coldly, but you only stare at his scarred hands hidden under his sleeves: "Do you think you can leave here?" "Of course." A hint of a reassuring smile flashes across his face, quickly masked by a piercing pride, "Just wait and see, you dirty maggots!" "We will." Internally relieved, you maintain an expressionless face, dropping a curt remark before leaving.
