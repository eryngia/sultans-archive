Title:

Woven by Favoring Winds

Text:

Mahir once argued with you that the world was born in a great explosion. And you once saw the Creator weave the world you lived in with... stories. Now, it is your turn. You rise into the void. Float, and float... Where has your new world gone? From this urgent longing, you summoned the wind. The wind stirred around you. And in the void, the wind created direction. So you began to wish for more. Let your wishes become winds from every side. But not only yours. You could feel it from the hand behind you, the warmth. Maggie. Maggie's heartbeat, Maggie's breath, Maggie's wishes calling forth more winds, wrapping around you both, fixing every direction in the void. And then, all the winds converged. You saw the keyhole. You drew out the Creator's gift. With that clay key, you unlocked a home. For you. For Maggie.
