Result 1

Title:

So Many Books

Text:

The cellar has a few small windows at the top that let in shafts of light. Through them, you can just make out the titles of the surrounding books. There are roughly dozens, perhaps even hundreds, of bookshelves here, each with three tiers, all packed with books. You drift through that ocean of knowledge. The scent of paper fills your nostrils, and your eyes gradually adjust to the dim light. Beside you, Asal moves in silence, scanning titles with feverish speed. Poetry. Reason. History. Logic. Hymns. Elegies. Nawfal's library is a wonder to behold, a hoard of wisdom gathered across ages. Even without time to read, simply standing among these relics feels like witnessing a miracle.
