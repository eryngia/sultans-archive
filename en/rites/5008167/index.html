Title:

Text:

The faithful's wild howls intensify - remarkably, amid this bestial clamor, you discern Badriyyah's words: "Give it to me." Enveloped in heavy fragrance, she approaches to take your card, raising it high: "Behold - Bloodshed! A Sultan Card permitting legal murder!" Badriyyah holds the card overhead as followers pound the ground, walls, even adjacent worshippers' heads - "We shall fulfill it, revealing to the entire empire that the Cultic God's children accomplished this great deed!" The followers surge away like a tide surrounding Badriyyah, apparently planning to kill someone matching this card's tier. But who? You remain uncertain.
