Shadows of Assassination

ID:

5006546

Text:

Bloodshed is like blood; when it drips, it soaks into your life, leaving indelible stains and traces. And you've stained yourself with too much blood. Now, at every corner, behind every footstep, outside every shadow in the window, you can see a complete murder. Those knives, those poisons, you can see them torturing your body in your mind... You urinate on the corpse of yourself stabbed to death by many knives; you spill wine over the vomit you spewed after being poisoned; in your sleep, you hear your own muffled sobs smothered by a pillow. If you don’t do something, one day, these will come true... These will come true!
