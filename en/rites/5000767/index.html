Result 1

Title:

You propose buying the barbarian female slave.

Text:

After a hand-to-hand exchange, the female slave is brought before you both. Even as her owners, her gaze remains defiant, like a wild wolf. "You are free," Nabhani says. "The gentleman and I have bought you, and we plan to set you free." "If you need a job, you can also come to me," you add, observing her sturdy muscles. Hearing this, Nabhani laughs heartily and playfully bumps you: "A job? Women don’t need jobs; you want her to find you to take another wife?" You look speaklessly at his handsome, privileged profile, and his prestigious attire that attracts attention yet nobody dares question. Nabhani has his own definition of kindness; perhaps the good intentions he spreads to girls are genuine, but... While you talk, the barbarian girl hesitates no longer, decisively turning to escape, vanishing into the crowd in an instant. "What will she do? Meet someone, then marry and have a child?" Nabhani smiles with interest. "I hope I won’t see her in a brothel — I've indeed seen ladies I rescued there."

Result 2

Title:

You propose buying the fragile slave girl

Text:

The slaver smiles as he unties the ropes binding the girl's hands, keeping praising: "You have such excellent taste! She was born in a brothel and has been trained in the full range of skills since childhood – you know what I mean! You've really gotten yourself a bargain this time!" Nabhani is always especially gentle with such frail and beautiful girls. "My dear, you are free now," he says, personally removing her shackles. "I'll burn your slave papers and restore your status as a free person. You're free to live as you wish." "But… but…" The girl's wide, desperate eyes seem to tremble as she kneels on the ground, clutching his robes with trembling hands. "Please, don't abandon me… I...I don't want a free life… I want you to own me! Let me serve you! I… I beg you!" Nabhani looks a bit awkward, clearly uncomfortable. He glances at the slave girl you both paid for, then at you: "What do you think?"

Result 3

Title:

You bought the female artisan.

Text:

You pay the money, and then Nabhani tears the slave papers to pieces right in front of the craftswoman. "From now on, you're your own master, my dear…" He glances at the name written on the torn pieces of the slave papers. "Habunah, this esteemed [player.name] and I have bought you for one purpose – to set you free." Habunah's eyes widen as she stares at the two of you in shock. Then, she smiles with relief, her expression brimming with joy and ease. "This is wonderful… it's unimaginable luck! Freedom – oh, heavens, I'm free again!" She drops to her knees, pressing fervent kisses to your feet in gratitude. "If it pleases you both, I'd like to start anew right away. I'll find a shop that offers credit, and then get some fabric and thread…" Habunah speaks quickly, already planning her future. Both of you gladly give her blessings.
