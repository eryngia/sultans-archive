Text:

When you visit Murtaz's residence again, you find it completely changed. The living room is covered by tapestries with strange totemic embroideries. Lanterns flicker in the sunless room. A neurotic fervor gleams in Murtaz's shifty eyes. He does not even notice your arrival, mumbling in prayers that you can barely make out, words like "condemnation" and "Sultan"... It seems he has no intention of rebellion. This coward has placed all his hopes for revenge on some obscure deity. How awfully disappointing. Now, you are faced with a choice. Will you kill him and report back to the Vizier, or spare this poor soul?
