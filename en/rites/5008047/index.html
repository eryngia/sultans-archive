An Example of Happiness

Text:

When you and your wife enter, whispers fill the banquet. Young ladies glance enviously at your wife, married to a powerful man for many years, whose relationship appears very blissful – even attend such an event together – and she is strikingly beautiful, elegant, and noble. Many young girls come to toast her, even kneeling before her, asking her to dip her finger into the wine and anoint their foreheads, a custom where married women do so to bless unmarried girls, praying they find similarly happy marriages. Though she tries hard to conceal her pride and satisfaction, you can tell your wife is very happy.
