Result 1

Title:

My gold! My gems! My dream of endless wealth...!

Text:

You desperately dodge the shadowy attacks, frantically throwing away the treasures clinging to your body. The dazzling golden hues vanish into the darkness. You are stripped bare, reduced to rags, then to nakedness – like a newborn, raw and vulnerable, wailing in heartbreak. And so, you fall deeper into the dream... Deeper. Deeper... Deeper! Then you see the dragon. Its enormous golden eyes, shimmering and fluid, fix upon you with fierce intensity. In front of the dragon, between you and it, stand numerous silver figures – a man among many women. "You will not win – even if you have entered my mind, you will not win," the dragon hisses, "You're just another worm!" The silver women walk to your side, surrounding you, whispering a name... "Adila, little Adila, our little Adila, she will win, we know, she will win..." A silver quenched longsword appears in your hand. You instinctively slash down... Then, you wake up. The Book of Dragonslaying has somehow crumbled to ash, while the knowledge of dragons has silently etched itself into your mind...

The Book of Dragonslaying slips from your grasp, its pages damp with your sweat.
