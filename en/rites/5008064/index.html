Result 1

Title:

Seven days later, Fardak returned alone

Text:

You set up a feast for him at home, and during the meal, he tells you that as soon as Lumera arrived in his hometown, she dived into the library there, and he follows her, reading, drinking tea, chatting, and making love there, spending time more beautiful than dreams. "But I know deep down that she does not long for me," Fardak says with a bitter smile. "Everything she does is out of a thirst for knowledge, a craving for more books. I love her, but she doesn't love me... It's pretty common, and I am not sad about it. However, I am willing to give everything to the one I love. That's why I'm willing to come back here, so she can be Lumera - the girl who spends her days in the library with her books, no husband to worry about." As he says this, a faint, happy smile remains on his face, and finally, he bows to you, thanking you for everything you do for him.

Result 2

Title:

Seven days later, Lumera returned alone

Text:

You pull her into the living room and ask what happened, while Lumera wears a relaxed smile on her lips, "Do you remember the first book you gifted me... It was then I knew what I wanted: I want to absorb knowledge ceaselessly, to make myself a tower of Knowledge. I won't be a good wife to him, and I can't handle running his estate or dealing with his clan. That's why I asked Fardak to let me come back." "Fardak cried and asked me not to leave him, but in the end, he changed his mind." She waves the books in her hand, "These are from his private library, many of which I haven't read before, and he gifted them to me before I left." At this point, she gives a slight smile. After giving you a respectful bow, she turns to her room in your mansion. She removes her splendid clothes, changes into a coarse skirt, sits cross-legged as usual, and earnestly begins reading the next book.
