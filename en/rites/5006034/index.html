Title:

Finally, Adila comes back

Text:

A long, long time passed – so long that you almost forgot her name. Until one day, Adila knocks on your door. She looks utterly exhausted, her body covered in scars, yet her eyes shine as if cleansed by a torrential storm. She tells you, breathless with excitement, that after a painstaking search, she has finally discovered traces of the dragon. According to the Book of DragonSlaying, dragons rarely leave footprints. These giant lizards are cautious, knowing their tracks are too distinctive and could easily be recognized. As a result, they only rest on hard, unyielding stone that won't retain any marks. But even the most careful plans have their flaws – they forget that the sulfurous fumes they breathe can also leave traces behind. "I found stones bearing these traces near a habitat that matches the descriptions," she says excitedly. "This must be the dragon's lair. All I need to do now is confirm that the creature is inside, and then I can prepare to strike…" Without regard for her exhaustion, she bids you farewell as soon as she finishes speaking, ready to set off once more. You ask her why she doesn't go home, why she doesn’t take a moment to rest. She simply smiles without answering, as if pausing would cause her to crumble to dust and scatter her name to the winds.. That stubborn smile haunts your dreams night after night. Deep down, you sense that her visit was nothing more than a final farewell to this world. You wake from dreams of the dragon and blood, only to realize you still have a chance to help her...
