Result 1

Text:

You kick open the door, and your wife who betrayed you stares back in shock, while the man who seduced her instinctively protects her. You recognized him instantly - Habib, the talented cook you'd summoned from your territory to prepare meals at your guesthouse. When had this liaison begun? The signs must have been there. You should have noticed. You are furious, completely losing your rationality. A war breaks out in this small room, with painful roars and the cries of a woman. This alarmed the nearby neighbors, and soon the guards of the security team arrived. They separated you and Habib, and this scandalous betrayal was exposed to the public, becoming a hot topic of conversation. People eagerly anticipate the tragic fate of Maggie and Habib, and speculate on who will be lucky or unlucky enough to become your next wife.

Your hands and feet are cold, almost unable to move. Maggie betrayed you; this thought lingers in your mind like a curse. You even find an excuse for yourself. If you just leave like this, you and Maggie would be even, right? She can go ahead and be happy with her lover, because you did the same before.
