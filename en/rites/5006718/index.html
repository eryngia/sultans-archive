Asal cradles the dragon's eye with trembling hands, yet quickly averts his gaze. "Oh... I've only read about it in a few fragmented records from ancient times... He records everything meticulously, then sighs. "A pity the dragon escaped... But perhaps that is a blessing. Future witnesses may see it for themselves, and this entry retains its worth, right?" He bows deeply, offering a long string of ornate metaphors to express his sincere gratitude.

A Game Fit for the Sultan

Text:

You suddenly flash the Sultan Card, and Asal jumps in genuine surprise. You quickly assure him you mean no harm—you're not here to embarrass him or set his shop ablaze. You only want him to see whether this item deserves a place in the Encyclopedia. He adjusts his glasses, studying the card carefully. "I feel a peculiar magic in it, yet its power does not come from mere sorcery... The rules of this game are what merit recording." He ponders, sorts his thoughts, and finally shakes his head with a wry smile. "Whether the world should ever know this game... I cannot say. I am but a chronicler. And you, having lived it—what do you think?" You answer only with a silence thick with meaning.

Title:

It's Different

Text:

You present a piece of blue silk to Asal, but he shrugs. "I've seen many such fabrics." "It's different," you insist, pulling him to the street. You show him the blue worn on the arms and waists of the poor, explaining its significance. This color is a symbol of defiance, a force unseen in history: one person alone may be weak, but thousands united can shake the world. Asal furrows his brow and thinks for a long, long time, so long he almost seems to drift off. You can't help but wave a hand in front of his face, then give his cheek a firm slap, and only then does he snap back with a sharp "Ah!" Finally, he agrees to include that utterly unremarkable blue veil in the Encyclopedia. Huh... and here you thought you'd have to talk him into it.
