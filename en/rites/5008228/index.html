Results

Result 1

Title:

Last Dance

Text:

The bells on her wrists rang clear, perfectly in tune with the music. Every curve, every glance, and every gesture gleamed like a lethal jewel. People stared, almost hypnotized, at Arzuna's sinuous form, imagining the woman you had just unveiled and claimed. How many kisses and bites hid beneath her silks and makeup? How wild had it been last night? Had she been this passionate, supple, and yielding? And those eyes—oh—under the lights, behind the curtains, how they gleamed straight at you!

After Results

Result 1

Title:

The Finale

Text:

Too bold, wasn't it? She was a dancer, not a fighter; a woman, not a man. She couldn't beat you—not yesterday, not today. You smiled as you snapped her dagger and pinned her to the wrecked table, the crowd roaring for another conquest. But then, without warning, she slit her own throat on a shard of broken porcelain. The blood gushed, then cooled; the hatred in her bright eyes froze, crystallizing into a trivial eternity.

Result 2

Title:

The Finale

Text:

Too late! The dagger plunged into your throat—then your palms, chin, eye sockets, chest... Her full, scorching hatred struck like a sudden storm, and in the last flicker of life, you saw only her icy eyes. Scarlet splashes across her face made her look twisted, mad, terrifying—but she was smiling softly. "You're no different from your father, my dear brother." She leveled the dagger at your eye. "If Mom had done to him what I'm doing to you... none of this would have happened." This was the last scene you saw. The last words you heard.
