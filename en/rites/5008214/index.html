Text:

Nabhani had become strangely industrious. He threw himself into "work", striding through the market in armor and trappings, eyeing the vendors one by one. In time, he marked out several prime patches of ground. Given his renown, charm, and place among the Honor Guard, no merchants dared complain, even though he took the best spots... "Let the girls sell flowers here!" He told you with a wide grin. "I'll make sure to visit often!" ...Well, so be it. At least some vagrants gained steady jobs. They sold not only flowers, but smiles, herbs, and lore of the wilds. After a while, even without Nabhani, both the young flower-sellers and the elder herbalists had found their place in the market.
