Title:

Desire's Soul Trap

Text:

You embraced [s3.name], then extended more arms to embrace everyone. Your dark power entranced them... Then you peeled away their skins like removing clothing. Your kiss wandered within their bodies, drinking their life essence one by one. All love and desire you ever shared, might have shared, or could have experienced across countless realities were harvested alongside their organs, becoming a feast for the abyss. The Old Ones gathered as ancient entities sampled the newcomer's offerings, nodding in approval... The Carnality Card, along with all sacrifices, crumbled to ash, opening the gateway to eternal darkness. From afar, the Magician uttered impotent curses, but you no longer needed to heed them - like a heartless wanderer, you tidied your lovers' forms and garments, ignoring the dark stains and the chaos left behind... You entered the abyss without hesitation.

Result 2

Title:

Suffering's Edge

Text:

Before everyone's eyes, you cut open your throat, extracting the first dagger from within. You then opened many old wounds, recounting their stories—some were physical scars, others wounds of the heart. From each wound emerged a weapon, and with each weapon created, another pair of crimson hands grew to wield them. Soon, you blossomed into something vigorous and terrible, like the hundred-armed monsters that battled gods in ancient myths. Yet you remained gentle, caressing the bodies of the witnesses with your powerful arms, demanding a final parting gift... Darkness surged as demons gathered, the Old Ones urging the newly-brave warrior to join the eternal game of violence... With the final Bloodshed Card, you sliced open their chests, extracting all blood, love, and joyful memories—henceforth, only hatred for you would remain, their suffering forever forging your blades... From afar, the Magician's impotent curses held no power over you. Four bloody blades pierced the wound in your neck, sprinkled with the Bloodshed Card's ashes... your massive form contracted inward, falling through this wound directly into the abyss, leaving behind only your solitary human head.

You gathered all valuables in your home - not enough, still not enough... Amid [s3.name]'s questioning, you begged from them...
