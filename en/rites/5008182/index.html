The Sultan doesn't dislike religion - it often helps him rule the people - but he definitely dislikes religions that encourage subjects to throw away their lives, as that amounts to stealing from his tax coffers. With your embellishments, he frowns but still doesn't speak. This is natural, after all - everyone knows these cultists are poor, so killing them all wouldn't cost much profit. So you grit your teeth...

What novel entertainment! The Sultan claps and laughs with interest. He commands his forces, and soon, before the summoning begins, all those cultists are captured. The Sultan personally forces the ritual's method from these terrified people. However, since Badriyyah seems to have committed suicide - or supposedly dissolved in that black pool - the remaining cultists fail repeatedly to summon the legendary powerful, beautiful, wise creatures...
