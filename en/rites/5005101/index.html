Title:

The Sultan chose the General as the target of Carnality

Text:

At first, the General thought he had misheard the order, but the Sultan never repeated himself. The courtiers remained silent, as if all their sound had been swallowed by a quicksand. Only the sound of rustling fabric was heard. The General collapsed, seemed too weak to bear his armor. He crawled forward to his master, eyes full of despair – he did not beg for mercy, for he had seen the endings of many who begged... When he finally began to lick the body of the Sultan, the courtiers turned away, could not bear to see. Later, some said he accidentally vomited and committed suicide out of guilt, while others said the Sultan personally punished him for his disrespect. None of them was willing to speak the truth. Nonetheless, the Magician announced it was a wonderful act of Carnality.

The consort tenderly came to the Sultan's side. If not her, who else could be worthy of this card of love? Soon, her smile froze. "I thought I would bed you tonight?" she bit her lip uneasily. Sultan did not answer and gave her a silent smile. The dress slowly slid down on the clean and silky-smooth marble floor, revealing her noble and beautiful body in the bright sunlight. Overwhelmed by sudden public favor, the woman forgot something important: the Sultan loves to watch women fight for him, but he does not like being used as a trophy for boasting. She swaggered to the Sultan's seat proudly, her voluptuous body pressed against him, her silk hair rubbed against his skin. During their pleasure, she flaunted enviously at other consorts, basking in the aged concubine jealousy; she even left claw marks on the noble Sultan with long nails... finally, her actions bored the Sultan. The next moment, on the golden throne, the woman struggling in panic as if a disobedient ram being throttled. At last – peace, she quells. Her empty eyes reflected courtiers' figures that gradually blurred, faded – perhaps because the court was dismissed, or perhaps because her life had ended.
