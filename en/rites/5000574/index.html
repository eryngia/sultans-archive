After Results

Result 1

Title:

Zazie's Intel: Crystal Vial

Text:

A painting arrives from Zazie's messenger—her handiwork! This sketch offers a one-to-one ratio depiction of a crystal bottle, with color and texture notes. According to Zazie's letter, during outdoor proceedings, the Sultan retrieves a scented, oily sheath from one of such bottles—to capture his precious seed. As for its nature and further fate, these are secrets even a concubine can't inquire.

Result 2

Title:

Court Secret Formula

Text:

Your discreet inquiries quickly bear fruit. The device is made from processed sheep intestines - more commonplace than you'd expect among the nobility. Many high-ranking nobles employ similar methods... You manage to obtain basic instructions for creating a passable imitation.

Result 3

Title:

Predictable Response

Text:

Whispers among courtesans tell a chilling story: a beautiful woman once captured the affection of a powerful figure who would visit the House of Delights in disguise, seeking forbidden pleasures. Consumed by ambition, she plotted to bear his child and secure her position. One night, she stole a golden box where her patron had stored his essence (now you understand he likely used one of these intestine sheaths). When she attempted to use its contents, a terrible curse befell her - agonizing pain like liquid fire coursed through her body for three days without relief. Driven mad by suffering, she leapt to her death, leaving only this grim tale behind. It seems the Sultan not only collects his seed but renders it unusable with chili or other caustic substances - a remarkably thorough precaution.

Result 4

Title:

The Final Details

Text:

Armed with Zazie's detailed specifications and whispers gathered from various servants... you've determined the exact spice blend the Sultan adds to the oils used for the sheaths... Now comes the delicate work of experimentation.
