You deliver the Sultan’s demanded tribute on time. The sum is vast –far beyond what the Sultan Card should command. But if it will soothe the monarch's wrath, who would dare say it was not worth the price? Fardak, unlucky son of an unlucky governor, offers you a bitter smile.
