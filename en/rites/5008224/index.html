Result 1

Text:

In truth, you didn't catch a single word Arzuna said. The voices were too many—buzzing in your ears, cramming your skull, arguing virtue and vice, preaching restraint, and glorifying indulgence. You wanted only one thing: silence. Your sudden roar startled Arzuna. She stepped back on instinct, but wasn't it too late? You seized her against the edge of the table, ripping apart her feeble guard and pressing forward relentlessly. Wasn't this what she wanted? Her fear flickered in your eyes, spilling into your palm. Why cry, Arzuna—your dear sister? Did she not know that cracking the dam of your heart would free a torrent that could only ruin her? You let out a cold laugh. Beneath the blood-red moon, only her trembling despair answered you.

Result 1

Title:

The Point of No Return

Text:

Arzuna froze for a heartbeat. "You think this is wrong? After everything we've done, my dear brother?" A teasing curve lifted her lips. "If I weren't your sister in this moment... if I were simply someone who desired you, would you push me away?" You shook your head with effort, explaining that last time's indulgence had been an accident of circumstance. But Arzuna stared at you, unwavering, as if she could strip your heart bare with her eyes. Finally, she stepped back. With a calm air, she lifted herself and brushed away the invisible dust from her clothes. "Ah, now you play the loyal, devoted husband. And the last time we... shared a bed? Heh..." Her eyes narrowed, a cold laugh curling her lips. "Men are all the same. Once you're off the bed, all the love and promises are gone. Reason creeps back into your head, and suddenly you're bright, upright, untarnished. How splendid, my dear brother. Let's see just how truly 'high-minded' you are in the end."

Result 2

Title:

Caught in the Web

Text:

You struggled to convince Arzuna that last time's indulgence had been an accident of circumstance, a mistake meant to be corrected. Yet, she pressed closer. Her warm skin, shimmering eyes, supple waist and arms, and the tinkle of bells with every move pulled at you with irresistible temptation. Perhaps it began with a kiss, or just a gaze, and already you were caught in her web. Through her eyes, you saw yourself. In the heat of desire, she became a mirror reflecting how your face, your life, even your fate had all been shaped by the need to please.
