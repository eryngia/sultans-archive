Results

Result 1

Title:

Buthayna

Text:

"No one is more suited than Lady Buthayna," you tell it. "For someone in her line of work, the greatest taboo is falling for a client. Her heart is harder than diamond; there is no fear she will ever love anyone. If Hassan finds himself at her side, in three days at most, he may see love in a wholly new light!" "So be it, baa! I'll set it up—just a few small adjustments, very simple! Baa baa baa baa—I'm thrilled! You have a remarkable talent for pleasing!" The lamb laughs so loud your ears ring. Its gaze never leaves you. "Next time I create a world, I must write a character like you... perhaps I will just whisk you away then, baa ha ha ha!" The clouds dissipate. Hassan calls after you, asking why you have not returned. You pause a moment to admire the garden, then slowly walk back to the room. His poetic talent, his passion, his desires—they are preserved. But his happiness in the days to come... well, that may not survive.
