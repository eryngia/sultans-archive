Result 1

Text:

You draw the runes you glimpsed deep in the mansion from memory. They are crooked and incomplete, but High Priest Iman recognizes them at a glance – they are part of some kind of sorcery spell. These dark apostates dare to sacrifices to the devil so blatantly by the royal capital. The temple cannot tolerate such blasphemy. But in order to catch them all in one fell swoop, Priest Iman cannot make a move himself. He hands you a White Honey Amber Orb, hoping that you can find the cultists hidden in the city. You know that such an orb can dispel all evil forces, just as the Purists banished the Cultic God into the void. Before it, the vengeful spirits of the mansion will have nowhere to hide. Even the magician behind it all will have no choice but to surrender. However, blessed though it is, it is still of this earth. If darkness stains it, even just by a little, it will lose its purity and perfection. It will lose its power and grow dim, becoming just an ordinary rock...
