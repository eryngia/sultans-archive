Title:

She originally thought you brought good news.

Text:

The room is filled with exquisite incense, a faint sweet scent hovering between you. You kiss her lips, silencing her troubling questions, wrapping your hands around her slim waist. As expected, she does not resist. The delicate silk carpet and gold-embroidered cushions embrace you, feeling like a maiden's bare skin. You remove the veil from Fatuna's hair and kiss her gently under the lamp. Think about it, she shows no mercy to other nobles! But you... you are different. You saved her, you killed for her, you are her hero... She must love you, how could she not? You strip away her dress, feeling the burning heat on this mature and voluptuous body. Her hand on your chest loses its strength to resist, becoming a soft silk ribbon. See, how can she not love you? You harvest this ripe and succulent fruit, embracing her passionately, over and over, admiring her trembling back. What an intoxicating night. When the candles go out, dawn light slowly spreads. Cold dark blue touches your weary brow. You don't notice that the visions of adoration that were once in Fatuna's tearful eyes have now faded. You are no different from other men.
