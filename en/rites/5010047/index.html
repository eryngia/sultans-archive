Dagger's Path

ID:

Text:

Having slain their servants, you enter the god's domain - this realm descends, merging with your world, revealing jagged claws. Stone transforms into blades, light forges mechanisms and stairs: this is the path to divine audience, recorded in sacred texts. The heavier one's sins, the faster the blade-wheels spin, the easier one's dismemberment... And your sins? Those wheels nearly spark from friction.
