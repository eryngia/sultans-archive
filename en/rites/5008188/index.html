Title:

Mind Resistance

Text:

The summoning-failed Cultic God savagely assaults you in dreams with endless suffering, piercing pain, despair and sorrow. How predictable! You grit your teeth in desperate resistance, fully grasping this deity's methods - They can manipulate your senses, rewrite the messages your skin sends to your brain, making everything around you twisted yet real. But that's all... just that! As long as you believe, remain unwavering, firmly commanding every nerve to obey your - not the divine - will, you become an unyielding reef in the Cultic God's mental tide. They crash against you but can do nothing to you.
