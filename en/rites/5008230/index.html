Result 1

Title:

Admire the Moon

Text:

The guests were seated in the courtyard, wine and food ready. Arzuna, veiled, emerged from the pomegranate trees into the midst. She stood serene, bathed in silver moonlight, drawing every probing gaze to herself... Then she swept aside her veil and stretched her body, setting every bell and bangle jingling in unison. She began to dance, eyes lifted to the moon, the treetops, the clouds—never meeting anyone present. Her arms were so long that when she reached for the moon, you almost believed she could cradle it in her hands. In the moonlight, she seemed to have four arms, each with a mind of its own, commanding every hand, every body, and every soul. She turned and stepped with deliberate grace; her body bent, collapsed, curled... then expanded again. In that instant, everyone there knew the same truth—Arzuna was the moon incarnate. Strangely, this Arzuna was not seductive. Her dance was not meant to please you. If anything, the moon alone was her honored guest.
