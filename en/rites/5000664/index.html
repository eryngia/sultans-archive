Text:

You have already noticed the troop's movements and know that the Sultan has dispatched forces to the city. Destruction is now irreversible; why not allow it to serve its last purpose? You present a card inscribed with royal power, humbly asking the Sultan for an opportunity to expand his territories. The Sultan laughs heartily and agrees. As the commander, you obtain the map that Manar gave to the Sultan — you suddenly notice that the locations marked on the map are incorrect, but it's not a problem for you, who personally experienced the expedition. You order the troops to besiege the stubborn city until its supplements are completely depleted, then eventually breaking through. In revenge for wasting your time, you massacre the citizens and loot all their possessions. This trampled land no longer holds any value marked on the map.
