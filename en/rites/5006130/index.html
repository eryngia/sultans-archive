Text:

The new medicine stands ready, neatly sealed in crystal vials. When you arrive, Samir is fastening labels to the glass. You lean closer. The words read: "Not for human use." He wipes the sweat from his brow and turns to you. "The time is too short, the demand too high... I won't risk giving these to people again... So I marked them—to prevent a mistake." He exhales, his shoulders finally loosening. Weariness clouds his eyes. "From here, it's in your hands. I can't face them again."
