Text:

This meeting reveals Iman looking increasingly weak and exhausted, his lips and eyes parched, as if faith no longer sustains him - as if his devotion drains his final vitality... "Make me hurt," he says, "like last time." You silently bind his hands, positioning him comfortably. You avoid all wounds, offering gentleness and compassion. This contradicts his desires, but isn't forcing unwanted experiences also painful? His jewel-like eyes moisten, losing characteristic coldness; his hoarse voice begs bloodshed, but the bound priest cannot self-harm, depending entirely on you. You control his pain and pleasure, his very life; you offer a moist kiss, prolonged climax. No deity intervenes this insect-chirping night; you reward this faithful servant yourself. You explain that pain-extracted faith lacks permanence, like loyalty forced through fear. He stares blankly, half-believing, half-understanding. In this emptiness, he fulfills your every request, including temporarily leaving the Purist Order for brief respite at your residence. You don't know how he explains this to priests; regardless, next day he arrives beneath your roof, back wounds unchanged.
