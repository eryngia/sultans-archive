Result 4

Text:

Had it been before the Sultan had obtained the Dragon's Eye Gem, he might have crafted some amusing ways to break his hard-won golden card, savoring how fear rippled across his courtiers' faces. His surviving relatives—the general, the princess, and Vasir—each met their end in rather creative ways. But today, the Sultan had found something far grander—the eye of a dragon that fled to another dimension. He could almost see that unconquered, pristine white land, his heart set on reaching it—no longer bound by the petty bloodshed of the mortal world. He gave a few brief commands to his attendants, breaking the card in half, and leaves the court with unhurried grace. Not long after, you heard of an unassuming aged consort's death in the harem—one who once served a former Sultan, and who, they say, gave birth to a demon.

Result 11

Text:

The Sultan toys with the dragon's eye gem, glances at you, and smiles thoughtfully. His golden accessories chime delicately as he rises from the throne, approaching you with measured, condescending steps. In an instant, countless thoughts flood your mind. You recall the folk illustrations and novels, and the treasonous words you once dared to speak... You dread hearing your name pass from his lips, yet cannot deny that faint hope stirring in your heart. If it were merely an act of indulgence, if – just if – you could profane that golden throne in this way, if... You lower your head deeply, unwilling to let your expression be seen by the Sultan’s piercing gaze. Then, a broken card clatters to the ground at your feet. The Sultan laughs heartily and glides away. Only after his footsteps fade completely do you find strength to wipe your brow. Of course – you should have known. For one who commands a vast harem, choosing a silver tier consort is not a difficult task.
