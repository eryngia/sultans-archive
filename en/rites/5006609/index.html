All words melt under the Rod of Life of golden tier, breakneck speed spinning scepter that ruled over love and lust and sang the praises of life. Nayla, drenched in hot sweat, is almost reduced to a fountain of spring water. "This is something I have never tasted... Oh, it's amazing." Nayla moans hoarsely, almost obsessively stroking the place where you merge, feeling that relentless vibration, "I might die here, really. If I were to choose a way to die, I would want to die here." You rarely see such seriousness in Nayla's eyes, as if she truly touched the end of life’s destruction. But how absurd was this earnestness mingled with ceaseless lust! It is even less scrutinized than love, collapsing yet once more into another deep, involuntary collision.
