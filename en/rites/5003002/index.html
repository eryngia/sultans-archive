Text:

Maggie is back. She nods at you as usual, but before she walks past, you grab her and ask why she didn't tell you in advance. She calmly replies that even if she had told you, you wouldn't have gone with her to visit friends, right? She's right. You were completely caught up in those cards. They gave you a lot, courage, power to do things you never dared or could do before. But for everything they gave you, they took away more. In this game of cards, you are neither the dealer nor the player anymore; you can't leave the game and say you've had enough and now will spend time with your beloved Maggie. There was a time when Maggie always smiled at you. That was a very long, long time ago.
