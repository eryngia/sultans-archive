After hearing your words, Nawfal's expression turns peculiar. " Do you believe they are under my command?" He pauses, then slowly says, "I am not their leader, nor are they my allies. This is just a place to talk – talk truthfully. If there's any price to pay for this truth, I am ready." He glances at you, sighs slightly, and adds, "That was my initial thought." He gently strikes the parrot adorned with chrysolite in the crook of his arm, and continues, "But now...you are right. We are planning something more important, and we must not let it slip. Besides, we will need a purer team, we need to gather more power... I will handle this." He solemnly looks at you, "If you really think he is a traitor among us, a... spy, then he very likely has forces against me. [player.name], I pray you'll allow me to handle this matter. No matter what happens, do not be the one who openly deals with him." You nod and agree.
