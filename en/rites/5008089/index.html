Title:

Peacock's Display

Text:

With ill-concealed pride, you described Maggie's culinary preferences in elaborate detail - naturally, no one understood her tastes better than you! Unfortunately, Habib absorbed every word with earnest attention, betraying no reaction to your boasting. Requesting brief patience, he donned his apron and soon presented an exquisite dish. He even advised you which lunchbox would best maintain proper temperature during delivery... Momentarily speechless, you finally snatched the meal away in irritation. After dining, Maggie's mood brightened considerably, and the recent tensions between you dissolved effortlessly. You would never admit the dish's true creator.

Title:

Send Inal

Text:

Inal provided Habib with remarkably detailed descriptions of her mistress's favorite dishes. He listened with intense concentration, even making written notes. Donning his apron, he welcomed Inal into the kitchen, generously sharing culinary secrets for future occasions when her mistress might need cheering. Inal returned rosy-cheeked with happiness, humming while arranging the dishes before Maggie. Learning you had commissioned this feast through Habib, Maggie finally offered a soft "hmph" and the beginnings of a smile. The petty disagreements between you melted away like morning frost.
