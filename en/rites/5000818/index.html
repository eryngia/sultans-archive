Text:

Nabhani warmly receives you and Riel, serving pomegranate tea mixed with honey, plates of pastries and preserved fruits. Riel eats and drinks without restraint, completely disregarding appearances. She downs an entire jug of wine like water, wipes her mouth, and says: "I remember you - you and [player.name] bought me from slave traders. I'm grateful for your rescue and all your help, but to prevent more of my people from needing your 'rescue,' let's solve the problem at its root." Her eyes flash dangerously, saying viciously: "Let me be clear: I want to eat those slave hunters' livers." Nabhani pauses, as if never considering this an issue, then contemplates, "You want to catch them? Hmm... these hunters hide behind layers of transactions, likely part of a vast organization - some specialize in capturing barbarian girls, others in skilled rtisansa... but ultimately, they all report to the same 'boss.'" Here, Nabhani suspiciously hesitates, looking toward you, seemingly troubled: "I do know someone... who frequently hosts these slave girl sampling parties..." Before you can understand his hesitation, Riel's fury ignites. Nabhani desperately waves his hands to clarify: "Wait, I've never attended, I prefer mutual consent, I never engage in rape - hey, [player.name], back me up!" Uh... vouching for this seems somehow wrong, but you guarantee Nabhani's respect for women. Riel coldly draws the dagger from her waist, licks the blade, then forcefully stabs it into the floor. "I must kill these people. My sisters cannot be slaves." She struggles to suppress her rage, saying fiercely, "Please continue, I said please, noble sir." As the Sultan's Iron Guard and darling of noble ladies, Nabhani rarely looks so helpless... but seeing your determination, he reveals everything he knows, giving you an invitation to that sampling party before you leave...
