Title:

A True Diamond!

Text:

You need not even step outside to hear of the lady who has commissioned a pair of diamond earrings. She wears them everywhere—even stooping so far as to parade them in the slums. The weight of her gems changes with every retelling, each time a little heavier. Jenna's design no longer matters; what truly gleams now is what these flawless stones represent—status, wealth, and the meaning people impose upon them. The tale spreads quickly, and both you and Jenna gain a great reputation from it. Completing a genuine diamond piece also teaches Jenna much in Jewelry Design.

Result 2

Title:

What She Truly Wants Is Not Rubies, but Prestige

Text:

A pair of sapphire earrings—each gem pale and translucent, like a handful of seawater. With careful cutting, they shimmer so brightly they could almost be mistaken for diamonds. The noble lady is utterly charmed. She flaunts them everywhere, encouraging others to assume they are diamonds. Later, she commissions several more pieces in a similar style, spending a fortune—though still far less than what true diamonds would cost. Jenna shares part of the profit with you. And beyond the gold, she gains valuable insight from this creation.
