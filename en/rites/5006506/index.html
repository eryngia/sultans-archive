Result 1

Title:

You held a low-key wedding

Text:

When bringing the second wife into the household, there was no need for an extravagant wedding. You held a simple parade, loudly announcing your union to everyone. After the necessary feasts and socializing, you took Fatuna away. That night, you fully relished the sweetness of this juicy and delightful fruit. Afterwards, no matter how generous Maggie tried to be, she remained cold towards you two for several days, while Fatuna knelt earnestly before her every day, holding her legs and promising to serve her. Gradually, they started having tea together again. This marriage earned you considerable support from the nobles, and of course, a lot of wealth. Zaki is now your son, and his prestige in the palace has also increased. Marriage is truly a wonderful thing, you sigh inwardly, unable to stop wishing for more... But you can only imagine, now that there are two pairs of eyes watching you closely...

Title:

The Wedding was Held as Scheduled

Text:

Although generally, the second wife’s entrance should not be so extravagant, you still organized an extremely lavish and opulent wedding that made all women envious and all men jealous. You held a grand parade, taking Fatuna through every decorated street of the city before arriving at a huge open space to announce your union to everyone. You also spent a fortune on the best band, dancers, and singers, and all the guests at this grand event were laughing and blessing your marriage. This unseemly wedding made Maggie very jealous and she ignored you for a long time, while Fatuna knelt before her every day, holding her legs and promising to serve her. Gradually, they started having tea together again. This marriage brought you Fatuna's generous dowry, and the city nobles began to curry favor with you and Zaki – now recognized as your son. Some of them even brought their daughters... But you could only politely decline their advances. These twin pairs of scrutinizing eyes were now a constant reminder...

Result 4

Title:

You have no idea how long I've prepared for this.

Text:

Through the crowd, the gentlemen sing, the ladies dance, and the children scramble for candy. The servants wave colorful torches, clearing a path for the banquet's final dish. Traditionally, this would be grand dish – a roasted camel, cow, or sheep. Each guest receives a piece of meat, symbolizing shared joy and blessings. However, this time, the dish is covered with a massive, shiny copper pot – it seems smaller than expected. A strange odor fills the air, halting the singing and dancing as all eyes turn anxiously to the covered dish... As the copper pot is lifted, silence shatters into screams. At the center of the plate lie the severed heads of Fatuna and Zaki, painted with festive makeup and draped in their finest jewelry. Blood pools beneath their heads, soaking the sliced cold cuts arranged like flowers – a jagged, beautiful yet mesmerizing scene. Under your blade, the guests shudder helplessly. One by one, they gingerly lift the petals, forcing the blood-soaked flesh past their trembling lips – bearing in the wedding's twisted blessing. As the feast concludes and all have savored their fill, the Sultan himself breaks the Bloodshed Card card, highly praising your artistry.

Result 5

Title:

You told Fatuna you would possess her completely, in every sense of the word.

Text:

Her entire family comes, each one of them. Of course this would happen – through her, the family has achieved an alliance with a powerful courtier! Finally, this woman has done something beneficial for the family... One by one, they step forward, offering congratulations more stale than halitosis... Ah, surrounded by these parasites, how could one breath free? Fortunately, you have never intended to humble yourself – your love for Fatuna is going to be long-lasting. You won't let these dirty family ties rip your marriage and life apart like barbs... certainly not like her previous husband, whose life was threatened. Thus, you personally fill each of their cups with a specially made fine liquor, even preparing preserves and candy for the children and infants... After ensuring the liquor and delicaciesare consumed, you tightly embrace Fatuna and Zaki – your wife and son. They stiffen in your arms, trembling like cornered prey, but they cannot escape your warm, strong arms. They watch as their relatives collapse one by one, foaming at the mouth, blood spurting from their ears and noses. When the last body hits the ground, and silence blankets the room, you raise your hands, break the card in two, and declare to the stunned guests the extermination and conquest of a noble lineage. From then on, everything that once belonged to this family now belongs to your wife and son – and they, in turn, belong to you.

Result 6

Title:

Double Blessing

Text:

At the peak of the celebration, tradition dictates that you take your bride on a luxurious carriage to tour your lands – fulfilling the ancient ritual of blessing the fertile soil through carnal union. The young men are in a frenzy; at this moment, even a powerful courtier like you and your wives aren't spared from their bawdy jokes. People shower you with spiced wheat and petals, shouting names of sexual positions, and you even hear some placing bets... But they'll all lose their wagers today – instead of only pulling the bride onto the carriage, you grab Maggie with your other hand. Why not? What's the point of having two wives if you can't enjoy them together? Perhaps in the future, there will be many annoying things, but today – at least today – no one can deny your desires. The carriage races through your domain; rich and lush fields spread on both sides, all yours now... They're worth your madness and tireless plowing, even if you might not live to see the harvest... This proves an exceptionally successful blessing ritual. The Sultan praises you as a model nobleman, not only allowing you to break a Carnality Card but also making an unprecedented decree – granting a year's tax exemption to all fields along your carriage route.
