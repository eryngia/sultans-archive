The royal guards return with grim news: Samir has already taken poison. His death is beyond doubt—a coward's suicide. The Sultan shows no satisfaction. To him, it means this traitor betrayed twice: once when he renounced his homeland, and again when he fled the Sultan's justice. As he leaves the Lapis Lazuli Palace, the Sultan orders coldly: drag the body away—and the body of his favorite besides. Feed them to the dogs. Lions and leopards are too precious for such carrion.
