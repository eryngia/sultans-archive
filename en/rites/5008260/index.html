So Close, Yet So Far

Text:

While you were lost in deep thought, Lamblet's form completely dissolved into overwhelming divine radiance, shedding all mortal shape and image. It had finished seizing the divine authority. As for this ridiculous question—ha, did it really need you to answer? Did it truly intend to create the world based on your ideas? Did it really need you to point out its enemies? Did it have to consider your will? Did it even need you as its servant? Just think! A brand new world had already bowed at their feet! What else could you possibly do?
