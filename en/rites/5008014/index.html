Text:

At first, Qais doesn't know why you call him over until you present the title certificate. He almost can't believe the seal on it, opens his mouth, but doesn't know what to say. Look at his flustered appearance! You laugh heartily, pat him on the shoulder and tell him, it’s his – now he can tell his mother that their family is once again hereditary nobility, their next generation will no longer live bereft of their forefathers' name!

Text:

Qais is extremely moved. In his eyes, filled with complex emotions, it seems he has never been treated so kindly, as if no one has ever helped him so unconditionally. He has no friends in the court, those people once linked arms with him when the Qais family was at its peak, also kicked him mercilessly when he needed help. Qais doesn’t need such filthy and hypocritical friendship. But you are different, you are so upright, kind, and tolerant... You are the model courtier in his heart, and he has utilized your influence, your kindness... A sense of shame arises in his heart, then he solemnly looks at you, holding the thin parchment recording the hereditary status, pledging a promise that will be passed down in eternity: "I swear by the ancient honor of the Qais, as long as this surname exists; one day, you and I, your family and my family, will be friends eternal."
