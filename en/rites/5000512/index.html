Roll 1

Text:

When he realizes that you are unmoved by pleas and tears, Amur puts away the pointless performances. He stands up straight. You suddenly discover that he is not as old as his age would suggest – few people in the Sultan's cities live pass seventy. A life is so easy to take, by the climate, poverty, sickness – but Amur looks young. Neither hunched nor gaunt, this body that should be close to death is full of an ominous vigor. He stares at you fiercely with his clear eyes and draw a dagger from his waist: "You asked for it." Your mind darts back to the Haunted Mansion, the evil ritual you still lack understanding of... And you realize that this fight may not be as easy as you imagined.

Result 1

Text:

The blade pierces through his chest. A dirty scarlet soaks through the layers of silk, and is cooled by the night breeze. Death comes as quietly and irresistibly as the night, petrifying his final expression of anger and fear. Amur's eyes slowly lose focus, until they are as dull as stone. You calm your breathing, still ragged from the fierce combat, and after a while, you pull out your scimitar and cut off Amur's head. The vengeful spirits entrenched in that mansion would appreciate this gift.
