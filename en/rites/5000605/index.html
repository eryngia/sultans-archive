Title:

It's basically settled

Text:

The vineyard owner staggers from the House of Delights. Drunk on rage and wine, he gathers his men and storms to Fatuna’s doorstep, voice raw with fury. That woman! She dares to make an attempt on the life of her husband’s own brother! He only wanted a little money... And besides, why shouldn't his bother's woman also be his? Fatuna watches this foaming joke of a man, her face impassive. She lets him rant, lets him unravel. And so, by the time the sun rises, the whole street has learned of what this ludicrous man has done. For goodness' sake, there is of course nothing shameful about what he has done, but how could he speak of these things in the open? Having lost face like that, the man soon leaves the city. An acceptable outcome, all in all. Though the nobles still glance at Fatuna with knowing smiles, she does not care. She remains grateful for your help.
