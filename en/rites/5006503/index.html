At dinner this evening, your wife sits beside you, takes your cup, sips it lightly, then whispers to you, 'Dear, I have something to say to you... but not here.' You excitedly grasp her fingers. Oh, has the day finally come? Maggie is always so considerate, and Fatuna is so clever... How did Fatuna convince Maggie? She truly loves you... You feel a sense of intoxication, not from the many drinks tonight, but from the happiness that is now within reach... a happy life with Maggie and Fatuna!

Text:

Maggie smiles as she leads you into the bathroom. The steam rising from the pool creates an intimate warmth on her face. The sweet scent of roses floats ambiguously, and Maggie helps you take off your outer robe, scrubs your back, and brings you a cup of tea brewed with cloves and a good bottle of wine. In the steaming mist, you drink wine happily, sip tea, and caress her smooth skin. The steam seems to seep into your skin through your pores, and soon you feel a dreamy daze come over you... A pair of slender hands gently stroke the top of your head, then those hands mercilessly push your head underwater! Wait, wait a minute...! Something's wrong! You struggle desperately, the will to survive nearly pulls you out of the tub, but another pair of hands climbs up like seaweed, tightly wrapping around your neck, and a younger pair of hands presses firmly on your back like a stone that won't budge. You can't breathe anymore, dizzy and weak. In the despair of near-death, you hear Fatuna's soft and vague voice, as if from the sky. “I will share everything with you, Maggie, whether good or bad... this guy doesn't deserve you!”
