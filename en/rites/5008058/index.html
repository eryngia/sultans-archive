Title:

I can escort you away

Text:

You tell Fardak you have a secret troop that can escort him discreetly onto the hazardous pathway to home. You can prepare enough food, water, and servants for him, allowing him to decide his future after returning home. Fardak remains silent, contemplating, and finally looks at you, shaking his head: "No, I cannot leave." "You've treated me so well; I cannot bring disaster to you, nor leave you to face the Sultan's fury alone... No matter how much I desire freedom, I won't sacrifice you for it." His eyes glow with unprecedented resolve. He chooses to stay.

Title:

You can leave during the chaos

Text:

You tell Fardak that you have resolved to rebel against the Sultan. When you launch your attack on that magnificent yet cruel throne, he can escape the city during the chaos and never return. Fardak is stunned. He asks many, many questions, finally confirming you are not joking. He remains silent, contemplating, and finally looks at you, seriously shaking his head: "I will not leave." "If I abandon you at this critical moment, I would despise myself for the rest of my life. Let me do something for you!" His eyes glow with unprecedented resolve. He chooses to stay.
