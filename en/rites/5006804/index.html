Hunt Details Unknown

Text:

At the edge of the hunting grounds, you waited anxiously... Finally, Jabal and Gullis returned! They proudly carried two massive wooden poles, from which hung the enormous Bharal they had captured. The beast was so huge that everyone crowded around in amazement to get a closer look. Jabal laughed heartily, recounting over and over how they had carefully crept into the den, how they had fooled the Bharal's keen ears, and how they had risked life and limb on the sheer cliff faces to subdue it... All thanks to Gullis's help, the Bharal hadn't been pushed over the edge at the last moment—an act that could be called life-saving. Before returning home, Jabal handed five gold coins to Gullis as a token of gratitude.

Still, Jabal had broken a leg, and the bharal had long since escaped. Accidents weren't uncommon during the Grand Hunt, so no one minded much; however, Jabal insisted on giving Gullis a generous reward for saving his life.
