After Results

Result 1

Text:

After sending off the last guest, Maggie half-jokingly asks what you’re planning. You bring her close and share your thoughts. Maggie's cheeks have been lightly flushed with a hint of drunkenness – perhaps she drank earlier. You think she will scold you, for she has always been strict and unsmiling towards servants. But now, she smiles, whispering in your ear, “Sure, I'll go to the kitchen to cook a dish to reward everyone.” You realize she has long been considered the servants as family. She understands everyone's concerns, knows the best place for each person, keeping the household running smoothly and worry-free. Each servant naturally gathers around her, often looking at her with admiration. You have Aziz bring out the best wines from the cellar, toasting Maggie, naturally the servants toast Maggie too. Thus, the restraint and unease between you and the servants vanish completely.

Result 2

Text:

After the last guest is sent off, the after-beast begins. Unlike usual, the servants don’t gather in the kitchen but come to the candle-lit banquet hall, sitting on embroidered cushions. They feel somewhat uneasy, no one dares to speak, let alone touch the well-prepared delicacies on the plates. But you have Aziz bring the best wine from the cellar and toast everyone. “Tonight, I am no different from any of you, merely one among the masses, each of you possesses merits worthy beyond me. Let us toast this damned fate! At least tonight, we are all the masters here.”

Result 5

Text:

Aziz sits beside you; before seating, he attentively cleaned his face and hands by the pond, tidying his clothes – judging by his demeanor, he could be a tribal chief. He calmly savors each dish before him, occasionally offering slow remarks. Many, devastated by abrupt changes and fallen from grace, find only death as a release. Many slaves forget they were once lords of their lands. What about Aziz? Unfortunately, you ask again if he thought about buying his freedom. Aziz looks at you; before answering, you signal him to wait with a hand gesture: “Come to me when you’re ready with your answer.” He nods slowly, you are unsure if he's taken it to heart.
