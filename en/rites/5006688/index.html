Title:

Jenna hosts him.

Text:

Jenna lounges lazily. She is, after all, an expert. Her finest clients either aim to become so beautiful that they stir a cold heart, or to charm someone they desire. Hassan asks, "But isn't there a simpler reason? Don't some people just buy jewelry to please themselves?" Jenna laughs. "Of course, many do. But those aren't my best clients—they haggle. Their minds are still clear; they haven't yet been swept away by love!" Hassan enjoys their conversation immensely, and later pens this verse: "To squander for another's love, is only so, because they have not yet learned to love themselves."
