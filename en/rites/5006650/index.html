Text:

You gather the poor together and then pay the temple priests to host a grand ceremony. They distribute food and read scriptures, preaching that suffering is a necessity and a divine test; only through this can sinners cleanse their souls’ filth, return with purity to the divine's presence and head to the eternal white distant shore... Many people sing hymns through tears, and in the endless sands, this place seems like the only haven.

Result 2

Text:

You gathered the poor together, then showed them the miracle of the dark night. You concocted a elixir with animal blood, certain herbs, spices and invited everyone to drink, telling them that after daylight's arduous toil, the night would bring the sweetest and most joyful dreams. People were entranced by your enticing whispers, murmuring praised to an unknown, vague name. Nawfal watched all night, tight-lipped, and only questioned you after everything ended. You smiled, using the prepared excuse: only secret faith can unite people, and besides, you did nothing to harm them – the every radiant gold leaf in the temple was extricated from the devotees' wallets by dubious means. Nawfal sighed gently and left the scene without word.
