Manar’s Farewell

ID:

5000665

Text:

Manar decides to leave. Her laments as she bids you farewell. She cannot bear to have her talents exploited by the Sultan any longer. She will journey to find a place untouched by the Sultan's Influence, never to return again. You. You are her only cherished companion. She smiles, "Wish me luck. If I find such a paradise, I will write to you." No matter how you loath to say goodbye, the time for parting has come. This journey will surely be difficult. Maybe you can help her one last time...
