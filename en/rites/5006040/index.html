Title:

You keep the gem for yourself.

Text:

You have the craftsman fashion the gem into a necklace, which you wear proudly each day. You become the talk of the empire, drawing nobles from across the land to witness the gem with their own eyes. Even the Sultan contemplates taking your head to claim it, though, for unknown reasons, he never acts on this thought. Later, when you finally tire of showing it off, you make a disturbing discovery – you cannot remove the necklace. The dragon's eye seems to have become bound to your soul, making it impossible to separate.
