Title:

Pure Blade Descends

Text:

It radiates light too brilliant to behold... No cutting edge visible, appearing less like metal than white clay. Piercing countless layers of dreams, it severs a nearly omnipotent corruption tendril - in ancient times, when the Creator usurped the One's privilege and defaced the world, the Immaculate Purity judged Their sin and banished Them. Now, Their blade can judge this transgression again... This sadistic pleasure makes the blade emit terrible screams, making you want to tear out your ears! Those tendrils, even the Corrupter's entire dreamscape, begin collapsing and burning due to sins unrepented since antiquity... For just a moment, it also begins erasing you, seeking to render you pure white - childhood friends' names, some delicious sauce recipe, Maggie's sensitive spots, your first pet's color... Before your beloved memories vanish, you feed this blade everything about the Sultan's Game: infinite cycles, endless suffering, countless choices... In destiny's grinding gears, it finally grows obedient, lending you its error-erasing power.
