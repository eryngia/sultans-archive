Text:

Soon enough, Habib seems to have won over the harem concubines' stomachs... They frequently seek him out for snacks, sweets, and preserves. This is dangerous, isn't it? The eunuchs receive orders, preparing tools to welcome Habib as their new companion. The poor chef, unwilling to lose his lifelong happiness, somehow learns of this and desperately flees to your compound seeking help - while the Sultan's guards search the neighboring streets for him.
