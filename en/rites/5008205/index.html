Result 1

Title:

The Title of Princess

Text:

On the fringes of the Empire, many barren, backward tribes exist, but they are at least recognized as nobles by Sultan. Your plan is to present Raed as the tenth daughter of a tribal leader, sent to the Capital to find a suitable husband. Naturally, this requires funding... but Raed surprisingly offered to pay it herself. She twisted a silk handkerchief in her hands, a little awkwardly—by its craftsmanship, it seemed to be a relic from her homeland. But a foreign princess with a taste for vintage trinkets? Perfectly plausible. "My mother left me a small fortune... likely earned from selling off a princess in the past, which is why I've never touched it. But now I need to become Princess Raedina, gain standing in high society... then I can help your cause, and you can care for the vagrants. Perhaps together we can accomplish something truly remarkable, yes?" You talked for quite a while longer. Before leaving, she reminded you to secure influential nobles to spread the word: "A tribal princess is coming to the Capital." The more people who hear it, the less likely her true identity will be discovered.

"I will tell the nobles who come seeking the Pure Word that Princess Raedina is soon to arrive. She hails from the most virtuous family, who once wandered the wastelands in austere devotion, and have only recently returned to the tainted world of men."

Princess Raedina is supremely beautiful.

Text:

Nabhani could barely contain his excitement—this was a princess, after all! A never-before-seen beauty! He bombarded you with questions about her appearance, hobbies, attire, tastes, figure, choice of mate, birthday, hair color, eye color, favorite gemstones—until you were thoroughly exasperated. "Alright, alright, I get it! We simply must tell everyone interested in the Empire's beauties that the finest flower is about to join the garden. A pearl-crowned princess, how splendid... I can hardly wait to meet her! Shall I arrange an audience in the next couple of days? Come on, don't pretend—I can tell she's already here. It's just the identity she wants, right?" You swore you would regularly create opportunities for Nabhani to meet the princess alone, and only then did he reluctantly relent.

"I cannot help you; it's wrong. We must not deceive the world," Qais said, staring at you. "If she truly is a princess, I will declare it openly; if she is not, I cannot call her one." You stared, momentarily confused. He continued, frowning, "So... she really is a princess, yes? I see no reason for you to deceive me." Enlightened, you realized—yes, yes, of course! She is a princess; you are not deceiving the righteous Qais. Satisfied, he nodded. In the following days, you could hear him proudly proclaiming to anyone who would listen, "Princess Raedina is the most princely princess; no one embodies a princess more than she does"—words with little meaning, yet impossible to forget.
