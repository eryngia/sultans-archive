Result 4

Title:

Your soul is noble

Text:

You decline the handmaiden’s payment with practiced grace, assuring her that the consort’s secret is safe with you. She is taken aback by such a display of nobility. Then, after a moment’s hesitation, she makes a request. The consort wishes to keep her secret buried, and who better to handle such a delicate task than one who already knows too much? By chance, Sadani has learned that on the night the Sultan visited her chambers, the Chamberlain, through negligence or intent, omitted several critical details from the logs. An oversight that, left uncorrected, could be the death of her unborn child. She wants you to amend it, to ensure no questions of her child's legitimacy arise. In return, Sadani offers a fortune. You ponder this. Then, at last, you promise her you will consider her proposal.
