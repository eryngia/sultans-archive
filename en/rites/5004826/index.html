True battles unfold within... The moment has arrived. You feel the conflict advancing to its next phase. Staring into the void toward a nonexistent horizon, you watch as ashen mist gradually forms a door. Upon it rests a small keyhole, wavering in the mist, its shape inconsistent, beyond touch. Can you discover the key this keyhole yearns for? Can you pass through the doorway into realms forbidden to mortal steps?
