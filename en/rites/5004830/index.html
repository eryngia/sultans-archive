Title:

Beyond Gods Power

Text:

You run fingers through your hair, rubbing your scalp against the grain. You know several small cysts cause pain and occasional infection. Were these cysts also the Creator's design, if you're His handiwork? You touch your throat, recalling childhood swelling that nearly prevented breathing. Your parents took you to Purist priests, who saved your life with sacred words... If this ailment was divinely created, wouldn't curing it with divine power constitute collusion? You don't know humanity's origin, but your reason, knowledge, and intuition confirm: human origins stem from no god you've encountered. They lack omnipotence - otherwise, wouldn't they rule directly rather than laboriously persuading you to serve them? This realization invigorates you, filling you with boundless energy.

Title:

Creation as Cooking

Text:

You think of Habib. His hands, stained with blood, oil, and flour, are creator's hands. He conjures delicious dishes from nothing, seemingly extracting flavors from ingredients through his touch. Perhaps gods create worlds similarly. Beginning with chaos, all materials mixed together; then skillful heating, careful shaking, constant stirring, timely seasoning... You consider that even master chefs like Habib must have begun clumsily. What about gods? Perhaps your "world" merely represents god practice - containing excessive bitterness from overboiling, lacking sweetness and fragrance because They forgot to add mercy's sugar... But Habib continues making more dishes, and gods continue crafting more worlds; everything earthly merely represents experiments, steps toward Their conception of "perfection." Yet you harbor your own vision of "perfection," and considering this, you cannot help imagining "world creation" scenes; these rich, mad thoughts spread like blood in seawater... attracting sharks. The Cultic God enjoys your thoughts. He devours your fantasies as you devour Habib's stew.

Title:

Imperfect Victim

Text:

You think of Shama. Her body... though providing you incomparable pleasure, indescribable experiences - remains misshapen, deeply troubling her, not to mention the abandonment, hatred and accusations she suffered because of it... If we were godly created, which god deliberately created such an imperfect victim? For what purpose? You cannot imagine the Cultic God or Purist doing this; the former would be more extreme, while the latter's priests helped her father expel her from home. Since gods couldn't create Shama, her existence must be random, occasional, uncontrolled; doesn't this apply to everyone? We all harbor unspeakable sorrows, unique damages... We're all imperfect victims, also works completed by ourselves. Wonderful - this imperfection wasn't god intent; terrible - that perfection bears no god relationship either.

The Purist has taken your offering, slightly elevating you as compensation.

Title:

Imagined Friend

Text:

If everything came from god creation, why would gods create their opponents? Did the Cultic God create the Purist, or vice versa... Or were They once inseparable before diverging? Considering this, your throat suddenly tightens, as if something strangles you from behind... The Cultic God catches your overly presumptuous thoughts, displeased - Their displeasure typically demands death. You quickly restrain your thoughts, close your eyes, desperately seeking resolution... Of course - perhaps the Immaculate Purity didn't create this world, but this pompous deceiver misleads ignorant followers into believing so. When people praise "our pure creator," They neither deny nor confirm, stealing another's creation... Therefore, the world's true creator must be the Creation God - the Cultic God. Isn't this obvious? Has the Purist created anything besides wounds on priests' backs? When isn't the Cultic God creating? Consider Their otherworldly manifestations! How magnificent, Cultic God supreme! They tolerate another god borrowing Their reputation... But not much longer - the Purist's pretense will end, and you'll return to greatness under the Cultic God's command! The force choking your throat gradually relaxes - the Cultic God approves your solution. Sweating profusely, exhausted, you think... Experience serving the Sultan works equally well with these gods.

Title:

Imagined Enemy

Text:

If everything is godly created, what about god-slayers? Are they also god creations? Why would gods create such things? These thoughts barely form before cold sweat breaks along your spine... The all-knowing Purist catches your thoughts, clearly displeased... You close your eyes, gathering your thoughts, seeking resolution... Perhaps god-slayers exist to demonstrate true faith's value... They futilely attempt to shake the true god's position, repeatedly emphasizing its unshakeable nature. They drive believers to explore deeper doctrines, both for debate and defense - like clever mothers deliberately exposing children to minor illnesses, developing resistance against deadly plagues. The omniscient god intentionally creates non-believers, helping followers develop unwavering faith. With these thoughts, the pressure behind you gradually subsides... You sigh with relief, wiping sweat from your brow.
