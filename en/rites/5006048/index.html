Text:

Adila comes straight to you and Maggie, excitedly announcing that she has been lucky enough to discover the dragon's trail. According to The Book of Dragonslaying, dragons rarely leave footprints. These giant lizards are extremely cautious, knowing their tracks are too distinctive and easily recognized. As a result, they only rest on hard, unyielding rocks that leave no traces. But even the most careful plans have their flaws – they forget that the sulfurous fumes they breathe can also leave traces behind. "I found stones bearing these traces near a habitat that matches the descriptions," she says excitedly. "This must be the dragon's lair. All I need to do now is confirm that the creature is inside, and then I can prepare to strike…"
