Title:

Anchor with effigies in the mortal realm, transform in twilight's threshold, reflect all through celestial orbits

Text:

Earthly affairs mirror stellar movements, allowing us to locate hidden star spirits through stellar trajectories. After days of calculations, Purist monks chant a secret coordinate to their great god - not a star's position nor earthly location, but the crevice where the false Highlord wanders between realms. Just as They once helped the Sultan's ancestors overthrow the highland empire, the Immaculate Purity again raises holy flames against these failures. Night stars dim as if hiding from invisible sunlight; the North Star stubbornly flickers thrice before settling, permanently dimmed. The next day, all capital roses bow toward the Purist temple, ancient family descendants weep blood while kowtowing, and lightning obliterates a farmhouse - the lingering, decaying star spirit finally subdued by the only true god, the people-saving Immaculate Purity.
