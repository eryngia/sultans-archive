Title:

Forgotten Legacy

Text:

When you hand over the women's belongings, the powerful mercenary gazes at you with grateful, tear-filled eyes. "These belonged to Princess Mekana - see her personal seal? They say this beautiful, kind princess vanished when the palace fell... No one knew what became of her. It seems she at least escaped through some hidden passage." The nameless swordsman caresses these relics, sharing forgotten chapters of history: Perhaps the princess discarded all royal identifiers and fled quietly, the wandering spirits being handmaidens she silenced; perhaps trusted confidants betrayed her, dividing her possessions while their souls were cursed to wander; or perhaps, as legends whisper, the stars themselves spirited this peerless beauty from the mortal realm in her darkest hour... Your home's wine cellar empties as these misty tales unfold, and the Roaming Swordsman seems finally unburdened. Rising, he embraces you as a nobleman would, arranging to meet again tomorrow after tending to these royal remnants - he has something important to discuss...
