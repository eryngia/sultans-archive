You do not wish to risk facing them yourself. As you hesitate, twilight deepens—they arrive. The sounds halt the moment they touch the crystal bottles. You hear the popping of stoppers, the pouring of liquids, the sighs of satisfaction... then silence. Long after, you open the door.
