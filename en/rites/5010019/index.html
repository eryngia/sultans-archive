Result 1

Title:

Their Names

Text:

No one can intervene in the battle between you and the Sultan. It is a solitary clash between those burdened with the mandate of kings. The brave stand hesitant and unsure, the wise quake with dread, the compassionate turn their eyes away... and the righteous waver in their resolve. As if decreed by the heavens, the fate of the many rests in the hands of the few. Must those untouched by destiny merely look on in silence? While all can only endure and wait, Gulis slowly draws his longbow—the poisoned arrow, a gift from Nawfal, now taut against its string. Does the dormant noble blood in his veins grant him the right to this fight? Or has his fractured fate, broken time and again, carved out this moment of vengeance? We know only this: he murmurs the names etched into the arrow’s shaft—names of mortals insulted, wounded, and slaughtered by the Sultan. At first, the recitation flows gently, like a sacred prayer, then surges into a frenzied incantation: there are far too many names. The chanting stretches on, as though spanning a century, yet it might last only a fleeting moment. Gulis' eyes roll back, and blood trickles from his mouth and nose—he has reached his limit as a vessel and conduit of power. In the moment when the Sultan's sword overwhelms you, nothing remains between him and the arrowhead. In that instant, Gulis releases his hold, allowing the mighty bow to snap forward, his own skin tearing as the arrow—laden with the names of mortals—vanishes from sight. No eye witnesses its path, yet it finds its mark in the Sultan's mouth, piercing through his cruel smile and wicked soul—it slays the invincible Sultan.

Title:

The Fall of the Warrior King

Text:

You have no idea how long this battle has lasted, but your body and mind are near their limit—and your weapons are the first to give in. With a magnificent slash, the Sultan shatters your blade and right hand, then turns and, with a flurry of intricate swordplay, clangs relentlessly to pin down the Blade of Lament, leaving it unable to move, as though he were hammering an unruly nail into place. Just at the moment of impending defeat, [s5.name] suddenly abandons his blade, seemingly overwhelmed by the Sultan's power, allowing the precious artifact to fall to the ground, where it turns immediately to ashes. The Sultan's blade stabs into his flesh, but he seems prepared for this, using every muscle and bone to trap the fatal weapon – for just a moment, but enough for you to pierce the Sultan's back with the Sword of Lament. This weapon is forever bound to return to the hands of the ruler willing to wield it! This is the true secret of its unbroken heritage. Finally, [s5.name]'s wish comes true, and you, exhausted, no longer have any monarch to wield it... so the divine weapon fades into the air along with the life of the Warrior King once more...

Witness

Text:

When you accomplish all this, you look back... Beneath the steps of the throne, hardly anyone remains standing – neither victors nor vanquished, all exhausted by the battle. At this moment, a child suddenly dashes out from behind the throne – he is the one taken by the Sultan from the orphanage! He had always been by the Sultan's side... "Go, tell everyone, shout with your loudest voice as you run," you command. "Tell them everything you just saw!" The youth nods; he runs down from the throne, down the steps... across the remains, and into the Lapis Lazuli Hall... Thus, in the clear voice of this youth, in a place unknown to all, a great monarch and his dynasty meet their end.
