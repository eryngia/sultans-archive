Result 1

Title:

Fickle Fate

Text:

The vagrants claimed they came to assist you... They were willing to do anything! Many of these stateless people survived by taking odd jobs—this could even be considered a form of dignified begging. You noticed that many of them carried themselves with a certain poise; they were not used to begging, nor to wandering the streets. Their attempts to claim leftover food were tinged with shyness. You paused, and a thought crossed your mind... If Sultan stripped you of everything—as he so enjoys doing—would you too gather outside a noble's mansion, hoping for work to fill your stomach? The thought stirred your hand. You reached into your purse, gathering a large handful of gold coins. Your servants converted them into clothing, shoes, and food for the vagrants. Then you retreated into your residence, unable to watch further.

Result 2

Title:

A Meal

Text:

The vagrants claimed they came to assist you... They were willing to do anything! Many of these stateless people survived by taking odd jobs—this could even be considered a form of dignified begging. You could not bear to see them stumbling, gaunt, and exhausted. You instructed your servants to fetch flatbreads, dried meat, tea, and dates. The vagrants sat and enjoyed a proper meal. They were thrilled—they had been waiting for this! Still, they avoided showing themselves as beggars. They first gathered to thank you, then helped move items and tidied things, before finally diving into the food. They may soon be hungry again, but for now, you've helped them survive another day.
