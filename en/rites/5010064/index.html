Result 3

Title:

Dream of Their Death?

Text:

How many times have you killed those dark phantoms? How many comrades lie fallen beside you? You've lost count. Clouds, stars, wind, sun, moon, beasts, insects, mountains... all witness your sacrifice, weeping for each warrior's death. Is this the final lament before world's end? The most tragic finale the Creator arranged? They made everything, then waited to watch us destroy ourselves? In such despair, amid the Corrupter's - the world's ruler, the Chaos King's - laughter, you swing one desperate strike - piercing something crisp, hard, long hidden within Their countless rotting bodies... What is it? The mad creator's final conscience? The Creator's uncorrupted ink? Or from the beginning, a weakness Their master arranged - the inevitable vulnerability in creation's balance? You'll never know, for as that core shatters, this mad dream, this storm-eye twisting the world... and all darkness, all monsters, begin collapsing... Perhaps you truly saved the world, correcting your foolish mistakes.

Combat + Physique + Magic - Opposing(Combat + Magic + Physique) ≥ 9

Result 4

Title:

Overflow

Text:

In the shattered dream, in chaos's fall, you sense some distant divine gaze, even showing approval... Their creation has saved itself again - does this surprise Them? As the instigator, will you receive reward or punishment? Or was this part of Their arrangement all along? ....... Finally, after waiting centuries or mere moments: sounds of breaking arise, like countless Sultan Cards snapping simultaneously... Something bound to your soul has shattered, your granted achievements exceeding its capacity to measure or contain... You watch the sorceress dissolve with the Corrupter's remaining mud, see the Sultan's spirit dragged into darkness as the game's originator... while you become the final remnant of this runaway game.
