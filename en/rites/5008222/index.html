Result 1

Title:

Apologize Before the Storm

Text:

You beat Maggie to the punch with a sincere apology, explaining it was a desperate choice, a trick of that game, never your intent. You swore to the sky you'd never let it happen again. Maggie said nothing, only watched, then let out a long, weary sigh. "I don't want to say much," she murmured, exhaustion heavy in her voice. You leaned in with care, massaging her temples as she continued, "I just need you to know... that girl, your sister Arzuna... she's not as simple as she seems." Her words pulled memories to mind: Arzuna's flawless dancing, skillful charm, and the way she gazed at you—her "brother." "I'm not speaking out of jealousy, my dear. Her eyes on you... It's more than desire, certainly not mere kinship! Truth be told..." She hesitated, then let it out. "It feels like she hates you!" Maggie grabbed your arm, locking eyes with you. "Promise me, my husband. Promise me you'll have no more intimacy with her. Let me be blunt—don't sleep with her again. There's something dark in her heart! I can feel it!"
