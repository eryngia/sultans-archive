Text:

Bharat sneaked into the palace at night and knocked on Ansuya's window. So many years had passed, but when Ansuya opened the window, he still found himself back in Tyria's humble town—he had once knocked on her window just like that, pouring out his "trophies" onto her desk. What useless things they had been! Beautiful feathers, shiny stones, and sometimes even shabby wood carvings he had made himself... Ansuya would smile softly and gather those worthless little gifts into the most beautiful jewelry boxes. They had been so young back then. Now, so many years had gone by, and that warm smile had long since vanished from Ansuya's lips. She accepted the money Bharat brought and thanked him politely, as if she no longer remembered her childhood playmate. She had long since sorted those trinkets away—counting her own savings, planning how to exchange some for new, floating jewelry and fresh garments. The Sultan would surely notice her change. In his hubris, he would think that this woman—who has always held her head high in a defiant silence—has finally resigned herself to the master of her fate, broken by a searing lesson in humiliation.
