Title:

You offer financial support to Adila

Text:

When you take out the pouch of gold, Adila repeatedly refuses it. She has already decided not to marry anyone and to prove herself on her own. You tell Adila that you are just helping a friend. Whether she uses the money to slay a dragon or not is her decision and has nothing to do with you. Hearing this, Adila thanks you gratefully and sets off on her journey without looking back. A few days later, she returns, full of excitement, and comes to you first. She tells you that she managed to find traces of a dragon. According to the Book of DragonSlaying, dragons rarely leave footprints. These giant lizards are cautious, knowing their tracks are too distinctive and could easily be recognized. As a result, they only rest on hard, unyielding stone that won't retain any marks. But even the most careful plans have their flaws – they forget that the sulfurous fumes they breathe can also leave traces behind. "I found stones bearing these traces near a habitat that matches the descriptions," she says excitedly. "This must be the dragon's lair. All I need to do now is confirm that the creature is inside, and then I can prepare to strike…" She excitedly outlines her plans, too impatient to wait another moment.

Along the way, she takes on jobs as a caravan guard or hunting wild beasts. Though these tasks greatly slow her search, not once does she regret her choices, nor does she bow to fate.
