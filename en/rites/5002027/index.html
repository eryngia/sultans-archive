Text:

You check thrice through the peephole in the training room cubicle; if not for the famous wound on his left leg and the indispensable magical Signet Ring... without the crown, silver knife, gold ornaments, and lavish attire, naked with a hood on, it’s damn hard to tell... the man panting under Jalila’s foot is the usual highest Sultan. The Bloodshed Card burns hot in your chest. As you roar in vengeance, the naked Sultan reaches climax under Jalila’s strangulation. Suddenly, as death awakens the ring's power, a crimson flash engulfs all of you... the Sultan's thrill-induced shaking and moaning body is your last sight before death.
