Title:

Because of Your Past Help

Text:

There were no secrets in this gilded cage, and the news soon reached Ansuya. She summoned Arzuna to her chamber, spoke at length, and handed her a healing salve—cruel mischief was no stranger in the harem. Before long, rumors spread that Arzuna had earned Ansuya's favor. This concubine always lived quietly, under the radar, yet recently she had inexplicably won the Sultan's attention. No one dared provoke Ansuya, and naturally, they stayed away from Arzuna as well—any schemes now required greater finesse and craft. That evening, as Arzuna clutched the salve in stunned awe, Ansuya smiled faintly and said, "If you want to thank anyone, thank your brother. He's helped me plenty."

Title:

No Big Deal

Text:

The next day, the Sultan summoned his newly acquired woman. Arzuna, veiled in sheer red silk, nestled at his feet. With a smile, she recounted the clumsy trick played on her. "So?" Intrigued, the Sultan lifted her chin and squinted, as if to see through the veil the scar left by poisonous herbs. Arzuna smiled sweetly, letting him brush the veil aside. Beneath it lay a face decorated with blood—yes, blood—fresh and warm from the hearts of the vile slave girls plotting against her. Scarlet flowers bloomed on her skin, vivid enough to captivate the Sultan. By the night's end, she had truly become Concubine Arzuna.

Result 3

Title:

Storm Under Her Control

Text:

Arzuna had real skill. She discovered what the other slave girls had mixed into her skincare cream. Then, quietly, she smeared it onto the beds of the schemers—and all those who had turned a blind eye. The result was a spectacle: every low-ranking slave girl in the palace was scratching their skin raw, faces red and swollen. The Sultan, astonished, asked the eunuchs what had happened. Before they could answer, Arzuna crawled forward on her knees. "It was me, Your Majesty," she said. "They tried to ruin me, so I ruined them first." The Sultan liked that answer. He laughed and told Arzuna to lift her head, curious to see her face. Arzuna wouldn't, revealing the rash on her arm instead. Normally, such defiance might have angered the Sultan, but a furious slave girl nearby suddenly lunged at Arzuna, trying to strangle her. The two women rolled across the floor before the Sultan. When others tried to intervene, he waved them off, watching with fascination as Arzuna slammed her rival's head into the floor until the girl foamed at the mouth. "Send the physician to tend her face," the Sultan said before leaving. With that, Arzuna gained the right to the court physician's care. She had officially become Concubine Arzuna.

You filled a chest with remedies for everything—rashes, wounds, stomachaches, and colds—and sent it into the palace. Arzuna's face healed quickly; her restored beauty and alluring dances unsurprisingly won the Sultan's favor. Result 2

Title:

Stalling for Time

Text:

The moment Arzuna got the perfume, she understood your intention. She sprayed herself liberally, then sought out the eunuch arranging the Sultan's intimacies. Ten paces away, his face changed dramatically—torn between rushing forward and recoiling from the overwhelming scent. She strained to hold back a smile, and with a delicate, innocent air, told him that a few slave girls had caused her trouble. With a few coins pressed into his palm, she whispered tearful entreaties, all the while certain he wouldn't dare bring a stinking woman to the Sultan's bed. Heads would roll for that! The result? Arzuna missed her chance to serve the Sultan that day. But it didn't matter. As long as she remained in the harem, as long as she was cautious—or bold—she would have a second or third chance to appear before him, to captivate his eyes with her most radiant face and dances, even if only for an instant. Soon, she moved out of her tiny corner in the harem and lived along the stream that ran through the entire palace, with the court physician and eunuchs checking on her needs every night. Everyone addressed her respectfully as Concubine Arzuna. She never thanked you in person—it wouldn't do—but whenever you caught her distant figure in the corridor, she would give a quiet nod, her smile a secret shared between you.
