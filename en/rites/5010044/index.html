Title:

The Destroyer Surprise

Text:

Now that's a privilege! Bringing a cultist into the Purist's sanctuary! Badriyyah giggled, praising your wisdom while entering this sapphire-like pool... Let this mortal woman soil the nectar prepared for the gods! She removed her garments one by one; the fabrics quickly dissolved like ink in the azure pool... Then her tattoos began fading—you had never seen her like this, naked and clean. When her hair began falling out, she smilingly prevented anyone from approaching, mischievously pressing her lower abdomen... Urine and blood flew down her legs into the pool, followed by nails, teeth, peeling skin and organs—black organs. As her final skeleton collapsed and dissolved in the pool, the formerly sapphire-clear water had become pitch-black, churning with foul bubbles... You quickly locked the vault doors securely, which still bore murals depicting the Purist drinking from the Aether pool! Better make sure nobody discovers what's happened to it before that fated day arrives...
