Only because she is worth it

Text:

You prepare a grand ceremony to announce to everyone that Lumera is your adopted daughter. Over time, Lumera no longer shows any signs of her former thin and withered appearance as a beggar. Her graceful, wise eyes and composed demeanor – and, of course, your prominent influence – convince everyone. People congratulate her, young ladies affectionately link arms with her, and Lumera gracefully greets them, maintaining a calm expression showing no surprise or joy at her new status. You know her heart is probably still tied to the study.
