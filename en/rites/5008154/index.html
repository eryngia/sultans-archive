Result 5

Title:

Poison Courier

Text:

Under your interrogation, Fadia confesses. She quietly lies prostrate, exposing her long neck. Faris stands red-eyed with grief, rage, and helplessness. You opportunely place a Bloodshed Card before him: "This is just the beginning," you whisper. "Yes, just the beginning," he repeats in a deep voice, drawing his blade without hesitation. Avenging a hound, resolving to kill one's sovereign for a hound - is it absurd? He wipes blood and tears from his blade, looking to you with clenched fist: "I understand - you should too - this isn't just for Luna. For centuries... my family served this empire like dogs, tearing at whatever enemies each Sultan designated, but..." He pauses, shaking his head tiredly, as if further words were pointless. Finally, he meets your gaze, sheathing his sword: "So be it. When you need me... use me like a hound."
