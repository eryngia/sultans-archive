The Sultan's Suspicion

ID:

5006591

Text:

Did you think that you would only be killed if you couldn't complete the Sultan Card? Have to admit, the crazy game emboldens you a lot... but you're still not dulled to the Sultan's brewing malice. Especially after becoming Vizier... you get close to your lord, you feel the venom of maglignity surging and echoing like tsunamis within him, shackling his mood and soul. Sometimes, you can feel him restraining his urge to kill—such angers, such madness, was brewed not from ill-works of anyone real, but because in the ruler's imagination, it is the voice of the devil that runs most amok.
