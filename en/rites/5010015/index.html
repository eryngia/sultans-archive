Result 4

Title:

Punishment for Oathbreakers

Text:

Each honor guard pledges unbreakable oaths to the ring, symbolizing the monarch's power – to protect their sovereign with their lives, unwavering till death. This is why the Sultan's Signet Ring does not alarm in their presence, allowing them to wield weapons next to the Sultan on the battlefield. Now, the honor guards break their life-bound oaths, and the penalty for this sin shall be death. As their master's life hangs by a thread, the ring bursts forth nearly limitless power – a terrifying magic surge blocks you from approaching, and each Iron Guard weathers this red light, crumbling, burning. Their tears turn into golden flames instantly – not shed for this sacrifice, but for lost glory, and their dearly respected sovereign.
