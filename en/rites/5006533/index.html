Text:

Lumera's young voice echoes in the study, though it lacks the calmness she used when telling stories to the servants. This book slowly narrates the life of a young girl, her once-happiness, lost affection, fall, hope, and most importantly, her revenge... In the end, her wicked father's blood covered an entire wall, finally freeing her from the long-imposed shadow... After tremblingly finishing the last word, Lumera bowed her head and awkwardly asked to leave. She probably didn't expect you would ask her to read this book. As you watch her hurried exit, you can't help but smile. There's no need to ask who wrote this story and placed it on your windowsill. The answer is already clear.
