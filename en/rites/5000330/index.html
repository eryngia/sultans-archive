You introduced [xiaochou.name] to Jabal, who saw in [xiaochou.name] a rare noble—one who understood honor and conscience, willing to aid the old soldiers... "The best of them! The bravest! The kindest! All here..." Jabal gestured to the desolate graveyard. "Those who live are worse—weaker, crueler... Once, men paid to ease their suffering. Now... the good grow fewer." [xiaochou.name] returned, dismissing Jabal's fears as folly—"I've watched long enough. Human were always wretched, not ‘the good grow fewer’."

It will falter for your hesitation, your fear—and then you both die..."
