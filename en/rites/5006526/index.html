Result 1

Text:

You arrived at the agreed location and met with the brothel owner. During the meeting, he cautiously referred to Lumera's mother's death, insisting it was an accident and offering compensation. He was adamant that the compensation should go to the unfortunate daughter, not the greedy father. It seems he's well aware that Lumera is now under your protection. However, you sense deceit in his words... Lumera looks at you, seemingly sharing your suspicion.

Result 2

Title:

You caught the flaw in his words

Text:

To cover up, he started weaving new lies, which you exposed again. After a few back-and-forths, he was panting and sweating, finally confessing the truth. "That woman was no longer usable, rotting and stinking... But a customer wanted to see a murder, I, um... just helped her end her suffering sooner, she was beyond saving... This isn't my fault, sir, truly..." Lumera trembled behind you, and you ordered him to be quiet: "You deserve hell for your sins, but Lumera is kind. If you compensate her sufficiently – " Hearing 'money', the brothel owner visibly relaxed. He quickly took out a handful of Gold Coins, saying he could add more if needed. You counted ten coins. Lumera remained silent, at least not opposing your choice. Satisfied, you took the money and left with Lumera, bidding the brothel owner farewell.
