The Poem of the Emerald

Text:

On the tiny emerald, Jenna engraves a love poem, praising the nobleman's charms from head to toe. Her craftsmanship is exquisite, the fine script covering the gem's surface and making it seem larger than it truly is. The nobleman is delighted. He flaunts his emerald ring everywhere, visiting the noble baths thrice a day until many are forced to memorize the poem. Alongside the payment for the stone, Jenna shares part of the profit with you.
