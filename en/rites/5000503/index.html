Result 1

Title:

The sinner is dead

Text:

The tendrils of darkness drape themselves around the severed head, draining moisture and life from its skin and flesh. They wither away, leaving behind a pallid skull, its hollow sockets staring blankly into the void. Finally, the darkness is sated. Light and wind again flow through this room, once filled with blood and resentment. In a haze, you seem to see the girls curtsying to you, bowing their heads in gratitude, before disappearing into thin air, hand in hand. And you realize it is already sundown. You come to your senses, your chest warm, and the unnatural frigidity is gone from this mansion.

Result 5

Title:

Let the sinner bear the backlash of his own evil magic

Text:

Look, you have brought their most welcomed guest! Endless hatred rushes the old legal official. He screams and struggles, but he could not defy them. They take back what he has taken from them – the youthful body, the skin and flesh that do not age – the breath of life that do not belong to him. Before your very eyes, life drains from this man at an astounding speed. He becomes gaunt, withered, and weak, a pile of bones wrapped in loose skin. Even so, he lives. He opens his mouth, but could not make a sound. He moves his eyes, but could not focus his gaze. He flails his arms, but – like a worm – he could not support his body. His living soul is trapped in a dead body, unable to return to his earthly delight, and equally unable to plead his case with his false good deeds before God. Let him enjoy this inescapable immortality! This is the most vicious curse left for him by the girls who died under his evil spell to prolong his life.
