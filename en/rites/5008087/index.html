Result 1

Title:

Bring delicacies to show respect

Text:

You brought [s4.name]—the best dishes from Dark Alley's best cook! Even the Sultan himself rarely tastes such delicacies. Every one of these pups—caught in that awkward age between childhood and youth—eagerly shares the food. Each get only a small bite, yet not a single quarrel breaks out among them. As laughter fills the air, they made space for two cushions. Alim sat across from you, breaking a melon in half and offering you a piece. "This is my Dog den," he said, gesturing at the children around you. "I found each of them on the streets—starving, skeletal, barely clinging to life. I fed them lamb pilaf until they could stand on their own, and they worked to repay me." There's a weight to his voice, and you listened in silence. "I don't claim to be charitable; I think the poor can't afford to be charitable... After all, how can you help others when you can't help yourself? But I hope these little pups will live better lives than mine – like eating honest food, not stolen scraps." Under his command, the strongest of the children, nearly grown, knelt before you. "Take them," Alim said. "Help them do something great—something that can change the fate of these little dogs. Let them, and their children after them, rise to nobility!" Then, Alim knelt before you as well. He added that these children know every street and alley in the city—and they would prove invaluable in the battle to come.

Title:

Gold coins will warm their hearts

Text:

Alim smiles as he distributes the gold coins to all the children. Every group has a leader, and they are left with the money – just like animals disappearing before a disaster, Alim's little pups will also evade the imminent bloodshed. In the end, only the oldest, almost adult children remain... they knelt with Alim before you: "Take them," Alim says. "Help them do something great – something that can change the fate of these little dogs. Let them, and their children after them, rise to nobility!" Alim implores you, stating that even if the city turns into a burning hell, these children can help you find the right path.
