Result 3

Title:

Questioning the Chef?

Text:

Amidst the commotion, Habib emerges. With his towering height, he stands beside the troublemaker like a robust tower. "May I see this rat?" The man falters, nervously waving the rat before dropping it into Habib's hand. "Indeed, it is a rat. However, a rat's neck is fragile and would break apart if cooked for long. Given this rat's condition, it must have been placed in the soup just moments ago." As he speaks, Habib draws a sharp kitchen knife from his apron and calmly dissects the rodent before everyone, revealing internal organs still flushed with blood. His Marvelous Mush had been simmering all morning, as everyone had witnessed. The troublemaker is speechless. He awkwardly mutters something about a "misunderstanding" and tries to back away, but the crowd prevents his escape. Habib seizes him by the collar with an iron grip and drags him into the empty room next door.

Title:

Eat up! Why the hesitation?

Text:

"What? Where's a rat?" Alim rises languidly, squinting at the troublemaker. He walks over, takes the rat from the man's hand, and with a chuckle, stuffs the entire rodent into his mouth, producing spine-tingling chewing sounds. Everyone falls silent, including those standing beside him. Alim seems completely unaware of anything amiss. "What's wrong? Eat up! Haven't you had this before? It's a rare delicacy!" Well, yes, living in the Dark Alley, who hasn't eaten even mud when starving? Those days feel like another lifetime now. Thanks to Master [player.name]... The crowd quickly mumbles a few prayers before returning to their mush. As for the dumbfounded troublemaker, just as he tries to slip away, Alim grabs him firmly - he hasn't properly thanked this brother who provided him with an extra meat dish.
