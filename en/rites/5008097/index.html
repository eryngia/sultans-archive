Text:

[s2.name] talks until their lips are chapped, but the troublemaker remains unmoved. "I said, if you don't give me money, I won't tell you anything." His eyes are full of greed mixed with fear - yes, fear of death - he knows he's cornered, but what of it? He's a thug living day by day, not caring if he dies. His survival strategy is to bite hard when someone important like you needs something from him. "Or..." he suddenly smiles, with a hint of madness in his eyes: "Let me meet Habib's gentle and beautiful lady. I've never tasted a woman's flavor in my life. If she comes to see me, perhaps I'll be willing to talk?" ...How despicable...!

Troublemaking Rat

Result 2

Text:

This is likely the first time the troublemaker has met a refined lady like Maggie, and he seems quite awkward. Maggie has someone bring him a cushion and some refreshments. Rather than directly asking who sent him to cause trouble, she gently inquires about his circumstances. As they sit facing each other, the troublemaker doesn't know where to put his hands and feet, his arrogance completely vanished. He stammers a few responses, then gradually reveals everything he knows. It turns out the person who sent him to cause trouble at your guesthouse was a nobleman. From his description, you can identify who it is. After confessing everything, the troublemaker remains silent for a while, then suddenly leans forward while Maggie is lost in thought and kisses her cheek - He dares!! Enraged, you draw your sword to strike him down, but Maggie gently presses your hand. She looks at you, then at the troublemaker, shaking her head: "Let it go. He's just a child. He doesn't know what he wants... a woman, or a mother..." The man pauses at her words, then suddenly tears well up in his eyes. He tries to wipe them away unsuccessfully, finally breaking into loud sobs. Through Maggie's soft sigh, you notice that this thin youth has a childish face. At fourteen or fifteen, an age when noble children would still be carefree... You strictly order everyone in the room not to speak of what just happened, then leave with Maggie. A few days later, someone mentions that a rat from Dark Alley has died. No one bothers to tell Maggie, of course, and no one knows who did it - perhaps for betraying his master, who knows?
