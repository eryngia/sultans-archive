Result 12

Text:

Seizing his chance while the others were distracted, Nabhani caught up with Gullis and led him to the edge of the hunting grounds, where a cluster of noble ladies watches the hunt from afar. Then, with a dramatic flourish, he dived into the water alongside the hunter-slave to wrestle a crocodile barehanded. The fight was mostly Gullis's doing. Nabhani, clad in pure white silk, let the water soak him until every muscle showed beneath the fabric. He rose and dived again like some swan, or perhaps a duck, while the noblewomen swoon with delight. At last, they hauled the crocodile ashore—mostly thanks to Gullis's strength. The hunter slave rested on the bank, catching his breath. Watching Nabhani preen and pose, he suddenly spoke: "I've heard you are the finest swordsman in the Capital. Will you not cross blades with me? With so many beauties watching, surely they would love to see a duel." Nabhani turned toward him with the look of a man finding family at last. Of course, he had been waiting for such a stage.

Result 13

Title:

Faris' Hounds Lead the Charge

Text:

Faris, with his pack at his heels, pressed on into the forest alongside Gullis. They tracked the heavy prints of a great beast, deeper and deeper into the wilds. At last, they came upon a massive stone cavern at the edge of a cliff. Inside, piles of animal bones lay scattered, yet the master of the lair was nowhere to be found. Only a few particularly large animal bones remained, hinting at the creature that once dwelled there. Gullis cursed their ill luck, convinced that the noise of the hunt had driven the creature off. But Faris was unbothered. "Good," he said easily. "If it fled, it still lives. If it lives, it will sire cubs. And then we shall have more lions to hunt. A hunt is never spoiled simply because you do not strike the final blow." On their way back, they caught plenty of other game. The hounds were wild with joy, long denied such sport.
