She Smells So Good

Text:

When Jawad storms in front of you, he finds you holding his daughter's bridal veil – he is the only one in town who bought such extravagant and gaudy things... "Don't be upset, old pal Jawad, Miss Arumina has left us a token of affection." You say as you bring the shimmering silk to your nose... Already fuming, you further provoke him into such a rage that his wig pops off, distorting his face... In his fury, he can't even draw his knife and ends up ripping the scabbard from his belt, about to beat you senseless... Unfortunately, before you can escape, the anger brings him to clutch his chest painfully, convulsing and gasping, and finally falls dead from the rage.
