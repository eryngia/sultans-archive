A Miracle in Restoration

ID:

5006558

Text:

When you walk in goodness, you see the current of evil. You feel the evil thoughts and strands of depravity spread out by the cards in your hands, and every good deed you stick to tears apart those strands and the connection with this corrupt world – this both weakens the Sultan Card itself and the great evil pervading the whole world. Unfortunately, that bloody countdown still haunts you, threatens you, tempts you – to make you fall into injustice. You are so exhausted, having persisted until now... perhaps only God can give you some guidance.
