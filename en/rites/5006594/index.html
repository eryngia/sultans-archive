Text:

Your neighbor – a fool who always competes with you – beat his own wife's face just to warn her not to talk to someone like you, a dirty bastards who fornicates with women using sorcerous cards. Since the mud has already been flung at you, you decide to accept it. Alas, in a way that will be a little different than he imagined.

Results

Result 1

Text:

You will demonstrate to your neighbor with action how devoid he is in imagination. Once you are done, it shall be as clear as water from the river of Indus to him that having an obedient lover and a gentle, respected wife is a blissful thing – and that he shouldn't have treated her so abusively. Initially, the man yelled at you and resisted like a rabid dog, but soon, in agony, he lost the strength to struggle, lying on the ground silently shedding tears. Now he resembled the poor woman he's abused every day – this is what he deserved, you told him, you were the retribution for his evil deeds. While disciplining him, Maggie took the poor woman back to her place, treated her wounds, and spoke of many things to her. The next morning, the woman took her unconscious husband from among the mess. Her gaze was resolute, and when she looked at you, there was a hint of gratitude in her eyes. As you brushed past her, you vaguely spied some sort of protruding shape from her pocket, perhaps a dagger, perhaps something else indescribable... a shiver ran through you, as you turned back, Maggie was smiling faintly at you. After that incident spread, many men instinctively lowered their heads and averted their eyes when they saw you.
