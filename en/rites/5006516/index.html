Text:

First to arrive is Zaki. You silently agree to avoid discussing what is about to occur. The bath is very quiet, so quiet that the sound of flowing water covers a few ambiguous breaths. The young man’s beautiful face is soaked with the color of roses, and when he looks at you asking for a kiss, he appears like a clumsy but passionate puppy. In the heat of the moment, none of you noticed when the bath's curtain was gently drawn aside. During a brief pause, you suddenly glimpse a pale figure wrapped in black gauze. A noble lady steps barefoot into the disturbed water. The rose-scented steam makes your faces appear blurry, hiding your expressions. Fatuna slowly comes closer to you, you hear a soft sigh from her, then the black gauze slips off her body like a butterfly shedding its cocoon. She sheds something before coming to you, revealing her snow-white body, glowing faintly in the mist. “We shouldn’t be doing this,' she says firmly but kisses you nonetheless, 'We really shouldn’t be doing this.” What convinced her? Is it the protection your power can provide, the security from your life-saving act, or perhaps... no, her soft fingers wrapping around your desire now like a serpent.Strong and soft bodies cling to each inch of your skin, embracing the most instinctual part of your soul. There's no room left for thought or reason. This is the first, the last, and the most marvelous time.
