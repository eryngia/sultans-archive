Title:

Nabhani Charges Into the Flames

Text:

The place on fire was Habunah's residence; lavish and grand一but whether fabric tents or golden courts, it is all the same to the fire. There is no exceptions. It mercilessly engulfed large areas of nearby low-rise housing. People running out scantily-clad and wailing, unknown blood splattering across the cherubic faces of children. Mothers futilely protecting their kins with burnt limbs, men roaring as they struggled for how little the water they are supplied with, and dogs huddled together shivering in the mud. In the chaos, you calmly directed people to fight the fire, as everyone frantically rushed left and right, upward and down. The flames slowly died down. At this moment, Nabhani finally dashed out of the fire carrying Habunah on his back. His handsome face was smeared with soot, and much of his cherished long hair was scorched, lending him a dishelved look that blended him distraughtingly well into the background of this disasterous cacophony. Yet his eyes glowed with an unusual, furious fierceness, like a leopard – its rage an inferno a thousand time worse than the hell it just walked out of. "I'll take her back for care; it's impossible to recuperate here peacefully," he nodded to you and said nothing more. You saw him carrying the unconscious unfortunate woman, her proud, life-bearing hands burnt to a crisp...
