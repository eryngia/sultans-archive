After Results

Result 1

Title:

Zazie's Intel: Cursed Eunuch

Text:

Zazie's messenger brings a hastily written letter. You barely register surprise at her literacy before the contents shock you. Zazie reveals her orchestration of an old eunuch's execution - the very man who ritually cleansed her with icy water after the Sultan's visits, washing away the royal "essence." The aged eunuch performed this duty with sadistic pleasure, inflicting needless pain on the women. Seizing her chance, Zazie arranged for several of his victims to voice complaints to the Sultan. Playing her part masterfully, she feigned defense of the old servant while casually dropping a poisonous suggestion: "But my lord, old Baru was your wet nurse, was he not? I've heard tales of you at his breast..." Disgusted, the Sultan ordered Baru's execution that very night. Between her calculated revelations, she urges swift action, as the Highlord's nightmares grow more vicious with each passing night... Folding the letter, you find yourself reassessing the woman entirely. The court sees a mere gold-adorned plaything vying for royal favor, but you now glimpse something far more dangerous - a mind so sharp and ruthless it sends a chill down your spine.

Result 2

Title:

Court Intelligence

Text:

Your network of informants quickly yields results. A younger eunuch named Savasar has assumed old Baru's duties. This pale-skinned man seems a stark contrast to his predecessor - thoughtful and composed where Baru was venomous and cruel. The challenge lies not in finding him but in convincing such a measured individual to commit treason against the Sultan himself. After greasing a few more palms, you uncover a potential weakness: Savasar harbors an insatiable passion for literature.

Result 3

Title:

Palace Horrors

Text:

Court legends speak of the Sultan's unique disposal method for executed servants - no burial honors for them, but rather a one-way journey into the palace drainage channels. There, they say, lurks an ancient crocodile of monstrous proportions, grown fat on a steady diet of royal discards.

Result 4

Title:

Forbidden Touch

Text:

Of course - how could you forget? The whispered rumors say these cleansing eunuchs eventually lose their tongues, ensuring the Sultan's most intimate secrets die with them. This grim ritual isn't performed individually but rather in scheduled ceremonies where the royal executioner attends to multiple servants at once. If calculations serve you right, Savasar might still possess his voice for a while longer.
