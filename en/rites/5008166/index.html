Title:

Invitation Extended

Text:

She exudes potent exotic fragrance, possibly floral or medicinal. Seeing you, she smiles, "You possess those remarkable cards, don't you?" "Surely you wouldn't mind using them to help our magnificent master spread earthly influence?" You explain your limited knowledge of her master... "How truly unfortunate. My master is the only deity worthy of worship, whom you might call the Cultic God. He brings followers ultimate pleasure and unimaginable gifts. Unlike that self-righteous Purist, he never imposes supposed morality on others." Without awaiting response, she leans closer until her nose nearly touches your face. "I'll await you here." She provides a note with a suspicious address crudely written in red, likely paint. "Bring a Sultan Card; we'll complete your task without payment - all for our master's glory."

Result 2

Title:

Uncomfortable Truth

Text:

Maggie and Badriyyah converse for half an hour; her face bears an awkward yet polite smile when escorting her visitor out. Turning to you, she sighs deeply. "Cult Badriyyah wants your help elevating their influence. She believes your Sultan Cards enable anything... She suggested - or rather explicitly described - numerous so-called pleasures that merely sickened me. But I understand, my dear husband, that for these cursed cards, you might need her suggestions... This isn't your fault." You embrace her. Being understood and accepted - this indescribable warmth flows through you as Maggie sighs with relief, having apparently sought the opportunity to express these sentiments.
