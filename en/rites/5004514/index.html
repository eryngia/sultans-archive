Let Me Examine Again

Text:

You hand him your broken Extravagance Card. Iman traces its sharp edge, feeling unmistakable pain from its concentrated sinfulness, furrowing his brow. "Is something wrong?" you ask. He brushes aside his hair, again revealing his extensively scarred back - layered, accumulated, heart-wrenching. "Having received blessing, you understand that only in pain, only when experiencing nothing but pure pain, can priests hear the divine voice and convey pure words to supplicants." He speaks softly as his cloak resettles, white stone ornaments tinkling, "This ritual forms Purist faith's foundation - just a little blood, and our god manifests frequently, quite generous, yes?" "But your card... what does it mean?" he whispers, puzzled, nearly making you miss the faint mockery in his earlier words, "Why would miracles appear now?... What have you done?"
