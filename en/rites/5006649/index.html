In private, Nawfal couldn't hide his excitement. "As you can see, I've done as you said in my own territory. Now, the women have livestock to look after, milk and eggs to eat, and a future to contemplate – they stay, so their men and children stay, too." This way, there are fewer wandering vagrants coming to his porridge stall for charity. "I always knew!... I always knew you could do it." He grips your hand firmly, looking at you with solemn seriousness, "You can change this country." And you give a slight smile, telling him he's wrong, it's "us" – perhaps many, many of "us" – who can truly make the change here. He understands what you mean, you two need more comrades, more like-minded people, but it still requires time.
