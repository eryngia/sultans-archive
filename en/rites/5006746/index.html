Result 1

Title:

You called the Mercenary over.

Text:

With meat and wine, convincing him isn't difficult. This swordsman stomped onto your pristine, expensive carpet, mud on his boots, and plopped down carelessly, grabbing some jerky as he eyes Malkina. She asked him careful questions about combat attire, weapon placement, muscle training, and even the posture one takes when striking an enemy. At first, the Roaming Swordsman answered halfheartedly, but soon his interest was piqued. Setting down the food, he began demonstrating for Malkina. "Shoulders back when you present yourself—looks bigger... hands at the sides, only fools cross their arms, how else will you draw a blade? Chest armor is unnecessary; ribs protect the heart, but wrap the abdomen with a belt! Fish skin over beast skin. It's smooth, deflects blades. Watch this." He pulled out his weapon and spent hours exploring techniques with Malkina, who tidied up his messy hair and outfit as they chat. They enjoyed themselves immensely.
