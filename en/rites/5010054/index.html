Title:

Last Embrace

Text:

You accept Iman's proposal for this ultimate sacrifice - not merely preserving the world and atoning for your transgressions, but escaping this maddening game. As the priests commence their ritual, you experience no terror... The Immaculate Purity shall never suffer Their adversary's descent into the mortal sphere. They unleash the most savage, horrific, blazing fires - that absolute purifying radiance illuminates the entire metropolis. The egg sac contorts and shrieks... yet They cannot flee Their doom, for you remain spiritually bound to Them, and you welcome death gladly - flames commence destroying Them and you... Unexpectedly, Iman also steps willingly into the inferno as though completing the ceremony: he cradles you, sharing the torment of burning flesh as any priest should for their believer. You smile upon him, understanding profoundly: somewhere distant, where your ashes ultimately settle, you both shall discover tranquility.
