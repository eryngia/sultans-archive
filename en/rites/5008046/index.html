Text:

Miss Arumina comes to find you. She must have had a huge argument with her father, her face still carries anger. "I don't need my father to tell me which man I can or can't associate with!" she shouts at you, "I understand everything myself! Please don't get involved again; every time my father mentions something annoying, it's you!" After dropping this heap of meaningless threats, she glares at you fiercely, as if waiting for a reply. You carefully examine this pampered young lady, suddenly realizing that for her, her father's protection is her entire world, so a man like you whom even her father fears and avoids intrigues her... That's why she will take such a blatant risk to come, experiencing real storms in life, to her, danger just seems fun!
