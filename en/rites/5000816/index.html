Result 1

Title:

The Auction Begins

Text:

After repeatedly warning Riel against acting rashly, you escort her into the House of Delights. The auction has already begun, with Madame Buthayna making a show of walking out, holding a thick rope that binds four barbarian girls. They all share Riel's tattoos, white hair, defiant eyes, and fierce tendency to spit at the audience. "These are the purest barbarian maidens, freshly captured from outside the city," Buthayna stops before a tall girl, casually stroking her bare skin while the girl angrily shouts but cannot break free. "I know these aren't the goods our esteemed guests typically seek, but everyone gathered here today represents the House of Delights' most noble clients... clients with the finest taste. Strong men should embrace strong women - don't you want to conquer these firm breasts? Don't you want to try these sturdy thighs? Their taste is unlike any other, tighter, more exquisite. I guarantee every client who's experienced them comes back for more - but such pleasure isn't common, as barbarian women often commit suicide after being dishonored, so enjoy them while you can!" Riel trembles with rage, using all her strength not to forget your warnings and charge up to bash Buthayna's head in.

Breaking Point

Text:

You feel your pocket, which indeed contains a card, but the image on it is neither of charity nor of lovers. Riel can't help asking what it is, and you smile, saying it's a license to kill... "Since when does killing need a license? You city folk and your complications." True to their barbarian nature, these girls are stunned for barely half a second before helping each other remove their shackles and ropes.
