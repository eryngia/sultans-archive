Title:

The Poet in the Mud

Text:

The opening of the poetry scroll reads: "You recite in the trench of the river's source. The moon descends from the clouds, filling your chest." The meaning is obscure—it seems to hint at Lamblet, a higher presence, and some past connection with Hassan. As your fingers brush the page, a few lines appear where there had been blank space: "Yet your heart remains unfilled, empty, until even the stars join within." Hassan's fate might subtly shift because of this. Before you can touch the words again, thunder roars overhead. "Baa... alright, now off you go. And do forget everything you've just seen, baa!" A violent force surges into your mind. If you can withstand it, perhaps you'll keep your memory... and with it, a chance to alter destiny. You know all too well what it means to serve a higher will. They are fickle things, granting favor one moment, striking you down the next.
