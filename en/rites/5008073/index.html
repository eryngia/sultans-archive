Result 1

Title:

All You Can Eat Today

Text:

You buy up almost all the flatbread and grilled meat in the Dark Alley, quickly arranging for clean well water to be brought before anyone chokes. Some kind people even bring some porridge and cheese—all of which vanishes into the street kids' stomachs like a whirlwind. White-Belly used to be a force to reckon with in the Dark Alley. Many people come specifically to watch these disabled children eat—praising your generosity and kindness, though plenty mock your foolishness too. When the last street urchin eats until sprawled out on the ground, the crowd laughs and disperses. As you plan your next move, Alim discreetly slips a wad of money into your pocket. "I picked it from everyone," he winks at you, "consider it their tip for watching the show, right? Haha..." He puts a finger to his lips in a hushing gesture and disappears into the crowd.
