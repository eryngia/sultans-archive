Text:

You spent some money to give each impoverished mother in your territory a pair of chickens and a pair of goats, promising that if they raise them well, they can receive another pair next year. These mothers, with tattered clothes, often carrying infants on their backs and leading others by the hand, listened patiently and remembered carefully. They assigned tasks to their young children on who would care for the chicken coop and who would clean the goat pen. You also sent people to warn these women’s husbands that if they dared slaughter the livestock for meat or sell them for wine, they would be severely punished. You share this with Nawfal, who nods and remarks, "I believe they can do it." Perhaps. The droplets of water held in mothers' palm may someday gather into a river.
