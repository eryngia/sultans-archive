Title:

The Meaning of Freedom

Text:

The matter of Salwiyah has lingered in Nabhani's mind. Despite often encountering similar situations before: rescued girls soon becoming Lady of Delights again, returning to that miserable, exploited state. “But why is it like this?” he frowned, perplexed. You sighed, telling Nabhani that perhaps they simply don't understand what freedom truly means, or how to live freely once they have it. What work can they do, how can they support themselves, how can they protect themselves and their belongings? If our city does not provide these securities, so-called freedom is merely a castle in the air, vulnerable to collapse at any moment. Think, if a delicate, beautiful girl falls to the streets, kidnapped by villains... her fate might be worse than it is now. Nabhani pondered, mulling over this for a while before sighing. “It seems I haven’t done enough, far from enough...” He looked at you, clinked glasses without saying much, but from then on, he came to your door every day, no longer finding excuses to indulge himself frivolously.
