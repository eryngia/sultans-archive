Title:

Moth and You

Text:

You recall another persistent pursuit: remaining virtuous within the Sultan's game. No simple task. You face not just survival threats but misunderstanding, confusion, even hatred from many. Abandoning this path would be easy - simply using the card on someone more deserving... Yet you avoid this when possible, though no one requires it. This parallels your magical explorations - perhaps everything you seek represents one thing - Maintaining "selfhood" amid turmoil. This "self" needs no clear definition. Simply altering fate slightly through free will confirms you remain alive. Night deepens, moth-catching lamps illuminate your estate. Watching those delicate-winged creatures repeatedly crashing into flames, you recognize yourself among them. You harbor no regrets. Living inauthentically seems worse than glorious death in flames.

Title:

Soul's Reshaping

Text:

You had thought of those Ladies of Delights, finding yourself time and again unable to resist seeking them out. Were you fascinated by them themselves—or by how they had accommodated you? Perhaps "obsession" had always derived from the joy of shaping others. You had molded them to appear infatuated with you, genuine or otherwise—and that illusion, though false, had proved irresistible. On closer thought, hadn't all intoxicating things worked the same way? Alcohol had shaped drunks into self-imagined men of importance; swords had turned warriors into life-and-death arbiters. The Sultan had reshaped courtiers into toys and slaves. Watching another human soul bend through your own actions—nothing had ever enticed you more. You had shivered slightly then, not from cold, but from the dark, approving laughter that rippled through the void. "Precisely," They said. "I appreciate your insight." ...Have you ever heard Their whispers so clearly before?

Title:

Desire's Blame

Text:

You think of Nayla. She is a creature of pure appetite—craving the finest fabrics, the brightest jewels, and the most exotic spices. She demands to be the envy of all, standing above the rest, wanting everything and then some! And yet, the world remains under her spell. Her greed is her greatest charm; shaped by a constant stream of tribute from men and indulgence from women, she has become a force of nature. But what is the root of this infatuation? Reflecting on your moments with her, you find the answer: desire. Nayla's allure is born from her sheer, pulsing hunger. It's the spark of survival—the urge to feast, to drink, to embrace. The more she wants, the more blindingly alive she appears, a trap for every shallow soul with a weak will. But right then, an icy thought strikes you, sending a shiver down your spine. "Indeed. Desire is the ultimate filth—vile, hateful, and begging to be purged," They say. "Follow me and purify your heart of all desire." ...Have you ever heard Their whispers so clearly before?

Happiness Overflows

You recall moments bringing happiness—that incomparable sensation surpassing any sensory pleasure or material delight. Only while happy can you enjoy life's sweetness without guilt; only while happy can you proudly continue existing; only while happy can your soul's reservoir store additional water against inevitable future droughts. Perhaps your magical fascination stems from happiness found in wielding power, which protects what brings you happiness. Life represents a one-way journey seeking happiness. You regret nothing. But wait... a paradox emerges. You concentrate intensely... god whispers and visions never brought pure "happiness." Do They scorn human fascinations, or... cannot achieve them? You gain deeper understanding of existence.

Title:

Boiling Cauldron

Text:

You rub the ink stain on your palm, summoning Them to answer your inner question. They respond immediately, as if eternally awaiting your call. They show you an enormous boiling cauldron... massive beyond comprehension, filled with thick black broth, stirred by a she-demon with two pairs of breasts. Hands lift you up. You kick and flail uselessly, held like a grasshopper between a child's fingers - then thrown into the cauldron! ...Remarkably, nothing burns. No, that's incorrect - it isn't cool, but your magic protects you. Watching other victims scream agonizingly in the boiling broth, the she-demon repeatedly submerging their briefly emergent heads with her ladle. They swallow filthy broth, intestines cooking from within - but you? Your magic forms a beautiful protective shield. The demon's ladle avoids you, broth cannot touch you, even heat remains distant. You understand Their message. Your magical fascination exists because it allows you to safely navigate the Sultan's game, witnessing others' suffering while remaining unscathed. How captivating. ... You awaken refreshed, slightly proud. Perhaps next time, ask whether the Cultic God might grant you this she-demon. Her appearance wasn't unpleasant...
