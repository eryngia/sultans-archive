You lift the golden Conquest card, letting its reflected light merge with the rising sun. The troops become restless, seeing the pinpoint reflection grow from the mountain peak... until it turns into a God's spear aimed at the Sultan's capital walls still shrouded in darkness. The Sultan's troops see this gleam, more dazzling than dawn, causing dizziness and madness – the signal to begin the assault. [s15.name] urgently blows the horn as they ride furiously down the slope, while you toss the glowing card in your hand towards the flag [s16.name] holds high. The moment the card meets the flag, it transforms into burning gold, dancing in the wind, emitting a terrifying radiance as your main force begins to march towards the Sultan's walls.
