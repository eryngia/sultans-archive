As the ceremony nears its end, Maggie pulls Lumera aside, as though preparing to share a few private words. After speaking to her alone for a moment, she also calls Fardak over. Lowering her voice, she tells them this is the perfect time to use "returning home for the wedding" as an excuse to escape this city.
