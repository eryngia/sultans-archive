Text:

Led by Raed, the vagrants surrounded your house again, banging rusted gongs and broken boards, shouting songs both wild and senseless. The stench of rain-soaked misery clung to them... "Lord [player.name], our needs are few—just a roof against the rain, and walls against the wind. Help us, and we shall surely help you. We have our skills, don't we, brothers? We have the skills!" Their cries rose like a storm, shaking the rooftop of your residence. Whether it is praise or threat was hard to say.

At the city's edge stood many manors of the old nobility, abandoned since their heads rolled beside their king's. Each manor had its claimants, yet few dared live within walls stained by blood, and fewer still could pay the price. You placed a weighty purse into the hands of a trusted servant, sending him to find the vagrants a dwelling broad and solid enough, one of those relics of the old dynasty: cheap, sound, and fit for many lives. In time, Raed returned with her most capable followers to thank you. You saw it clearly then: these were not mere beggars, but people of talent, once educated, now displaced.
