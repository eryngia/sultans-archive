Title:

Arzuna's Opinion Matters

Text:

You relayed the noble's invitation to Arzuna word for word, asking if she would accept. She raised an eyebrow. "I thought you'd accept for me. After all, everyone sees me as your dancer, your slave girl." She spoke as if it mattered little, toying with the bangles on her wrist. "I love dancing, but... hmm... I worry I might tarnish your reputation. What do you think—would I?" Without hesitation, you told her that having a passion was a rare gift. If she liked to dance for others, why hold back? And your reputation? It should mean the least to her. Nothing should restrict her freedom to follow her heart. Arzuna paused, stunned, before bowing deeply. "I will be there, my brother. And I will not bring you shame." She had never seemed so earnest.

Title:

The Mistress's Favor

Text:

Maggie, already invited to the banquet, couldn't suppress a smile as she watched you fumble through your hesitant request. "Alright, I understand. I'll take care of Arzuna." And so, Arzuna went, dazed, linked arm in arm with Maggie into the noble's estate. Under [s3.name]'s hands, she stiffly applied the newly bought rouge... Being waited on didn't suit her one bit! She had always prepared herself alone. The noble was stunned, nervously probing her identity, too wary to show the slightest disdain. After all, what slave girl could have the company of a mistress? It was a flawless performance. Beyond the applause, there was no disruptive noise, intrusive gazes, or malicious teasing—none of the petty cruelties she had assumed she'd endure forever. Arzuna had never known such pure admiration, and it was all because of Maggie and you. She held back tears as she took her bow, later raising a glass to Maggie with sincere gratitude.

Entrusting Arzuna to [s2.name]

Text:

At the noble's estate, Arzuna delivered a flawless dance. When the music ended, whispers rippled through the crowd, speculating about the beautiful dancer—look at her, in her expensive silks and vivid rouge, even attended by a servant. Heaven, how favored she must be! Was she your new slave girl? Or some woman you had taken a fancy to? "That's enough." Amid the chatter, [s2.name] set down her glass with a cold, decisive weight. "If you wish to provoke Lord [player.name], go ahead. Let's guess how many more cards of Bloodshed or Conquest remain in the deck." As if to memorize them all, she scanned every man present. They shrank under her gaze, none daring to meet her eyes. She snorted lightly, then stepped onto the stage and took Arzuna's hand. "Let's go." Sheltered at her side, Arzuna lowered her head and nodded softly, concealing the tender flicker of surprise in her heart.
