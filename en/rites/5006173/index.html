Result 1

Title:

The Queen Descends

Text:

When the Queen's flower blooms at the crossroads, you are ready. The jinn rasps a word, a syllable that cannot exist in the mortal world. In answer, the flower unfurls with impossible speed. And with it come strange music, drifting lights, strange scents... and thoughts, elegant thoughts, flooding your mind. You reel, overwhelmed, while the jinn kneels before the bloom. Bud, blossom, harvest... Flower to fruit in a heartbeat. The fruit's skin bristles with thorns. Its calyx hangs heavy. Then, as if exploding, the ripe fruit splits open, flesh crimson and raw, and within, the Queen of Jinn is revealed. "Who? Who dared to..." The Queen's voice is a harp, a song. Her body fits in a perfume bottle. She floats in the air, borne on the backs of two winged attendants, and gazes down at you with a haughty stare. "What is this? This sticky touch... revolting. You dared summon me to the material world with these lowly creatures? Then die. My wish is this: that you suffer a thousand and a hundred and eleven deaths, each in agony." At last, those eyeless eyes turn to you. Mortals. Are you ready to receive the Queen?

Result 2

Title:

The Queen's Wish

Text:

Before defiance can harden into words, the world itself shifts... literally. You stand in a forest. Fireflies drift past. Mushrooms nudge up through the soil. The scent of grass and tuberose surrounds you. "Did you think a queen could only rule from her throne, flesh-bound mortal?" The voice comes from above. You look up. The Queen towers over you now, vast as a mountain, gazing down. "Even here, in this wretched place weighted by flesh, I can crush you effortlessly. And then I will draw out that rebellious mind of yours and stitch it into my bedcurtains. Each night, before I sleep, you will sing me a song of despair." The others from the summoning ritual fling magic wildly in all directions. But the Queen's will has already carried them to another world. A world shaped entirely by her wish.

Title:

The Joys of the Material Realm

Text:

Of course you prepared a gift for the Queen. Only a fool trusts jinns and picks a fight with a powerful being from beyond. You kneel, arms raised, offering it up. The Queen taps the servant beneath her foot, just lightly, with her toe. The servant darts forward at once and accepts your tribute. The moment the tiny creature touches your gift, it begins to shrink... until its size fits the Queen perfectly. "This is a mortal world thing? This... sinking feeling in my hand... What is this feeling? Ah. Weight. Is this what they call weight?" The Queen turns your gift over in her hands as her gaze slides across your face. She's playing with you, weighing you. It feels like a thousand arms pressing against you, like hips and breasts the size of mountains crushing in... You're about to break. Fall apart. Suffocate. Then the Queen looks away. "Good. I'm pleased. An amusing trinket. I could draw out the thoughts within and place them in my museum... Yes. You are forgiven, mortals who summoned me."
