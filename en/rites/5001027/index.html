Pay for atonement

ID:

5001027

Text:

he Sultan declares that you can save your own head with its weight in gold. For this matter, five craftsmen and sixteen scholars are summoned before the Sultan. They argue endlessly, using ropes, basins, massive livestock scales, and gallows hooks to measure you for an entire day and night. They even kill two slaves of your height in the process… but eventually arrive at a few conclusions that aren't too far apart. To prove to the great Sultan that their respective methods are the correct ones, they prepare detailed illustrated boards and diagrams. However, the Sultan simply chooses the heaviest result.
