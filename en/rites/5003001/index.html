Text:

You notice that Maggie no longer waits for you to have breakfast together in the mornings and only responds with cold silence or brief, dismissive answers when you speak. Despite living under the same roof, you find that you meet less and less, as if you are no longer companions of fate riding the same camel. She is still as gentle and calm as usual, but you always feel an undercurrent in her deliberate avoidance of your gaze... could this be an illusion?
