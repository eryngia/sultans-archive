Title:

Blood-Veined Marble

Text:

Workers secure ropes as one-third of the capital's oxen and horses assemble - the rest were rejected for impure coloring... replaced by purebred faithful. The stench and dust from these beasts obscures heaven... As the sun reaches zenith, at this brightest moment, you order work to begin. The earth trembles, prayers and animal bellows harmonize as the two heaviest pillars in imperial history slowly rise... The true challenge begins: can the foundation bear this tremendous weight? How can the pillars avoid tilting or collapsing? As religious leader, you fearlessly approach the still-swaying columns with your most devout followers to pray... Red substance flows down the pristine white marble, rooting into the ground like veins, miraculously stabilizing the structure. This is believers' blood! The foundation of faith! You shout, commanding everyone to praise this miracle - though you know well this is murder committed in God's stead. To enter this world, God willingly harms innocents.

Indulgence

Text:

Engineers warn these massive pillars exceed any foundation's support limit - they'll tilt by sunset and collapse before moonrise, destroying everything beneath. You know they speak truth, but miracles require not engineering and stone, but sacrifice and devotion... As religious leader, you risk praying between the pillars as they fall, their thunderous impact swallowing you in dust - you hear your god's frustrated revelation: blood sacrifice alone isn't enough; you need the Sultan Card - to enter this world, God requires the Sultan Card to absolve the sin of harming life!
