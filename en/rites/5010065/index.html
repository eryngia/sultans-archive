"You know this, human? The gods can never catch a dragon—They are not strong enough in the real world, and Their servants are not clever enough!" At this, the sheep bleated triumphantly. Before you could process the terrifying things it had revealed, everything snapped back to normal, and Hassan was drinking your finest bottle of wine. You shook your head, chasing away the fragmented thoughts, yet felt your spirit stirred by... some kind of encouragement, real or imagined? You burned with renewed resolve—yes, what are you waiting for? Find someone to handle this Dragon's Eye at once!
