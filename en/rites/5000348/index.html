Text:

[xiaochou.name] was gone. Not only that, when you asked the servants if [xiaochou.name] had visited today, they stared at you in bewilderment. "Who?" they asked. You understood then—only you remembered that name, only you recalled the visitor from the mirror. Ah, yes—the mirror! You rushed to the dusty storeroom. Where the mirror once stood, now lay an object wrapped tightly in eastern silk. A lute, its surface shimmering like polished glass. When plucked, its notes flowed like mountain springs, slipping effortlessly into every heart... On its back, tiny characters were carved: “Each heart hears their own joy, plays their own unique melodic ploy." This, perhaps, was [xiaochou.name]'s parting gift. Such an extraordinary being was never meant to linger long in your world. Maybe it sought a more comfortable place to rest, or perhaps it had grown weary of unraveling human tales. Yet those who hold this instrument will forever remember the poet called [xiaochou.name].
