Result 7

Title:

Temple Desecration

Text:

Following a hastily assembled yet remarkably effective cult ritual, cultists hiding behind the Purist temple completed their preparations. Nearby, followers crossed crystal-clear streams flowing over pristine white stones, discussing scripture with priests—a scene repeated countless times before. Suddenly, innumerable locusts darkened the sky! They swarmed frantically, forming a massive, writhing human silhouette suspended in mid-air... The priests and followers stared upward in horror as the locust-figure crouched, positioning its posterior directly above them and then... A cascade of excrement descended with perfect timing as the locust swarm exploded amid your thunderous laughter... The entire Purist temple became defiled beyond recognition, with people covered in filth. You remained protected behind a column, relatively clean. The scene proved so traumatic that several witnesses immediately renounced the Purist, turning instead to the Cultic Gods... After all, if a deity could not prevent such humiliation in their own sanctuary, how could they possibly protect their followers?
