Mysterious Assistance

ID:

5006557

Text:

A tiring day is about to end, as you walk through the shadows of Sultan's palace walls, ready to return home, a woman in white blocks your way. The snow-white linen covers her face, while her bony hands and flowing silver hair appear even paler, piercing your eyes in the dark. "Do you need help?" she asks with a slight appreciation: "This is what you deserve." – Whether she is camouflaging as a mortal to hide her identity or an emissary of sacred power, wouldn't you raise a request?
