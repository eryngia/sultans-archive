Text:

When Badriyyah's long needle approached your palm, a tremor ran through her. A massive, indescribable black pillar hovered above you two, as if the entire earth had been inverted and forged into a shimmering obsidian. Carrying the unconscious Badriyyah in your arms, you rose undaunted and strode forward with eyes shut. Those thieves of destiny deserved your despise. Counting to ten, you opened your eyes. The chaotic, free world of mortals came back into view.
