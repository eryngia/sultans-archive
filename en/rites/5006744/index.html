Title:

You called Fatuna over.

Text:

At first, Fatuna was reluctant to join a "study of evil," but as the conversation unfolded, she softened. Malkina praised her smooth skin and elegant posture, and Fatuna laughed, saying it was the fruit of long discipline. She shared skincare secrets, traded eye-makeup tricks—and then, just when you thought their meeting would end on a light note, Fatuna suddenly clasped Malkina's hand. "I am not guilty. But that does not matter to you. Guilt is not eyeshadow, nor the powder on one's skin. Guilt cannot be seen. If you believe yourself guilty, then you are. If you believe you are without guilt, then you are blameless." Malkina returned her grasp. The two women gazed at each other in silence for a long time.

After Results

Result 1

Title:

A Conclusion on Evil

Text:

From these meetings, Malkina learned much about "evil." She sketched face after face onto sheets of mica, then held them up proudly. "Villains should have narrower faces, deeper-set eyes, hollowed cheeks. Their colors lean toward blue, green, even violet." With swift strokes, she altered a bland, forgettable visage until it radiated menace. "See? Now they look crueler, wealthier, more powerful—less to be trifled with!" Malkina laughed softly. "Thanks to you, I've truly learned a great deal." Then, with brushes and powders, she turned her attention to you. A touch here, a shadow there... Soon, you looked like a villain yourself. And really—what's so wrong with that?
