Results

Result 1

Text:

You met Mahir, but behind her was a silent, veiled 'person' on a leash. This 'person' had metal stitching, preservatives, and countless copper pipes placeed into its flesh, as well as an exoskeleton of steel, making it more massive than the strongest soldier. “How do you like it? Isn't it great!” Mahir demonstrated by stimulating specific copper pipes to control its movements: “After ether is imbued, nerves and muscles can still remember simple activities, forming reflex actions, meaning it can perform many simple tasks...” Suddenly, a copper pipe sparked and emitted a foul, black smoke, and the gigantic humanoid lunged at you...

You pry open the hand of the work... as that copper pipe was utterly burned, it returns to cold, like an artisan repeatedly mimicking the action of strangling air. But in any case, it's a success, we've animated a corpse!" Mahir rejoices in claiming credit from you, and if ignoring the bruise on your neck, this would indeed seem intriguing...
