You fold your arms to watch, but find yourself straightening up almost instantly. There's good reason – something about the pugilist's form seems strangely familiar...

Result 5

Title:

The pugilist doesn't discover you.

Text:

He passes through the dirty, muddy slums and slips past a few bustling brothels, but the women fail to slow him down. You keep following him, step by step, until he reaches the edge of the noble district... To avoid drawing attention, perhaps, he has his carriage waiting for him nearby. The carriage, far too luxurious for the dark alley, would have caused a sensation if seen there. Then you see him remove the mask from his face and accept a silk robe handed to him by a servant. Hidden in the shadows, you watch as the Captain of the Sultan's Iron Guard, the empire's general, the illustrious Lord Jabal, steps into the carriage. As the carriage rolls away, you pick up the mask he has left behind. A perfect leverage, something worth exploiting... you realize this instantly.
