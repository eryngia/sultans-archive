After Results

Result 1

Title:

Gullis Won

Text:

Gullis held back just enough, stopping the tip of his blade an inch from Nabhani's chest without so much as nicking the Capital's finest swordsman. Nabhani paused for a moment, then burst into laughter. "Look, look!" he exclaimed, grabbing Gullis by the arm and parading the slave before all the noblewomen watching the fight. "Here is the fiercest, most skilled, and handsome warrior! Can you believe you didn't recognize him until now, my lovely ladies!" The women laughed and bombarded Gullis with questions—how old was he, where had he learned to fight, had he served a mistress before? Flustered, Gullis hurriedly dragged the crocodile he had claimed as a trophy away, cheeks burning red. Nabhani followed leisurely, chatting with the ladies as they walked. The Grand Hunt was a resounding success, and tales of it lingered long in noble circles.

Result 2

Title:

Nabhani Won

Text:

Nabhani had captured the grandest prize of the entire Grand Hunt—all the hearts of the ladies. Noblewomen crowded around, asking if he had been hurt. Gullis was pushed to the side, while Nabhani laughed, dragging the slave back beside him. He clasped Gullis's hand and announced to all the ladies, "See here, another brave and handsome warrior worthy of your attention!" Gullis, cheeks burning, trailed behind Nabhani through the swirl of powdered skirts and fluttering fans as they hauled the crocodile together, Gullis carrying the heavier share as usual. It was, without a doubt, the most exhilarating hunt they had ever known.
