Result 1

Title:

Sultan gave the beggar 5 Gold Coins

Text:

Everyone praised the Sultan for this show of benevolence. Someone said 5 Gold Coins were enough to feed a family for a week, only to be immediately ridiculed by another who claimed a poor man could never earn 5 Gold Coins in a year. Someone suggested he could buy a head of livestock and live off it; others argued that he could invest the money in a small business, growing his fortune... While they were imagining the offspring of the nameless beggar would repay the Sultan tenfold, a hundredfold, and even more, the beggar had his throat slashed in a dark alley in the lower town. His fingers were mangled because he held onto those coins so tight. Those coins were supposed to change his fate...
