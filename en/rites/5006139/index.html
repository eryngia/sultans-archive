A Bloody Diamond Pit

ID:

5006139

Text:

This is a secret diamond mine unknown to the Sultan. The miners are gaunt, hollow-eyed, their strength sapped. Overseers crack whips without mercy, trampling those who collapse until they no longer cry out. It is plain to see: these people are expendable. To keep the mine's location secret, the workforce is meant to be consumed and then silenced. Buying slaves would be cheaper than these losses. Most of these wretches were seized from the Dark Alley by the mine's owners.
