This day you talk about many things. Qais tells you, his mother eventually passed away. After finishing his mother's funeral, he thinks repeatedly and finally comes to bid farewell to you. "Perhaps, I should search for something truly meaningful... I should think clearly about what I truly live for... And clearly, the answer doesn't lie within this city." Before leaving, he gives you an Ancient Ring, thanking you for your care and help during this time – it might be the only treasure left in that impoverished home.
