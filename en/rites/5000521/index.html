Title:

Tonight, you claim Consort Ansuya

Text:

You have claimed the coveted Ansuya, the most unique jewel of the Sultan's collection. You toyed with her like toying with a broken statuette: its grace, dignity, compassion, resolve and divinity snuffed out alongside the candlelight. You write your victory with the tears she finally shed – no longer caring if you ever got close to that bitter heart. Later, you hear that Consort Ansuya's palace caught fire one night. The fire raged until dawn, reducing everything to ash. In the flames, the night wind carried a handful of pure white sand away into the vast, unsullied sky.

Result 2

Title:

You entered the consort's body, and her mind

Text:

In between kisses, you exchange cold and hard convictions. You beg her to help you. "You want to kill the Sultan?" Dark fire burns in Ansuya's eyes. She holds your face in scrutiny, determining whether you truly are companions on the same dark path. "What do you want me to do?" You reveal an unformed plan in hushed tones. You tell her that you need her to help you steal the Sultan's ring. It is a powerful magical artifact that has foiled foolproof assassinations and erased tribes from the surface of the earth. Your only hope of success depends on the ring being removed from the Sultan. And except in a moment of true intimacy, the Sultan will never take it off. A ray of hope lights up Ansuya's gray eyes. She reaches the summit in your description of regicide. "I will help you." She promises in the afterglow, steadying herself to sit up straight. "After I return to the palace, I will try to win the Sultan's favor and look for a good opportunity. However, I am afraid I will need your support." She gives you a token and tells you to seek her in the harem some time later. It is a string of old bells woven together with colorful ropes. You shake them gently and find that they are all mute, making no sound.

Result 4

Title:

You conspired with the consort

Text:

You take Ansuya to a quiet room and dismiss everyone. "Please forgive me for the way I arranged for our private meeting," you apologized to Ansuya, "but only you can help me with this matter." You need her to help you steal the Sultan's ring, which is a powerful magical artefact. It has helped the Sultan resist all assassination attempts. It has erased a tribe from the surface of the earth in an instant. Except for in moments of true intimacy, the Sultan will never take it off. And only by taking it from him does your regicide plot have any hope of success. This ray of hope lights up Ansuya's eyes. Through the hard frost, you can almost touch the humiliation, recalcitrance, and hatred she has suppressed in the deepest part of her soul. "I will help you," she looks at you and promises, with the solemnity of swearing an oath, "Upon my return to the palace, I shall endeavor to regain the Sultan’s favor and await the right moment to act. However, I must confess – I will require your support." She hands you a token of trust and asks you to find her in the harem after some time has passed. It is a string of old bells woven together with colorful threads. You shake them gently and find that they are mute and make no sound.
