Even the painter—famous for his quick hand—barely finishes the portrait before Lumera closes her book. His final stroke adds a glint to the diamond resting on her brow. It is an excellent painting. The copies are sent throughout the empire, carrying Jenna's name to every corner of the realm. There is no price listed beside the diamonds—after all, those who can afford such stones would find numbers insulting.
