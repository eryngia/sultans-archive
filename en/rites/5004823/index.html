Title:

Light's Connection

Text:

You keep thinking. Gradually, you seem to see a golden thread appear between your forehead—why you can see your forehead is another topic since you're not using your biological eyes now but your Insightful eyes—you reach out to grab it. You pull on this golden thread, as it leaves your skin, it induces waves of peculiar sensations... perhaps after a while, a heap like a golden mist gathers around you. One end remains buried in your body, while the other pulls towards the sky, its endpoint unseen. An epiphany strikes you. The "Divine Light" surrounding those holy priests is likely akin to this golden thread. It connects you to the Gods, and if your thoughts aren't pure or virtuous enough, it can easily be broken; while if it's extensive enough, you can always be found by the Gods... The thread trembles, your soul ripples as a result, it appears the Gods acknowledge your found answer.

Shadow's Reach

Text:

You continue contemplating. Gradually, your shadow beneath dissolves, rippling outward like someone cast a stone into your soul's waters. The shadow flows, covering floors, climbing walls, coating ceilings. It gradually envelops you, surrounding like a massive mother goddess embracing you with abundant breasts... This inexplicable feeling overtakes you. Rippling, flowing, rippling. Trembling, shaking, swaying. Pungent scents, strange growls, satisfying blood-warmth. Revelation strikes. The cultists' herb juices must share origins with this shadow. By tattooing marks on skin, impure gods tag souls, entering them into their registers. They flip this ancient, heavy book, observing thoughts on each page. If you haven't fallen deeply enough, if your soul lacks sufficient weight, your shadow remains too faint... gods cannot find you, though you're uncertain whether this benefits or harms you. The shadow invades your orifices, piercing every opening... This god dislikes your questioning, seeking only amusement.

Thought's Messenger

Text:

You think of Zephyr. This slave carries messages across the city in minutes, delivering and returning swiftly. God thoughts must utilize similar transmission mechanisms, with you playing Zephyr's role. They project thoughts onto you, requiring you to spread them through the world... You're chosen as messenger, explaining why you repeatedly hear these voices, smell these scents, experience these visions... But what benefit does this offer you? However important Zephyr's messages, to him they represent mere labor. However sacred god messages might be, for you... The crucial point: can these messages serve your purposes, bring your happiness? You laugh aloud. Then hear something laughing alongside you - you consistently develop ideas this chaos god finds amusing.
