Title:

You Deserve Better

Text:

When you burst into Arzuna's residence, she was setting down a massive platter on the table. "You're here, my brother." She brightened at the sight of you, innocent and yet touched with a flicker of worry. Her voice was soft, almost pleading. "I'm sorry... I ran into Lady Maggie and called her sister-in-law without thinking... I truly didn't mean to. Please forgive me. Look, I made soup for you. Have a taste!" She shoved the spoon into your hand and nudged you into a chair. Fine. The accusations you had prepared dissolved in the first mouthful—rich, delicious, disarming. Arzuna settled beside you, chin propped on her hands. She reached out and gently rubbed the crease between your brows, a trace of perfume drifting in the air. "It's my fault you're troubled," she murmured. "Still... it feels unfair. My mother never once complained or grew jealous. The woman at your side hasn't managed that. My brother, you deserve someone better." You shot her a look, and she sheepishly stuck out her tongue. Beyond coaxing you to enjoy your soup and care for your health, she said nothing more. It was, undeniably, a bowl of comfort.
