The Gradually Perfected Creation

ID:

5006016

Text:

Mahir has built a dedicated workshop for her greatest invention. Although it feels more like a slaughterhouse to you – dozens of limbs from various body parts are categorized and hung on the walls, some from the living and some intricately assembled from wood and metal. Metal wires and rivets have been removed, the corpse's body has turned into a pile of chaotic connections of water pipes and lead canisters, only the spine is soaked in sparkling ether solution, indicating its origins from a once-living human. Mahir couldn't wait to start working.
