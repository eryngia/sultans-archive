Title:

He chanted a spell

Text:

Voluntarily, Asal, the owner of The Angler Bookstore, begged to be burned with these books – and the Sultan graciously granted his request. As flames engulfed him, he opened the book in his hand, chanted a curse towards you and the Sultan... using his life and soul to curse you to be sucked into this book after death, into a collection of terrifying, despairing, bizarre tales... to experience eternal fright and torment. The Sultan sneered at this, as the monarch's demeanor and that powerful Signet Ring made ordinary curses ineffective against him. What about you? God, is there a more frightening game than the Sultan's Game? This is truly the most boring curse.

Title:

An Unforeseen Sacrifice

Text:

Wasn't it the girl you once ignored? She sat atop a mountain of mixed firewood and books, heedless of the soldiers' shouts, the malicious cacophony of the onlookers, ravenously reading the books in her hands – there were countless free books piled up for her to read right now. Until the flames rose, in the unbearable fire and smoke, there always seemed to be a silhouette, calmly reading. In the end, everything turned to ashes, leaving only the shadow and a sigh before it dissipated.
