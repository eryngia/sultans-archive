House Occupied by Vagrants

ID:

5002502

Text:

You overhear a noble in the Bathhouse complaining, intentionally or not, about how his house outside the city has been occupied by a group of cunning vagrants. The steamy mist makes everyone laugh, dismissing it as a joke, because everyone knows that if he were willing to spend some money to hire a few mercenaries, the problem could easily be solved. He hasn't done that. Perhaps he knows that you have been chosen by the Sultan, and you are the one in this city who most likes, or rather, needs to meddle in other people's business.

After half a day of observation, you also notice that the group of vagrants is not completely disorganized; they vaguely have one leader. Perhaps, besides violence, you can try other methods.

You use your silver tongue to both threaten them with the dire outcome of occupying a noble's mansion, and promise benefits if they left on their own accord. The untraveled, starving mob are shattered by your words, murmuring hesitantly. You take advantage of their lapse in vigilance to capture the leader, sending the vagrants scattering headless. The noble, overjoyed, showered you with endless gratitudes over and over again. You stop him just as he is about to hurry into his house. With sudden realization, he slaps his forehead and offers you some coins as a reward.

Title:

This can barely be considered a Conquest.

Text:

The Sultan finds this story dull, dismissing the guards to take the leader of the vagrants slandering before him away. Before you can break the Conquest Card and retire, you hear the Sultan yawn, telling the noble, "Ahmad, I heard there's fine wine in your mansion?" The noble hastily promises to dedicate all his wine to the Sultan. But the Sultan smiles without remark. That miser knelt, shaking with cold sweat, takes a while before reluctantly claiming he misspoke, saying he'd offer the whole mansion instead... The Sultan waves, accepting the wine only, and giving the mansion to you—such is most befitting, the spirit of the Conquest Card.
