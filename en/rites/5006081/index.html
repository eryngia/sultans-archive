After Results

Result 1

Title:

A Thrilling Battle

Text:

His strike sends tremors through your blade to your grip. If not for the dragon blood tempering your bone, flesh, and soul, this single blow would have torn the weapon from your hands. Before the pain can register, an unspeakable joy and thrill surge through you. A chuckle escapes your lips – and you hear the same from the Sultan. Blades clash like relentless thunder and rain. Blood sprays wild and free, turning into a song of battle that races towards its end with no regret. Until the drumbeat of your hearts strikes its final note, your blade cleaves through the Sultan's chest. His blood, you discover, runs just as hot and red as any other. Twin blades clatter to the floor, something finally departing the vessel that had confined it for too long. The Sultan's gaze drifts from your face, freezing into one last unbridled smile. You have never understood his thoughts, neither when he was monarch nor now, as simply himself. But in this moment, you know – he died as a warrior. Perhaps this is the deliverance he desires most.
