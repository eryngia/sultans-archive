Result 1

Text:

Mahir brings you three vials of impossibly clear liquid, indistinguishable from water – yet tests reveal a vitality pulsing within. She tells you how these tinctures have saved the dying, halted a plague, reversed the decree of death itself. But they must never meet open flame. The concentrated essence, distilled to its purest form, would unleash all its energy in an instant. You accept her gift. And while she speaks, elated, unguarded, you drive a dagger into her heart. Her words falter, her pupils glaze, the light within them arrested and then dispersed. The Water of Life does not save her. You regard the blasphemer’s corpse with cold detachment – eternal stars, eternal life, these are the dominion of the divine. She has paid in blood for her trespasses.

Text:

Mahir has failed. Something has gone wrong in the distillation – her process yields only disappointment. At last, she arrives at your door, head bowed, burdened by defeat. With a wan smile, she lifts a few bottles for you to see. "At the very least," she says, "I can promise this is the strongest liquor you'll ever taste." You take the gift. And while she stands there, unguarded, you drive a dagger into her heart. She stares at you, stunned, unable to comprehend what has just happened. Foolish blasphemer. Did she not know? The devout do not drink for pleasure. She not only dared to stain your faith, but also dared to reach for the heavens – the domain of the divine. And for that, she must pay in blood.
