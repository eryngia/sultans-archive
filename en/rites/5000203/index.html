Title:

Mortals Immortal

Text:

You completely cannot modify this ring protected by powerful magic. After much effort, you couldn't even scratch it... As you and Mahir pondered desperately, nearly giving up one night, you suddenly grasped a fleeting insight... All things pass, all things pass - this is surely a curse for kings, but how can it not be a blessing for countless common people? Winter passes, torment passes, suffering passes, tyrannical rule passes... All things pass, except the poor who toil between sky and earth - they are immortal! If you're not a king, what's frightening about the Ring of Inevitability's curse? If no kings exist in this world, what could the Ring of Inevitability protect? You woke Mahir that very night, having her help engrave your oath to the people on the ring's inner surface... Skeptically, she took the smallest engraving tool, pouring the last bit of Aether onto the Ring of Inevitability. This time, the ring's vast magic fell silent, and she successfully carved new inscription beside the curse. Now it belongs to you.

Result 2

Title:

Stars Immortal

Text:

You completely cannot modify this ring protected by powerful magic. After much effort, you couldn't even scratch it... As you and Mahir pondered desperately, nearly giving up one night, a massive star suddenly visited your dreams... You'd seen it in Mahir's Mirror of Stars and glimpsed its shadow in dreams, but never faced its transcendent true form... This was a blazing, magnificent flame that had illuminated your grain-of-sand homeland since ancient times, stirring earth's tides. Ten thousand years hence, its flame burns eternal, regardless of belief! How could you not worship this existence beyond mortal scale, this greatness that warps time and space? When you finally awoke with tears streaming, the Ring of Inevitability bore obvious burn marks. You had Mahir seize the opportunity to erase the old inscription and carve an oath you made to the Star upon it.No longer does it mourn fallen dynasties - now it serves you alone, child of undying stars.
