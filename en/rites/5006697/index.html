Title:

You're Cast Down From the Clouds.

Text:

A squirrel leaps onto your lap, then scrambles up to the crown of your head! With a sound like a cork popping free, something inside your mind is wrenched loose—snatched away by that uncanny creature. Panic and dread overwhelm you. The world spins, and you tumble helplessly into the billowing fog below... You fall, and fall, until at last you crash onto a banquet table. Blinking, you see Hassan seated across from you. "What is it, my friend? You look adrift... Too much wine in your head, perhaps? I've felt the same. Come, drink again with me. Let new wine wash away the old." For a long moment you sit stunned, unable to recall what just happened. It's as if invisible threads had scraped through your mind, stripping away whatever didn't belong. And so you forget. The troubling questions dissolve into mist. You drink with Hassan until the cups blur together, until he finally staggers to his feet and takes his leave. The matter ends there. As for what you've lost... no one remembers, not even you.
