Title:

Maggie's Captivity

Text:

Convincing her to don this garment took considerable time, but in an instant, her body was suspended by writhing metal serpent chains. Those malevolent designs - even when the wearer tried to avoid such usage - fulfilled their original purpose under magical compulsion. The venomous fangs, sharp forked tongues, and tail stingers pierced your wife's flesh, injecting curses that twisted human will... The ancient consciousness dwelling within the serpent chains tempted and threatened you, demanding you personally repeat their transgressions. Did you anticipate this when you had your beloved wife wear this ancient garment? Was this born of ignorant curiosity, or deliberate intent? Under magic's influence, her serpentine form coiled tightly around you. As your pleasure peaked, the vipers bit your lover more fiercely - and her ecstasy and agony blurred together in your vision. Finally, when you lay exhausted, the serpents retracted their fangs and stingers with satisfaction, clattering to the floor like ordinary objects... You quickly gathered the dazed Maggie... but your mind was already reweaving this tale, recalling her expressions, your sensations... hoping this depraved story would please the Sultan enough to break that damning card.
