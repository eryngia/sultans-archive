Title:

Territorial Outrage

Text:

Lady Becky immediately bristles, tail raised high, hissing loudly at Little Moon. You struggle to carry away the defensive cat, but Little Moon seems delighted with this playmate, circling frantically while you hold Lady Becky, its tiny tail wagging furiously. Meanwhile, Lady Becky hides in your arms, continuously cursing at the dog - though unintelligible to you, clearly nothing pleasant - this vulgar muttering continues until nightfall, long after Faris has taken Little Moon away. Some say this jealousy, this fiercely independent attitude, is precisely what makes cats so charming... perhaps.

Title:

Puppy Love Incarnate

Text:

"Oh! I've always wanted an adorable little dog!" Maggie immediately embraces Little Moon, her face radiating a brilliant smile you haven't seen in ages. That day, Maggie plays with the puppy for hours, finding various toys and treats, speaking in childish reduplications. By evening, when it's time to return the puppy, you ask Maggie if perhaps you should get a dog too? "No... better not. The Sultan's game might claim our lives any day. At least people can seek their own escape, but a puppy... would just keep waiting and waiting if we disappeared. Just thinking about it breaks my heart." She smiles sadly, shaking her head, giving Little Moon a final kiss before returning it to Faris.

Title:

A Song of Two Moons

Text:

Mutton Stove seems quite interested in Little Moon. You witness this sheep and dog affectionately basking in sunshine together, while the poet himself snores nearby. They pass the entire day this way. That evening, after Little Moon departs, the poet shows you his latest work - this paper remarkably free of Mutton Stove's saliva and not impatiently eaten. "From little moon, to crescent, to full moon, each moon, is countless moons I've gazed upon. From me, to you, to him and her, each person, gazes upon their own moon." This truly is a fine poem.
