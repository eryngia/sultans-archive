Result 1

Title:

A Fish Among Pearls

Text:

"Ha, a woman infiltrating the House of Delights? That's simple!" Nabhani confidently agrees after hearing your plan. Unsurprisingly, when he meets the female bandit leader, his smug smile freezes: "You've grown up to be... quite... uh... healthy." Nabhani examines Riel's muscular arms and scarred body before managing a comment, "Healthy to the point of hardly resembling a woman." "Only you city folk prefer those sickly skinny women," Riel snorts. "Women are people, and people should be strong. I only like weakness in one case - when it's my enemy." Nabhani confusedly sips his tea in silence. Before Riel's patience runs out, you quickly explain his plan: the simplest way for her to infiltrate is to disguise herself as a Lady of Delights - before you can finish, Riel nearly smashes your skull with her fist, luckily Nabhani pulls her back with all his might... You quickly adopt a "this is the only way" expression, and Nabhani repeatedly agrees. Grudgingly, she resigns herself to Nabhani immersing her in a bath, carefully washing away the "war paint" made with blood, then shaving her armpits, applying perfume, intricately braiding her wild hair, and finally dressing her in a silk gown she could tear to shreds with one hand to conceal her muscular contours: "This is called hiding the tiger..." Before the candle burns out, a transformed Riel stands before you uncomfortably, now resembling a curvaceous Lady of Delights, except for her tightly clenched fists and eyes flashing the murderous light of "if this plan fails, I'll kill you all."
