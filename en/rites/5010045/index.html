Chronicles of Humanity

Text:

Songs echo through the sky. You recognize Lumera's voice leading... Stars pierce through the Destroyer's radiance, twinkling before that soul-crushing gaze. For countless millennia, stars have delighted in observing humanity. Now they return that knowledge through song: Be not downcast~ Be not sorrowful~ Be not lost in transgressions~ The Destroyer's gaze floods human hearts with repeated errors and sins... In their eyes, these are abhorrent, meaningless cycles; but in the stars' song, they witness humanity climbing these repeated mistakes, wounded yet ascending the spiral staircase, step by step creating history... Along this starlit, chaotic path, humanity climbs ever higher, approaching the lofty divine... Everyone hears this celestial melody, everyone sees the stars twinkling for humanity. Hope rekindled, their eyes reflect stellar brilliance, gazes focusing skyward, creating a spiral staircase for you - for warriors still willing to defy divinity.
