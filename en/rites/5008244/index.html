Result 1

Title:

Because of Your Past Help

Text:

Slave girls were everywhere in the harem. Despite Arzuna's beauty, elegance, and skill in dance, she had no stage to shine. She slept in the crowded dormitory, ate bland meals morning and night, and spent her days scrubbing floors, sweeping dust, wiping away blood... Then came the Sultan's fleeting glance. Arzuna barely had time to feel a spark of hope before the other slave girls struck. They doused her bedding with dirty water, snickering at the shadow of confusion and fury on her face, and at her fists clenching and unclenching. At that moment, Fadia came to Arzuna's side, pulled her close, and shared a faintly scented pillow with her. No one had expected Fadia to intervene—she was the most favored among the slave girls, a woman who had lain with the Sultan and lived to tell it! Before sleep, Fadia lightly silenced the childish cruelty, her gaze lingering on a few guilty faces. She said no more, for that was enough. Arzuna stood behind Fadia, protected by her small frame. It was an unfamiliar feeling. Arzuna wanted to smile, but tears glimmered in her eyes.

Result 2

Text:

Slave girls were everywhere in the harem. Despite Arzuna's beauty, elegance, and skill in dance, she had no stage to shine. She slept in the crowded dormitory, ate bland meals morning and night, and spent her days scrubbing floors, sweeping dust, wiping away blood... Then came the Sultan's fleeting glance. Arzuna barely had time to feel a spark of hope before the other slave girls struck. They doused her bedding with dirty water, snickering as they waited to see her dismay or tears. But there was none. Instead, a voice rang out, "Who drenched my bed will see their blood spill on theirs." The girls laughed it off. But the next morning, one of them lay lifeless. Blood gushed from her mouth and nose, soaking her pillow and blanket. Yet, her body bore no other wounds. Arzuna brushed by with a sneer, then set off to answer the Sultan's summons. No one knew what she whispered to delight the Sultan—only that the dangerous beauty never returned to the slave quarters.

Result 1

Text:

That day, you sent a servant to the harem passage, bearing a golden bracelet and exquisite sweets in your name for the fattest, most arrogant eunuch. No words were needed... Everything slid into place silently. From then on, the young eunuchs patrolling the harem at night scolded any slave girls who messed with Arzuna's bedding and made sure no tampering reached her food. When an old eunuch caught a girl cutting Arzuna's hair, he thundered, "You are destroying the Sultan's property!" The slave girls quickly learned their lesson. They still hated Arzuna—but now, their hatred was buried beneath the surface.
