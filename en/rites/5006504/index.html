Text:

During the dinner that evening, your wife suddenly stands up, sits beside you, takes the cup from your hand, and takes a small sip. Then, she whispers to you, “Darling, I have something to tell you... but not here.” You grab her fingers excitedly. Oh, is today the day? You've noticed that Fatuna has been visiting your wife more frequently lately. The two women whisper and stop whenever you get close, shifting their gaze elsewhere... What are they plotting? Are they shy about you overhearing? You suddenly recall Fatuna's earlier proposal, and your heart races. She must have been conquered by your charm. She still wants to marry you, doesn't she? She must have convinced Maggie. You can't help but smile and let Maggie, who is also smiling, lead you to the bathhouse.

Text:

Maggie smiles as she leads you into the bathroom. The steam rising from the pool creates an intimate warmth on her face. The sweet scent of roses floats ambiguously, and Maggie helps you take off your outer robe, scrubs your back, and brings you a cup of tea brewed with cloves and a good bottle of wine. In the steaming mist, you drink wine happily, sip tea, and caress her smooth skin. The steam seems to seep into your skin through your pores, and soon you feel a dreamy daze come over you... A pair of slender hands gently stroke the top of your head, then those hands mercilessly push your head underwater! Wait, wait a minute...! Something's wrong! You struggle desperately, the will to survive nearly pulls you out of the tub, but another pair of hands climbs up like seaweed, tightly wrapping around your neck. You are dizzy and can't breathe, losing all your strength. In the despair of near-death, you hear Fatuna's soft and vague voice, as if from the sky. “I will share everything with you, Maggie, whether good or bad... this guy doesn't deserve you!”
