Title:

Luna, My Abomination

Text:

What stands before you is no longer the graceful hunting hound of memory but... a monstrous patchwork seemingly stitched together from a dozen different dogs' limbs... The thing breathes, even affectionately licks its master with multiple dripping tongues protruding from various parts... Only Little Moon, the small mutt, desperately tugs at Faris's clothes, trying to pull him away from this abomination... But the tiny dog is casually batted aside by "Luna's" paw, crushed into a bloody pulp... Little Moon's eyes remain fixed open in death... Meeting this gaze, something like a tear forms in Faris's eye, but the blood-soaked Luna barks joyfully, wagging its tail as if proudly displaying a fresh kill. That momentary spark of sanity vanishes. He clutches his queen, his beloved dog, in a crushing embrace.
