The Crowded Throne

Text:

On this day, the Sultan brought both Arzuna and Zazie to the Lapis Lazuli Palace. The two concubines sat on either side of the throne, both heavily made-up, exuding exotic fragrances, and posing alluringly. Neither so much as glanced at the other. "My lords and ladies, you may raise your heads and look," the Sultan said, in high spirits. "Come, tell me—who is more beautiful: Zazie or Arzuna?" When no one dared to answer, he casually pointed at you. "[player.name], you may speak first."

Title:

Concubine Zazie Is More Beautiful

Text:

You respectfully replied that Concubine Zazie was undoubtedly the more beautiful. She had been with the Sultan the longest, knew best how to please him, and had granted him so many wondrous pleasures... You firmly declared that there was only one measure of a woman's beauty: whether she was favored by the Sultan. Your answer greatly pleased the Sultan. Knowing you were the one who had recommended Concubine Arzuna, he had tested you to see if you would favor the girl you had brought to court... "Ah, no—I won't accept this!" Concubine Arzuna suddenly let out a sharp cry. She threw herself onto the Sultan, rubbing her soft skin against his hair. "I won't lose—Your Majesty, tell them! Just as Lord [player.name] said—tell me, who do you think is more beautiful: Zazie or me?"

Between that girl and me, who do you prefer?"

Text:

"You both please me greatly, but..." The Sultan smiled, pinching Zazie hard enough to draw a soft cry. "I do prefer you, my beloved Zazie." Zazie lit up, radiant, nearly floating with joy. And Arzuna? She was crushed, defeated, her breath coming short and fast. "Your Majesty, you will grant your dearest one a small wish, won't you?" Zazie purred, kissing his cheek. "There is something I desire!" "Speak." She leaned close to the Sultan's ear and whispered sweetly. He roared with laughter. He slapped Zazie's bottom playfully, lifted her light frame, and strode toward the inner chambers. No one dared follow—not you, not the courtiers, not the despairing Arzuna.
