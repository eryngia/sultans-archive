Result 1

Text:

Curious, you walk over and pick one up to taste. However, Maggie stops you: "Ah... these are better for women," she gently scolds as she takes the preserved fruit from your hand. "Habib made these especially for me. He says eating them makes a woman's body warmer from the inside out... and more beautiful." "What's wrong with me trying one?" you sidle up next to her, pretending to be quite hurt. Maggie laughs, appearing to be in good spirits. She tosses a preserved fruit at you: "Look at you! You always want everything - always want the best of everything! Even a plate of women's preserves, you must have your share!" You catch the preserve and examine it carefully. It appears to be a peach with its pit removed, stuffed with spices, then preserved in honey. Just imagining the process makes you think it's incredibly labor-intensive. It's hard to believe Habib finds time to make these while taking care of so many people. Maggie seems completely unaware of your thoughts, just giggling as she says: "I'll ask if he has something suitable for men, and I'll tell him how greedy his master is!"
