You and Faris review the evidence together, both falling silent. Now, there's only one answer, one neither wants to voice, one painfully obvious, one cruelly clear... "So that's it," Faris says hoarsely, "it was him all along. No wonder this dog-fighting pit has operated so long..." You nod. Then ask what he plans to do. "What to do... of course I want to avenge my Luna, but behind this is - is..." he takes a deep breath through clenched teeth, "the Sultan himself! He owns everything - the entire country - yet still takes what little we have... a handful of gold, one wonderful dog... we can't possess anything he doesn't... his greed truly has no end - none!" The conversation halts abruptly, leaving you deep in thought...
