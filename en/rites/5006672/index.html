Result 1

Title:

You and Nawfal reached the meeting point

Text:

Cloaked heavily with your face concealed, you lead Nawfal bound with ropes, wearing a humiliated expression. Upon your entrance, the Vizier laughs loudly, circling Nawfal while clicking his tongue: "Ah, look at you, the righteous Nawfal, reduced to such a state!" "Unbelievable, truly unbelievable! A life dedicated to the nation, only to end like this – " Abdul strikes his chest in exaggerated dismay. "For a mere filthy prostitute, your reputation, your principles, your standing – all destroyed! Henceforth, when people recall the great Nawfal, their first thought won't be your righteous advocacy for disaster victims, but rather you in the arms of a male prostitute! All ruined, completely ruined, do you comprehend? Hahahahaha..." Nawfal remains silent, lips tight. This silence angers the Vizier. "Are you still playing the righteous noble? Let me tell you... The Sultan has wanted you dead for ages! Who wants a gadfly constantly stinging at their side? Everything you find objectionable is the very essence of the Sultan himself! And you – you just talk endlessly as if you alone are discontented, as if righteousness is your exclusive domain, as if I'm merely a sycophant at the Sultan's feet – but I am the Vizier, the VIZIER!" Nawfal sighs deeply, shakes his head, and says, "Poor guy, you've been turned into the best servant by what you hate the most." "Poor?! What qualifies you – " Abdul's face contorts with rage, but then he sees, the ropes binding Nawfal fall loose, and you remove the cloth over your head. At this signal, your people quickly rush into this courtyard, overpowering the few guards present. Abdul looks at you and Nawfal, confused as to how you can stand together. But questioning is futile, the tide has turned, no amount of begging, howling, flattering, or promising will help. You and Nawfal kill him without hesitation.
