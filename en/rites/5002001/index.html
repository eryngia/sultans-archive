Result 1

Title:

Perhaps illness makes you melancholy.

Text:

During the nights when your wound keeps you awake, you think longingly of a certain friend renowned for his skill in medicine... At that time you have only just inherited your father's title and entered the Sultan's court. You are injured while accompanying the Sultan on a hunt. That night, in pain and unable to sleep, you are encamped far from home and cannot summon your own physician. In desperation you secretly appeal to the court physician. It is a rash choice, but—unexpectedly—he helps you, and a friendship begins. Later, as both of you grow busier with the Sultan's demands, the contact between you dwindles... Moved by these memories, you write a letter addressed to the palace.
