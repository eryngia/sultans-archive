Text:

This minor lord’s family has a long-standing renown for kindness – what but merely a facade. Evidence that Nawfal holds confirms they are the main sponsors of the local slave trade and possess a hereditary tradition of evil practices... They continuously 'consume' slaves to satisfy some twisted sadistic desires. When slaves are insufficient, ignorant commoners nearby disappear...Yet these crimes mean nothing before The Sultan, and the law is powerless against him – only the King of Flames can execute justice.

After Results

Result 1

Title:

Cruelty Spreads

Text:

The King of Flames had little trouble disarming the lord. His soldiers dared not confront the legendary bringer of justice... and under his guidance, the farmers armed themselves, finding the bone-laden cellar... Some even found their long-lost loved ones. Quickly, things spiraled out of control. What should have been an executed ceremony personally overseen by the King of Flames turned into a massacre by the wrathful farmers – the already disarmed lord's soldiers, imprisoned servants, and housekeepers, including the entire lord’s family... his wife, three sons, five daughters, along with two toddlers and a infant, were all hacked by scythes, smashed with shovels, and hung with pitchforks... displayed in front of the burning manor. Even the King of Flames couldn't, or dared not, stop the furious crowd; their anger pent up too long, their suffering deep as it is unleashed, their power shocking, their cruelty unspeakable.

Result 3

Title:

Blood Gathered

Text:

Amidst chaos, the manor burns fiercely, blood flows like rivers. Madness and death, anger and revenge, spiral into the sky along with ash and howls, rising high – until awakening something darker. Under the guidance of mysterious powers, the King of Flames begins slaughtering the remaining residents, who only dance frenetically, desecrate corpses, and wail, showing no resistance. When the last body is thrown into the fire, with no other living soul in the manor, all the blood – whether soaking in the soil, burning in the flames, or flowing through water – rise and gather onto the King of Flames’ sword, armor... then seep into the mask’s opening, filling the interior... This is the scene you and Nawfal’s retinue see upon arrival: the birth of a monster. Realizing how deep into darkness you’ve ventured, Nawfal silently distances himself from you.
