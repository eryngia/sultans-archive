Text:

As soon as you return to the capital, the Sultan summons you to his palace, showing keen interest in the dragon head you have brought back. He even personally opens the dragon's mouth to verify its authenticity... Finally, he wipes the blood from his hands with a silk scarf offered by a attendant and looks at you languidly: "My dearest [player.name], I think this will look magnificent above my throne. What do you think?"

Title:

Royal Favor

Text:

Because you presented this peerless dragon head to your Sultan, you gained great fame and prestige in the city instantly. The Sultan rewarded you generously – treasures, wealth, his private cellars, his harem, and treasury all laid open before you, and you indulged in them day and night. Though absent from court, none dared impeach you. Wealth poured into your coffers unbidden – no need to manage your estate, no need to tend to your affairs... Gradually, people got used to it, and later, people no longer cared what absurd things you did, until finally... no one noticed as you faded away into the palace's extravagance, like a drop of water into sand.

Your silence makes the Sultan smile. "Oh, you don't want to?" he says with a smile, lightly raising a ringed finger, "Then die." The Sultan glances at his ring, puzzled for a moment, but he brushes off this little accident without concern "Very well," he says, rising from his throne.
