Result 1

Title:

Today's House of Delights Seems a Little Different

Text:

The patrons are dressed brightly and elaborately, with the men's hair covered in rose water and shining longswords encrusted in ancestral jewels, while the Ladies of Delights sit behind veils, concealing their faces like noblewomen. "Ladies and gentlemen!" Buthayna, the elderly mistress of the House of Delights, came out, "Today we are choosing the most popular man, the one closest to the ladies' hearts, the one who qualifies to enjoy the greatest pleasure!" The men stir, sizing each other up... Eventually, all eyes focus on three individuals – you, Nabhani, and a masked noble guest. Today, Nabhani wear the Iron Guard's uniform, a few braids mix with his loose hair, each adorn with large garnets matching his honey-colored hair, keeping all women's eyes on him. As for the masked guest... How strange, why wear a mask in such a setting?

Result 2

Title:

Now You See the Truth...

Text:

All other patrons are familiar faces, except him... You carefully observe this man hidden by a mask, his face completely covered. Finally, your gaze sweep over the large familiar gemstone ring on his finger... It's... Suddenly, you break into a cold sweat. He's the Sultan himself! ...Why would he...? At this point, the masked guest notices you are watching him; he turns and looks at you, seemingly smiling. However, before you can react, Buthayna's clapping interrupts your thoughts.
