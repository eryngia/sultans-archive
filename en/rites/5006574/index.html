Text:

This is a report about you: you and the Sultan's Game, and those cards, have now become legends among the people. Many soldiers, especially the younger ones, have started carving designs they've heard about from the Sultan Cards onto their shields or armor. Most of these are Carnality Cards, often featuring your face alongside other indecent symbols or organs. On a small scale, this might seem like a harmless prank; but on a larger scale, it raises a significant issue, as only the portraits of successive Sultans are deemed worthy of being engraved on officers' weapons.
