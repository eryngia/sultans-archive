Title:

What's done is done

Text:

One day, an old man blocks your door, hurling curses. He rails at you for meddling, at the Sultan for sparing Samir, and at his own son for dishonoring their house by serving the Sultan. His fury grows louder until a crowd gathers, eager for scandal—gossip of the Sultan, the court, the fallen kingdom, and a father against his son... Now everyone knows: a stubborn old man, loyal only to his former homeland, cast the corpse of an infant into the harem—an act born of fury at his son's submission to the Sultan. Such things are all too common in the slums. His aim was to see his own son destroyed, yet his scheme was laid bare by the Sultan's composure and your keen judgment, driving him into a frenzy that ended in madness. Though the guards rush in at once, the tale has already spread from mouth to mouth, known to all. Before their very eyes, the culprit glares with bloodshot eyes, hands trembling, then suddenly foams at the mouth and collapses. By the time the royal guards drag him to the court physician, the old man has already breathed his last... Even so, the royal guards breath a sigh of relief. They urge you to bring the body, together with the evidence, before the Sultan. "Now Lord Samir's name will be cleared, right?"
