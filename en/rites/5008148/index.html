Result 1

Title:

Crescent's Fall

Text:

The cage opens and the racing hound s burst forth, chasing the fleeing rabbit. But Luna... It charges forward with lightning speed as usual, then suddenly drops its head. Its momentum prevents it from stopping properly, causing it to roll forward and slam its neck against the track barrier. It stops completely, tongue lolling, pink foam spraying from its mouth. The spectators gasp as something flashes past you - Faris, already rushing onto the track. In the disorienting commotion, you help Faris carry Luna back to his room. The poor hound is already dead. Tears well in its beautiful eyes, and despite Faris's efforts, its eyes won't close. Its neck is broken - racing hound s' bodies built for ultimate speed have no protective fat, making one fall potentially fatal, as happened now. Faris cradles Luna's body, burying his face in its beautiful fur. "Please leave... I'm sorry you lost so much money. I'll find a way... but please go. Today, I don't want... don't want to say anything..." You have no choice but to leave the hound -fighting pit alone.
