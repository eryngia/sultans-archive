Result 1

Text:

Old Kuk is a gaunt, elderly man with jaundiced yet piercing eyes. He listens carefully to the Roaming Swordsman - no, Prince Sirshana's words. Unmistakable joy floods his withered face. "Your Highness... Your Highness, do you truly intend to... kill that... bastard? By the Highlord who illuminates our mountains, the Prince will avenge us all!" He weeps, face covered. After composing himself, he produces a ledger-like book for the swordsman. "When our kingdom fell, witnessed by the Star-Souled, we who remained faithful pressed blood prints onto this contract... One day, we would answer the Oath of Gilded Blood's call and restore our rightful succession... Since His Highness has decided, we'll gladly die to become your stepping stones to the throne." He performs an almost-forgotten ritual bow to his prince. "Visit the nobles recorded in this sacred contract, Your Highness! Let them know the promised day has arrived!"
