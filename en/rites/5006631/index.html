Inal screamed, throwing herself on Aziz's corpse, desperately trying to cover his bleeding wound. But it was too late. She stared with large tears dropping one after another. "Just kill me too, Master," she trembled, whispering, "I have wronged you, I am not a good slave, nor a good woman. I have caused the death of the man I loved... kill me!" Yet, while you considered whether to forgive her, she had already seized the sharp knife Aziz had used and, without a second thought, slit her own throat. Perhaps it was the only decision she made for herself in her life. In the end, she was free.
