After Results

Result 1

Text:

Manar doesn't expect you would come. She looks very downcast, no longer showing the excitement like her first adventure. She tells you that her dream was to explore the world and record it faithfully on her map – that corrected parchment where the location of the oasis you found has been burned into a scorched hole. Manar shakes her head sadly, saying she never thought she would only bring disaster to them. With a forced smile, she says, "Actually, I never thought of coming back... but you came... perhaps this is fate..." You want to comfort her, tell her it's not her fault, but what can you say?

Result 2

Title:

You overcome the trials and tribulations

Text:

You trudge through the choking haze of sand and smoke in silent determination. The deathlands do not claim you. The mountains rise, red as dried blood. A valley, long and narrow, cut through the land like the scar left by a god’s blade. And at its end – a city. The streets teem with traders. Orderly. Prosperous. A place of wealth and law. You wander through its markets, dazzled, unable to resist a few exotic indulgences. As you leave, Manar lingers, murmuring to the valley’s jagged walls. "Easily defensible... isn’t it?" Then, shaking her head with a rueful smile, she turns to you. "No matter. Thank you."

Result 3

Title:

You could not change anything

Text:

The storm was merciless. You have never looked death in the face so clearly. But when you wake, all is still. The merchant who saved you says he followed the sound of camel bells and found you – only you. Yes. You remember now. When all was lost, Manar pressed the last of the supplies into your hands and smiled. Peaceful. Resigned. As though she had foreseen this moment. As though she had always been waiting for it... You carry news of her death back to the city. The Sultan expresses great regret for her failure to fulfill her mission. Then his attention drifts to brighter things: fairer women, newer diversions, treasures more worthy of plunder... Manar's name fade from memory.
