Title:

Darkness Desires

Text:

"Poor child," Badriyyah caresses your cheek: "Come taste something truly good..." She guides you, placing your lips to her nipple. Needing no explanation, you instinctively begin to suckle. How sweet—a taste rich and abundant as earth itself, how noble—an experience light and silky as godly nectar... You suckle on the gloom until you've had your fill. She withers as you feast, but why stop now? There's a darker tide coming to replenish her hollow shell soon enough.

Title:

Hope Fills Emptiness

Text:

Iman commands you to prepare flatbread: you must mix the dough yourself, bake it yourself. You fail perhaps six times before succeeding, having eaten the dough during previous attempts. Then he instructs you to find seventeen beggars, promise each a task, write their names on the bread, telling them they may eat it upon completion. When the bread is covered with names, Iman gives you holy water, telling you to consume both together... Finally sated, you feel the bread expanding a hundredfold in your stomach... Everything consumed during your strange binge transforms into gold and gems, which you vomit along with stomach acid. As for the seventeen beggars fighting bloody battles for these rewards, you no longer care, wanting only peace...
