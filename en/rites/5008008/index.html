The Awakening of Happiness

Text:

You and Maggie walk into his room hand in hand with the moonlight. His nervousness makes him rigid like a statue, but you can see his waist and legs trembling crazily. So, you step forward to comfort him, soothing his back, trying to steady his breathing. Meanwhile, Maggie takes off her clothes, slightly shy but her eyes full of affection for this young man – not expressing desire, but rather appreciation, both physical and spiritual... Faraj has earned it all through his support and selfless help. This emotion, along with Maggie's beautiful body, is projected into Faraj's eyes. At the moment he receives it all, he relaxes – you've seen all his expressions, joy, shyness, sadness, anger, longing... but for the first time, you see happiness in his eyes. He stands up, kisses you like a true mature man, also kisses Maggie, then contentedly bows, opens the door, andleaves leisurely, leaving behind just you two and the bright moonlight. The next day, he tells the Sultan the story with you in the Lapis Lazuli hall, and you see him talking with more ease than you, describing to the Sultan his spiritual awakening journey: "There was a time I felt unworthy, but now, I can calmly enjoy all the happiness, it should be considered a great act of carnality." The Sultan frowns... but as Faraj speaks, the Carnality Card in his hand silently breaks itself. The female magician bursts into laughter, praising everyone's wisdom, of course, including the Sultan's – thus, this matter is recorded in the court for future philosophers and theologians to endlessly debate.
