When you entrust Lumera with this momentous ritual, the books tumble from her hands... Yet this resilient child nods firmly - as if understanding your deeper meaning. The ritual begins. She weaves darkness skillfully, manipulating the rhythm of all things, channeling the dark lord's will here... The seed in your heart transforms from phantom to reality. The divine beacon enters the world - becoming something that can be destroyed. Seizing this opportunity, Lumera converts all remaining ritual power into a destructive command: the spell to shatter stars, knowledge etched in her soul's depths through countless cycles since birth - allowing star-spirits to sacrifice themselves for higher purpose when the world has need. A deity coveting the physical realm loses Their key, and you find liberation... Hereafter, you will exist only within stellar songs, which will speak only of the care and joy you brought Lumera - the tenderness that saved the world.

Alone, you lay at the center of the ritual, here soaking in all filth, becoming the conduit for God. One by one, they filled you—through every orifice of your body and the fissures ripped open by the shadows—until you were blackened inside and out, utterly profane, drawn ever closer to the Supreme Lord… In that abyssal darkness, the perilous seed in your heart materialized and took root in your belly. [s3.name] raised a blade and, before the dark egg could devour your flesh, swiftly extracted it and placed it into an etheric solution—not out of mercy, but because the god's egg required a vaster, more abundant vessel; one mortal alone could neither sate its hunger nor reflect its majesty! As the followers examined your blood and Body, pulling at the tendons on the egg sac, you dreamed amidst extreme pain. The Abyss called to you, the evil forces once friendly to you were agitated by The Creator’s breath: For countless times, we longed for it to play with us, but it kept refusing the Abyss's invitation. Now, it has found you, bring it to play with us! We'll reward you with legions! Let’s have endless games on the lower realm! Don’t let this arrogant guy ruin the material world, or no new players will join us! Before you can reply, they eagerly deliver their summons—your flesh, blessed by the Abyss's power, churns and contorts... extruding a mass of uncanny flesh suspended between life and death. It radiates unbelievable magic... a gift any god would covet, yet also a trap the Abyss has set for it.
