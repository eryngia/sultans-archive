Sultan's Suspicion

ID:

5001016

Text:

You cannot tell if the Sultan is genuinely displeased with you or is just toying with you. He furrows his brows. His eyes are shrouded by shadows, but you can still feel a prickling against your skin. "People say, you indulge yourself like the Sultan." His voice becomes lighter, as it does when he kills – "You've reaped much from this game. Perhaps it's time you repay your master?" The Sultan taps his ring against the armrest of his throne. His words are law. "Relax, I'm not looking for a new consort. Nothing so formal – just some indulgence. You understand, surely?"
