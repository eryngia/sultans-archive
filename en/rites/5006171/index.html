Title:

Grave Mistakes

Text:

Following the jinn's guidance, you come to a graveyard on the city's edge in that darkest hour just before dawn. It doesn't belong to the Purist. So only the poor and outsiders without the faith's protection are buried here. You never even knew this place existed. The jinn blesses your sight. Now you can see the faint glimmers beneath the graves, the shallow earth... You dig. And dig. Ducking behind crumbling headstones whenever the gravekeeper passes with his lantern... When the light fades, you glimpse a name on a stone. The grave of — [player.name]! Panic takes you. You tear the grave open, hands shaking, because the corpse inside... it looks exactly like [player.name]! The gravekeeper hears. He's running at you now, lantern raised. And in its light, his face is the spitting image of Lord [player.name] when angry! No time to think. You grab the body and run, blind with fear... until, somehow, you stumble back onto familiar streets. By sunrise, the corpse crumbles. All that's left is a ribcage forged of pure gold, a cage, perfect for your needs. After that, you search again and again. That graveyard is nowhere to be found. And the jinn, try as it might, can never quite explain what happened. To it, it's simple. Obvious. Just the way things are.
