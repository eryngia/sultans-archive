You found Asal in prison. "This is the punishment I deserve," he said gloomily. "Just as a mousetrap is the grave a mouse deserves. That lord values his collection of books above all else, as a father treasures his beautiful daughter. Naturally, a thief who dares to plunder such treasures must be taught a lesson." He spoke so, yet the moment the guard opened the cell at your signal, he darted out faster than anyone else could. Once outside, he seized your hand, tears of gratitude welling in his eyes. "Just as Lord Nawfal predicted—you would surely come to save me! Thank you, my lord [player.name]. I shall never forget your kindness!" "Wait," you said. "What else did Nawfal tell you?" "That lord! Ah, what a man—so generous, so noble, so refined! Even after I committed such a crime, he never truly wished me to rot here. He even, as if by accident, reminded me that some passages in Errant Thoughts might best be revised before republication—lest they bring me trouble." A vein throbbed on your forehead as you struggled to contain your anger. You reminded him sharply that you were the one who had paid for his release. It was you! Asal laughed, soothing you with raised hands. "Of course, of course, my lord—I never meant to suggest your taste was any less refined! Enough talk—I must hurry and prepare the new edition of that book, the one that vanished from the market a hundred years ago. I have a feeling it will be quite the success!" He bowed deeply, then turned and dashed toward his bookstore without a moment’s pause. Ah... how you longed to throw him back into the cage!
