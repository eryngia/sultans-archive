Result 2

Title:

Arzuna Did Not Grow Careless

Text:

That day, she noticed an unfamiliar eunuch preparing her meal. Suspicious, she forced him to taste the pastries he had made... and discovered he had been bribed by Zazie, along with a packet of poison. She went to the Sultan, overjoyed—yet appearing terrified on the surface—claiming that Zazie had intended to assassinate him. The meal was meant for him, she said; if she had not uncovered the plot, his life would have been in danger. Such a thing was unforgivable—everyone ought to serve the Sultan with all their heart! The Sultan agreed. Zazie was executed. Her body was taken to a remote graveyard outside the city. Before the jewels could be removed, beggars, vagrants, and thieves dug her up and stripped her naked. Birds and beasts feasted on the soft skin once touched only by the emperor, and worms made their home in her once-beautiful eyes. This was the end of one of the Sultan's favorite concubines.

Result 3

Title:

To Victory, Eternal as Gold

Text:

Arzuna was overjoyed. She even held a lavish feast to celebrate her triumph. During the banquet, she drank heavily and tasted rare delicacies... then died in agony. The Sultan was furious. A woman's death meant little, but he had only just declared her his favorite. This murder felt like an insult to his authority. All eunuchs and maids present at the feast who had helped prepare the food were forced to take the same poison and buried with Concubine Arzuna. It took nearly three days to fill the empty positions for the grand palace. Three days later, the silence was broken. For another woman—golden and brilliant, yet temporarily overshadowed by Arzuna—had soothed the enraged Sultan. The empire carried on as usual. The Sultan sought fresh diversions once more.
