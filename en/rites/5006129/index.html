After Results

Result 1

Title:

No Time to Explain

Text:

You hurry to Samir's chambers in the palace. Normally, the royal guards at his door interrogate every man who passes, but today they look stricken with fear. Without a word, they step aside and open the way. Samir drags you inside. His back presses against the door, eyes darting nervously toward you. Only now do you notice the sweat soaking his face. "They... they were just here. They spoke—if you can call that speaking. That wasn't a human voice... not even close. Oh God, it was terrifying... I think it's connected to the medicine—the one I gave you. But I—" He freezes, then whispers hoarsely, "Listen! They're here again!" From outside comes a strange scraping, followed by the startled cries of the guards. Then, a taut, sickening creak—like a rope pulled to breaking... Silence falls. Then: a knock. Once. Twice. Thrice. Perhaps a tail. Or a bone. Or an eye. But not a human hand... Something beats against the door, shrieking in a voice beyond comprehension. The echo alone sends chills crawling up your spine...

Result 2

Title:

Unfortunately, You Fail

Text:

You steady your mind, wrapping every word and syllable in the power of your Insight. Yet the harder you try, the more the voices shatter your focus. The door groans. A crack splinters across the wood. Darkness floods the room. It swallows you and Samir whole. Your consciousness slips away—yet even the monsters who kill you seem bewildered, as if they had meant to bargain for something else.

Result 3

Title:

Fortunately, You Succeed

Text:

You steady your mind, wrapping every word and syllable in the power of your Insight... You try to communicate with beings far beyond Samir's comprehension, from a height he could never reach. "Ten bottles... at the entrance... we... need... will... give you... reward..." You wipe the cold sweat streaming down your forehead and shout out loud, promising that you understand—you will make sure Samir prepares the medicine. A rumbling sound shakes the air again, as if from some enormous belly... or perhaps it is the sound of a wound? When it passes, Samir collapses to his knees, utterly spent, gazing at you with awe. Outside, the royal guards seem to have just awakened from a dream. They whisper among themselves, spreading tales of your deeds—Lord [player.name] not only pleases the Sultan but can even converse with demons!
