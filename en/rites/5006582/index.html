Supervision on Officials

ID:

5006582

Text:

This is a proposal for a new system designed by honest and intelligent people. It aims to limit officials' power through constraints between different governmental departments and officials, taking advantage of their selfish nature of fearing responsibility. It will also enhance the treatment of low-ranking officials, preventing them from having to rely on bribery for a living... So it's a win-win proposal. The only problem is, it requires money – a lot of the empire's money.
