Venture into Mountains

Following wild horse and gazelle tracks across barren gobi and stone beaches, you penetrate deep mountain encirclement. You see snow-capped peaks - the book says between annual snow accumulations lies a red dust line. Only that mountain birthing this world lacks one, because all life and heat get absorbed by the secret treasure buried beneath, because summer never visits that peak.

Title:

It Belongs to You

Text:

One exhausted night, you sleep alone in your tent. You dream companions becoming strange, peculiar, vanishing one by one in snow, leaving only ghastly white silence. Life and death brand painful marks on your respective palms. You see a massive scale rising from earth, solemnly and coldly weighing something - your love on one side, unknown on the other. A voice poses several questions - how did you answer? You cannot recall. Awakening, you find a heavy stone heart in your embrace, strange flames dancing within yet providing no warmth. You stare at it dazedly briefly, wake nearby companions, and descend together.
