Result 1

Text:

You slaped the Sultan Card across his face, asking if he'd like to taste it. Briefly grimacing, he clenched his fists, but then bowed to you. "Please forgive me," he apologized submissively, though deep down still unwilling, "But you can't blame me, it was your slave girl who seduced me. She's so beautiful, she looked at me among so many people, if not showing her willing..." Under your cold gaze, he swallowed the rest of his words. Matters seemed to conclude in this way.

Though you were unwilling, you ultimately had no choice. Alas, was it worth it? Unlike you, he had not accustomed himself to accepting these things... Though, perhaps he should find it fortunate, at least, that he won't die. What could be said... at least you resolved the affair with Inal. Rumor had it that the noble, having lost his face, left the capital overnight. You just wished whatever wind carried him away carry you with too.
