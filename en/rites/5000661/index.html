Result 2

Title:

You did not take advantage of the situation

Text:

You wait in endless torment, until the news reach you: the Sultan's army flattened the new oasis and brought back endless wealth. In celebration, the Sultan is throwing a feast in his golden palace for all his courtiers. Amidst the festivities, you spot Manar. Her face bears a bitter smile. With an almost sobbing voice, she says, 'I didn't know it would turn out this way.' No one can fathom the Sultan's savagery. You are thrown back to that cold, winter night of your adventure. It is as if you are again stranded on that empty moor, stalked by night predators, with only the two of you guarding the fire, huddling for warmth.
