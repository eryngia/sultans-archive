A Nation's Pillars: Sacrifice and War

Text:

They gather beneath [s1.name]'s banner, toward the light you shed. Ants and insects, countless, faceless, covering hills and fields, chanting your sacred name. The whispers of millions swell into a roar, rising to your heavens, binding the lowest to the holiest. They are no longer insects. They are the Swarm. For the first time since becoming divine, you feel yourself enter this world completely. That countless mass has become your body, your eyes, and your hands. Sacred hymns echo from the sky, the sound of your own delighted moans. And clouds reshape into your visage, like a sovereign head, beginning to move its colossal form.
