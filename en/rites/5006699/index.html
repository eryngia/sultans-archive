Baa, Baa! Enough of That!

ID:

5006699

Text:

Clouds surge in from all sides, pressing toward the center. You stretch out your arms with all your strength, barely keeping them at bay... From the wool-like mist, threads shoot out, weaving themselves into forms. "I told you, didn't I? Baa? Baa baa! Stop prying, won't you? Fine, have it your way... Baa—ah!" The clouds churn and twist, dark masses rolling forward as lightning arcs across the sky. The world around you shatters violently... The creatures formed from the mist return, fiercer than ever, their shapes bristling with menace.
