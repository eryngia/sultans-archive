Title:

Last Wish

Text:

Dark tendrils embrace Their designated sacrifice - yet Inal harbors no fear, only compassion. She has eternally served as the Creator's priestess. Since the master god's exile, this bloodline has wandered lost through countless ages... yet still comprehends all the god's anguish. My god, my god, You have endured excessive suffering! Misunderstood, forsaken, cursed, imprisoned... until You surrendered ideals and achieved this corrupted form! Within this ocean of endless distortion and agony, one pure tear falls into Inal's palm - pristine as agate, beautiful, reflecting no light in absolute black... This represents the Creator's ink, the final remnant within the Corrupter. "Dance, my cherished people." The teardrop's voice resonates in Inal's mind: "Offer prayer-dances for the new Creator! Through your desires, your god shall reshape this world!" Such tremendous power - infinite possibilities explode in Inal's consciousness, nearly causing her to faint: She could possess wealth, nobility, beauty, accomplish anything... Should she make any wish, for the new world's birth, the old world shall naturally face destruction - then the Corrupter shall overflow within it, completing Their long-desired eternal dominion. Thus, amid expectations from god and world, the girl voices her wish: "Knowledge, power, magic... these prove wonderful things. Under Your blessing, with [player.name]'s assistance, I already possess these, though merely a little, though gradually... I can already transform the world around me! Great god, share this world-transforming power with everyone who needs it! Especially children as helpless as I once was! Let all girls transform the world around them!" The Creator's remaining ink transforms into sacred script with these words. Throughout heaven and earth, the sound of shattering glass continues endlessly - the world's laws undergo rewriting, the girl's wish materializing.
