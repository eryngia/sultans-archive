Text:

You dismiss your servants and venture alone into the tunnel depths. Ancient Cultic God worshippers would dig downward until the earth warmed and strange gems appeared - merely the first step of their journey. Crawling through, soil filling your nostrils, ominous water sounds surrounding you, you question your sanity - if you don't return, will servants have to retrieve your corpse? Wedged between massive boulders, dizziness overtakes you... perhaps this is the necessary path to transcendence - approaching death brings you closer to divinity. You abandon what must perish to approach what endures! A sensation unlike any before - your thoughts become a rope whose other end someone violently seizes! Something pulls desperately at your heart, soul, and magical essence! The rope tightens, nearly dragging you into unfathomable depths... As you struggle, the vibrating cord creates a rhythm, revealing a path to hear the alluring voice more clearly. Your servant awakens you... The mine, darkness, rope - all gone. Was it mere hallucination? As you wonder, the voice from the depths echoes in your ears once more.
