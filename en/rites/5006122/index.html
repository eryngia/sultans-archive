Results

Result 1

Title:

They Deserve to Be Remembered

Text:

Here you consecrate a burial ground for healers. Be they noble physicians serving statesmen, or wandering doctors handing out herbs to the poor, regardless of rank or gender—if they eased suffering in life, they may rest here in death. When the grounds are finished, you tell Samir. Soon after, the first body is laid to rest, a coral-stone marker raised above it. Together, you stand beneath the shade of trees, watching people come and go. Many physicians arrive with their kin to see this promised place. Among them are familiar faces, watching with the same wary gaze... Samir notices some of his own kinsfolk, but does not approach. Instead, he turns and smiles at you. "I have nothing to tell them. In the end, all we have done in this life will be judged beneath the earth. When the time comes, I shall meet them—here, beneath our feet." The wind sweeps across the hill, carrying no trace of decay—only grass, sun, and the scent of soil. In the stillness beyond the wind's reach, the healers at last find their hard-won rest.

Result 3

Title:

Too Much Zeal

Text:

Here you consecrate a burial ground for healers. Be they noble physicians serving statesmen, or wandering doctors handing out herbs to the poor, regardless of rank or gender—if they eased suffering in life, they may rest here in death. Also, you assign the finest guards to patrol the grounds, raise a bronze statue of a physician, and even place a copy of the medical codex in the warden's hall at the very center. Nowhere else in the empire can boast a cemetery so splendid. Yet, such grandeur keeps common physicians away; few dare even to approach the warden to arrange their burial... For a long while, the only grave here is that of an old court physician of the fallen kingdom. His name is etched upon the coral stone by Samir's own hand. That day, the two of you stood together in the shade for a long time, saying nothing. Only when the moment came to part did he smile faintly and say, "I have nothing to tell him. In the end, all we have done in this life will be judged beneath the earth. When the time comes, I shall meet him—here, beneath our feet."
