Text:

Revisiting this square chamber, you distinctly smell flower fragrance, subtly different from before. After considerable thought, you realize - no blood scent requires masking. Opening the door, Iman absently tends jasmine near the window, nearly cutting the finest blossoms. Seated across the stone table, he examines you, confirming your recovery before gradually speaking. "I've decided to temporarily relinquish High Priest responsibilities." He meets your eyes, "Command me freely, if my inability to bestow pure words doesn't concern you." His matter-of-fact, calm admission surprises you greatly - considering many Order priests, unable to accept divine abandonment, repeatedly attempted contact until fatally self-flagellated. When you question why, Iman merely shakes his head silently, offering jasmine-petal tea. Perhaps someday, but not now. Perhaps eventually he'll reveal the truth: that no pain could surpass those few evenings.
