Your spirit strengthens, power filling your body. Perhaps some virtue-loving god rewards you, though you never sought god favor.

Title:

Stained Hands

Text:

You consider Alim. This pickpocket feeds orphans with stolen money. Without his "transfers," these children would starve. Are Alim's actions virtuous or wicked? All moral systems denounce theft - but how do they address poverty and hunger? They merely urge the wealthy to donate scraps... They praise generosity but wouldn't seize purses from the unwilling as they would sever a thief's hand! What else could Alim do? He commits wrong to achieve right. His hands, blackened by sin, are brushed with gold... These hands play fate's melody, harmonizing virtue and vice. This insight brings relief; morality isn't black and white - mostly gray areas exist, like Alim, like yourself.

Sonata

You reflect on Qais. When confronting the Sultan, didn't he anticipate imprisonment? Didn't he know his family would suffer? Yet he acted anyway, revealing virtue as sacrifice. Those worshiping virtue love sacrifice, yearning to offer themselves so others might flourish. Being remembered and honored provides life's greatest satisfaction. You contemplate Jawad. He never questions his actions' righteousness. He simply wants wealth, finding loopholes in rules to acquire it. Who deserves blame? Jawad who exploits the loophole, or the rule containing it? If rules are designed this way, doesn't it suggest rule-makers encourage such behavior? Abandon moral judgment, instead carefully discerning the rules beneath your feet; then dance with them - one, two, three steps - then twirl.

Result 10

Title:

Rhapsody

Text:

Moral contemplation exhausts you. You collapse onto your carpet. A mouse scurries past - a cat pounces, stunning it with one paw, then scratching it awake to play with it. Watching this predatory game, you find your answer. Which is virtuous or wicked - cat or mouse? Such distinctions don't exist. The cat derives pleasure, developing sharper claws and hunting skills. The mouse is merely sacrifice, summoning this game through its existence. Who would call a rodent virtuous? It simply lacks choice. Yes, morality is meaningless because we lack true choice. Pleasure matters - enjoying the game matters! You laugh aloud. The cat regards you with emerald eyes and golden pupils, seemingly sharing your amusement... Then cat and prey vanish as if never existing. Whose cat was this? You don't recall owning... Ah, an omen - god message from some chaos-loving god revealed through this elegant predator's game.
