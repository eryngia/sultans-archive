Title:

They bring news of Adila's death

Text:

Along with the news of Adila's death, a long parcel arrives. Her family, stone-faced, place it on your table as if it was not their child who died. Perhaps they already foresaw that this sword-wielding girl would die young; maybe they had long since stopped considering her family. And now, more than ever, they want to distance themselves with her. Regardless, this is the final gift Adila left to the world, to you. You open the parcel and are surprised to find her beautiful sword. Upon the water-clear edge, a smudge of red remains. Her family coldly informs you that is Adila's blood. This is the sword she took her life with. Beneath the sword, you find a note. Her handwriting has the same edge as her blade. "Isn't this sword beautiful? Remember, it is also sharp. Through it, I guard my last honor as a warrior – forever unsullied. You, trapped in this game, shall one day need it, just as I do now."
