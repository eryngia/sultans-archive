Sirshana came to you with blood still fresh upon his blade... "I honored her. In the end she died as a warrior... not as a princess, not as a traitor's child, but as a warrior."He tucked the broken blade away. "That is a good thing. One day I wish to die as a warrior too, not as a prince—but not yet. For now, at least I have done a little for my mother... Such a tiny revenge, and yet so much warrior blood spilled for it! At least I no longer have to fear seeing her tears in my dreams." He bowed with a prince's courtesy and left without another word.

The End of It All

Text:

Raed returned to you alive, soaked in blood, and—almost savoring it, recounted every detail of the fight... Everything between the traitor's daughter and the orphan of the consort ended here. She did not reproach you; on the contrary, she approved of what you did. "We fought to the death as warriors—not as prince and princess, but warriors," she said, nodding. "We are both satisfied. Truly, I do not want to be a princess, and he did not wish to be a prince. So I suppose... dying in such a fight would have suited him." She had the vagrants lay Sirshana to rest, and the grave's location was revealed anonymously to the remnants of the fallen kingdom.
