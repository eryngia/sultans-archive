Meritocracy / Kakistocracy

Text:

A principled nobleman has spent years gathering evidence against corrupt officials. If thoroughly investigated, countless capital nobles would face dismissal and property confiscation…Conventionally, just one scapegoat needs to be taken, as all of the nobles are from this court and, come time most proper, would repay their debts in earnest... You glance at the evidence, detailing many deeds of which even the evil find unjustfiable. However, as a humble player of Sultan's game, are you qualified for this capricious joust, or is it necessary to uphold this justice?
