Visiting Nawfal

ID:

5000640

Text:

Nawfal's gates are shut, a silent declaration that unwelcome guests will find no entry. Of course, he has no intention of receiving you. And in any other circumstance, you would have no intention of gracing the doorsteps of an enemy. But that pointed, knowing look lingers in your mind. Nawfal is not afraid of you. Or… wait. Is he? He should be. After all the public humiliations he has put you through, has he forgotten that you can now kill him without consequence? Yes. I can kill him. The thought slides into your mind. All you need is the right Bloodshed Card and a few trusted hands... If you bring a gift, coax him into lowering his defenses, then, one strike, clean and final. Or, if not that, at least you can get an answer out of him: what, exactly, did he mean by that look?
