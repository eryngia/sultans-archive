After Results

Result 1

Title:

A Confused Mess

Text:

Your first meeting dissolves into heated arguments about the approach. Some advocate creating pre-filled fake sheaths to switch after use but before the Sultan destroys them - though concealing such items presents obvious difficulties. Others suggest creating sheaths with imperceptible holes, allowing the essence to escape naturally during use - but the Sultan would surely notice. Another faction recommends creating harmless fake chili powder that wouldn't damage the captured essence - but how to test such a concoction? Volunteer yourself? After endless circular debates over these absurd, headache-inducing proposals, you decide to simply create everything and determine the best approach later.
