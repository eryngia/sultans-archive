Result 18

Title:

Storm Recognition

Text:

Just as you had foreseen, the Destroyer had arrived. You had long harbored this terrible knowledge... and had made your preparations. When divine radiance flooded your consciousness, it stood resolute against the tide of annihilation, granting you this apocalyptic revelation: The Destroyer would first cleanse human souls, consuming them as nourishment. Empowered by countless lives, They would expand Their form without limit, devouring ever more mortals until They blanketed the world... How far might the Sultan's realm have aided Their expansion? Could vast seas and endless deserts have halted Their march? Once the world lay purified, would They have birthed a new creation? Or would They have let all existence, including Themselves, sink into eternal silence? Perhaps They had spoken truth—humanity festered with corruption, and the world writhed with error... Perhaps They were nothing more than discarded tools of the Creator, broken instruments cast aside... Yet in heralding Their coming, you too had transcended mortal bounds. Your spirit now cradled the world's destiny—you, not They, would decide what was to come.
