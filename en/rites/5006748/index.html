Title:

Discourse on the Divine

Text:

You know Qais won't agree if you just ask. You have to start with philosophy. The debate deepens. You speak of gods, their existence, and their countless ways of shaping the world. The great labor it would take to create one. Blossoms drift out of the void, clinging to Qais from head to toe. Outside the window, the sinuous silhouettes of demons drift by, along with their lascivious laughter. One by one, the signs appear: you are approaching the truth of the world. And so, as Qais stands stunned into submission, you dispel the visions and—as if nothing had happened—ask him to join you in a little experiment: playing the part of gods.
