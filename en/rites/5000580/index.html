Stellar Guide

Text:

The Highlord has witnessed dynasties rise and crumble. Under Their guidance, humans once built temples for communing with star spirits; under Their watch, they toppled those pillars to construct uglier structures. Yet the most secret, critical foundations never changed - humans exist within the stars' design, blissfully unaware. A forgotten path appears in your mind - leading from the city wall directly to the Lapis Lazuli Hall's side entrance. No need to face the Sultan's army; you can deliver death directly. Astonished by the Highlord's wisdom, you wish to inquire further, but the pact is complete, the contract sealed, the binding magic dissipated.
