Text:

Your servants easily overpower and bind him. He curses you wildly, using every foul word he knows - unfortunately, his repertoire is limited, his worst curse being "may you die miserably." Finally, he breaks down sobbing: "She was the best woman - the best! The best! You had her but you - you - may you die miserably - she didn't even have a proper funeral... She! She! She was the best woman! You... you son of a dog!" Ah... so his rage stems from Maggie's death. But you have too many concerns to waste time on a deranged chef. You order him dragged away. The next day, your steward informs you Habib has vanished. He didn't return home or to your estate. His belongings remain scattered in his room, nothing taken... Other servants believe he was so heartbroken over Lady Maggie's passing that he decided to follow her. This feeling is difficult to describe, but at least... someone was willing to die for Maggie, even if... that person wasn't you.
