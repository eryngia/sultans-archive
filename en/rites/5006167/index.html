Text:

That morning, you wake with a stiffness between your legs. You try to roll out of bed, but something strange brushes against you down there. The electricity that spikes along your spine, the signals that make you tremble... all matter." She pulls away, rejoining the others. They all gaze at you. "But our mistress scolded me for this. The jinn's realm despises matter. The mistress is the master of wishes, so she needs only think, and all bends to her will. She needs only speak her wish, and every jinn moves to make it so."
