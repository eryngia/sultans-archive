Title:

Never a Disgrace

Text:

You asked Arzuna for her opinion, and she seemed puzzled, tilting her head with a playful air. "My opinion? Does it matter to you?" Yet she quickly composed herself. "Madam Buthayna... I've heard of her. They say she was an extraordinary dancer in her younger days and even invented a dancing dress strung with coins! The girls at the House of Delights must be skilled—they earn their living that way. Dancing with them could be fun... though of course, I don't have to go. Performing at a brothel feels dishonorable. Even if I'm a concubine's daughter, I know that much." You shook your head, not agreeing with her. Her dancing was exquisite; she never needed to feel shame, no matter the venue. Even in the "gutter," among the Ladies of Delights—the very same who could captivate the Sultan—the stage was no dishonor. They loved to refine their art and share it; masters had come to guide them and also learn from them. In that light, Arzuna had every reason to feel proud. Madam Buthayna's invitation was a recognition of her skill, a testament to her reputation as a true master of dance. "Really? You won't be angry if I dance in a brothel?" Arzuna asked, her eyes searching yours. "Shouldn't a noble... I mean, a courtier like you care most about family honor? What if I told everyone I'm the daughter of your father's concubine—oh, of course I wouldn't... I just mean..." You smiled and shook your head, then spoke earnestly: you had never once thought her shameful. On the contrary, having a sister with such extraordinary talent filled you with pride. Arzuna turned her face away, hiding her expression. Her farewell sounded a little hoarse at last.
