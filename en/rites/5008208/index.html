Title:

So long as he is pleased.

Text:

Jawad led literate vagrants to study how to record market intelligence and inventory. He patiently taught them how to list entries, calculate, and even forge accounts... "My lord says he cannot sleep at night without at least two sets of ledgers." In the end, those best at sums, and willing to help him falsify accounts, became Jawad's confidants. The rest, armed with new skills, easily found work at other trading houses. Rarely had one seen Jawad treat others so kindly—it turns out our own poor arithmetic was the true reason.
