Text:

Her eyes burned red, as if lit by flame. She pressed you into a corner, hot breath on your face; one hand covered your eyes, the other seized your arms—she would take the lead in this. You felt her body turn soft, yet iron-strong and fierce. You tasted blood on her lips—salty, warm... sensations that intoxicated and soothed, almost making you forget the bitter game you were in... For a heartbeat you wanted to go with her forever, no matter what road she walked. Fortunately—or unfortunately—the frenzy that fueled this passion was not so strong in her veins. When at last you both collapsed on the carpet, spent, the madness and its temptations dissolved like a fading dream. Embarrassed, she slid away through the tangle of sweat, blood and limbs, putting distance between you. "I'm... not as I ought to be. Bloodshed stirs passion in me... It will not happen again!" Your next attempt at comfort only angered her completely, and from that day on she kept far more distance from you.
