Text:

You help remove his sacred garments, causing many stone fragments to fall tinkling like believers' sighs. Iman's expression remains somewhat cold; you attempt arousal, hoping desire might color his eyes, but he restrains your hand, shaking his head. "I require pain," he states tonelessly, indistinguishable from blessing or prayer intonations, "Proceed directly." This somewhat irritates you. You comply roughly, thrusting forcefully. Your hands press his bare back, feeling scars - old, new, healed, reopened - spreading like maps beneath your palms, trembling, submitting, seeping blood that stains your fingertips. His neck arches higher through this pain, throat emitting hoarse whimpers like pleas or prayers, gradually transforming into stranger murmurs from his chest... you recognize this previously heard... god pure words explosively resound in your ears; in this strange, sacred observation, cold moist air lifts your soul cloudward...
